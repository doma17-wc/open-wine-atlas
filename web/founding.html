Questions

Do I pay anything now?

No. You reserve a spot with your email. When membership opens, you get first access at the founding price and decide then.

Is the founding price really locked?

Yes. CHF 19 for the first year, then CHF 39 a year for as long as your membership continues. If you cancel and join again later, the regular price applies.

What happens after the first 1,000?

Founding spots close. You can still join the list and hear when membership opens, at the regular price of CHF 69 a year.

Will the map stay free?

Always. The map, every place and every open dataset stay free to use. Members pay for tools built on top of the atlas.

Who is behind Open Wine Atlas?

An independent project built from official open data. It is not tied to any wine seller, and no place on the map is sponsored.

How do I leave the list?

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. One click and your email is off the list.
