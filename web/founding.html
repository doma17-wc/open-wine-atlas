Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. A Founding spot is free, and so is your atlas account. You reserve it with your email and a sign-in code.

What do Founding Members get?

A Founding badge with your number, first access to every new tool, a say in what we build next, and the best terms we will ever offer, whatever comes next.

What does a free account open?

Burgundy in full depth: every village, premier cru and climat with terrain, soils, rules and producers. Your saved places, tastings and notes also follow you on every device.

What happens after the first 1,000?

Founding spots close. Accounts stay free, and you still hear about new tools if you want to.

Will the map stay free?

Always. The map, every place and every open dataset stay free to use.

Who is behind Open Wine Atlas?

An independent project built from official open data. It is not tied to any wine seller, and no place on the map is sponsored.

How do I leave the list?

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. One click and your email is off the list.
