Entre Arve et Lac

Sub-zone

Entre Arve et Lac is a named sub-zone of AOC Genève in Switzerland across 12 communes. It lies 5.1 km north of Annemasse and 8 km east of Geneva.

The left shore of the lake between the Arve and the French border, from Cologny to Hermance and inland to Meinier and Jussy.
