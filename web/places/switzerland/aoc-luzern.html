AOC Luzern

Appellation

AOC Luzern is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

A young wine canton that grew from about 10 ha in the 1980s to over 90 ha. Vines grow around the lakes of Lucerne, Sempach, Hallwil and Baldegg and in the lower Wiggertal. Close to half the area is fungus resistant varieties such as Solaris and Divico, far above the Swiss average of 3.5%. The AOC dates from 2005.
