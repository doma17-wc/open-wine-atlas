Jenins

Sub-zone

Jenins is a named sub-zone of Bündner Herrschaft in Switzerland in Jenins. It lies 6.7 km north of Landquart and 18 km north of Chur.

A village high on the slope between Maienfeld and Malans. With 73% Pinot Noir it is the most red of the four Herrschaft villages.
