Untersee

Sub-zone

Untersee is a named sub-zone of AOC Thurgau in Switzerland across 8 communes.

The south shore of the Untersee, the western arm of Lake Constance, from Eschenz to Tägerwilen. Known for Müller-Thurgau and Pinot Gris. Arenenberg in Salenstein is one of the listed sites.
