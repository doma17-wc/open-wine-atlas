Walzenhausen

Sub-zone

Walzenhausen is a named sub-zone of AOC Appenzell Ausserrhoden in Switzerland in Walzenhausen. It lies 5.1 km north-west of Lustenau and 12 km north-west of Dornbirn.

Small vineyards on the slope above St. Margrethen, mostly fungus resistant varieties and Kerner.
