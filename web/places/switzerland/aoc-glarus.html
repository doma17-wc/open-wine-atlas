AOC Glarus

Appellation

AOC Glarus is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

1.9 ha in 2025, the smallest single canton figure in the federal statistics. The historic Bürgli vineyard is run by the Stiftung Bürglirain; Riesling-Silvaner and Blauburgunder are grown.
