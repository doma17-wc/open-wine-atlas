Sarganserland

Sub-zone

Sarganserland is a named sub-zone of AOC St. Gallen in Switzerland across 11 communes.

The Seez valley and the Walensee, from Wartau and Bad Ragaz to Amden. Pinot Noir dominates at Mels, Sargans and Walenstadt. Wine from a commune on the Walensee may be called Sarganserland-Walensee or Walensee. Walenstadt has over 60% of its vines in organic farming, Pfäfers the highest vineyard of eastern Switzerland at 740 m.
