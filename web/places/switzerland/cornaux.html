Cornaux

Sub-zone

Cornaux is a named sub-zone of Entre-deux-Lacs in Switzerland in Cornaux. It lies 7.5 km north-east of Neuchâtel and 16 km south-east of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

Small wine commune of the Entre-deux-Lacs, west of Cressier.
