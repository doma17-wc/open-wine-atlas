Neunforn

Sub-zone

Neunforn is a named sub-zone of Unteres Thurtal in Switzerland in Neunforn. It lies 11 km north-east of Winterthur and 14 km south-east of Schaffhausen.

A wine commune in the lower Thur valley. Two of its listed sites, Trottenhalde and Fahrhof, may make Lagenwein.
