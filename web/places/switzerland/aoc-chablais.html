AOC Chablais

Appellation

AOC Chablais is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland across 8 communes.

The upper Rhône valley between the end of Lake Geneva and the Valais. Chasselas from Yvorne, Aigle, Ollon and Bex is the classic, and the Föhn warms and dries the grapes in autumn. Villeneuve at the lake end is the gateway. In 2012 a Premier grand cru from Yvorne, L'Ovaille 1584, became the first wine of the Vaud government.
