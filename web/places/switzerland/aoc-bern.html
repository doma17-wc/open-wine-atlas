AOC Bern

Appellation

AOC Bern is an appellation of Trois-Lacs in Switzerland.

Two wine regions by law, Lake Biel and Lake Thun, plus scattered vineyards elsewhere in the canton. Nearly nine tenths of the vines are on the north-west shore of Lake Biel and the Jolimont. The cantonal law leaves the AOC rules to two growers' societies, the Rebgesellschaft Bielersee and the Rebgesellschaft Thunersee-Bern.

On the label
Bern AOC: 100% from the canton. Region AOC (Bielersee, Thunersee): 100% from the region. Village or hamlet name: at least 85% from it, the rest from the same region. Lage (site): 100% from a site listed in the KUBR annex, written site + village + region (KUBR art. 3). The grapes are listed in descending order; listed grapes make at least 85% (art. 6).
Grapes planted
Pinot Noir 33.8% Pinot Noir 2025: 78.0 ha at Lake Biel, 5.3 ha at Lake Thun, 2.0 ha elsewhere (BLW, Das Weinjahr 2025, table 1.3).
Production rules
Grape lists, minimum sugar and maximum yield are set by each Rebgesellschaft every year (KUBR art. 6 and 8). If the yield limit is exceeded the whole lot is declassified (art. 8). Federal AOC list 1 Jan 2026: Bern/Berne, Bielersee/Lac de Bienne, Thunersee.
