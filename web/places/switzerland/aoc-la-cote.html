AOC La Côte

Appellation

AOC La Côte is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland across 72 communes.

The long gentle slope between Lausanne and Geneva, facing the lake, mostly on glacial moraine. The largest region of Vaud by far. Its heart is the run of villages above Rolle, Mont-sur-Rolle, Féchy, Tartegnin, Vinzel and Luins, while Morges in the east is the largest single lieu de production of the canton.
