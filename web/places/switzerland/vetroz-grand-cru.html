Vétroz Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Vétroz Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Vétroz in Switzerland in Vétroz. It lies 1.9 km west of Conthey and 6 km west of Sion.

Grand cru since 1993, now under rules of 2016. Amigne may come only from sectors A and C to F of the Coteau de Vétroz; Chasselas from the coteau, sectors A to F; Pinot Noir and Gamay also from the plain, sectors A to H.
