Döttingen

Sub-zone

Döttingen is a named sub-zone of Unteres Aaretal in Switzerland in Döttingen. It lies 6.9 km south of Waldshut-Tiengen and 31 km north-west of Zürich.

A red wine village on the lower Aare, with seven approved site names such as Oberer Berg and St. Johannser.
