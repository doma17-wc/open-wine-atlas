AOC Zürichsee

Appellation

AOC Zürichsee is an appellation of AOC Zürich in Switzerland across 30 communes.

A cross-cantonal AOC for both shores of the lake, from Zürich to the Linth plain in Schwyz. About 150 years ago the shores held nearly 2,000 ha of vines; little more than 130 ha remain on the Zürich side. This is the home of Räuschling, the old local white grape. Stäfa is the largest wine commune of the lake.

On the label
Commune name: at least 85% from that commune, at most 15% from another commune of the AOC Zürichsee. Region, Ortsteil, hamlet or Lage: 100%. Minimum 65 °Oe white, 70 °Oe red; at most 1.4 kg/m² white, 1.2 kg/m² red (Schwyz decree, identical to Zürich rules).
Known producers
Schwarzenbach in Meilen, Diederik in Küsnacht, Lüthi in Männedorf, Erich Meier in Uetikon am See (Falstaff)
Schwyz side
The Schwyz side of the intercantonal AOC Zürichsee, shared with the canton of Zürich. Most of the Schwyz vines lie here, on the slopes of the Höfe and the March above the upper lake. Approved site names include Leutschen Klostergut and Insel Ufnau in Freienbach.
