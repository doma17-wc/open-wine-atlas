Zürcher Unterland

Sub-zone

Zürcher Unterland is a named sub-zone of AOC Zürich in Switzerland across 44 communes.

The vines between the airport and the Rhine, up to the Rafzerfeld on the German border. Mostly Pinot Noir. Main wine villages are Eglisau, Freienstein-Teufen, Rafz and Wil.
