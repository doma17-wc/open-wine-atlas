Aubonne

Sub-zone

Aubonne is a named sub-zone of AOC La Côte in Switzerland in Aubonne. It lies 11 km west of Morges and 21 km west of Lausanne.

Aubonne west of the Aubonne river, without the Curzilles area that belongs to Féchy.
