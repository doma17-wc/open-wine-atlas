Iselisberg

Sub-zone

Iselisberg is a named sub-zone of Unteres Thurtal in Switzerland in Uesslingen-Buch. It lies 6.1 km north-west of Frauenfeld and 13 km north-east of Winterthur.

The largest single entry in the Thurgau vineyard statistics, a hamlet just north of Uesslingen in the lower Thur valley. Swiss Wine names Uesslingen the largest wine commune of the canton. The outline is the whole commune of Uesslingen-Buch.
