Saillon Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Saillon Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Saillon in Switzerland in Saillon. It lies 5.7 km north-east of Fully and 15 km west of Sion.

Four specialities and no Chasselas or Pinot Noir. Parcels are registered with the grand cru commission by 1 June each year.
