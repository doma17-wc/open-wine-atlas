Stabio

Sub-zone

Stabio is a named sub-zone of Distretto di Mendrisio in Switzerland in Stabio. It lies 3.9 km west of Mendrisio and 12 km north-west of Como.

A commune at the foot of Monte San Giorgio on the Italian border, the end point of the trail Viticoltura e territorio through the Monte San Giorgio villages.
