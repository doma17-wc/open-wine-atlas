AOC Appenzell Innerrhoden

Appellation

AOC Appenzell Innerrhoden is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

The canton made its own wine law only in 2022, modelled on St. Gallen, so that its wine would not have to be sold as Landwein. All the vines are in the Bezirk Oberegg, an exclave on the hills above the Rhine valley. The larger of the two producers is based in Berneck SG.
