St.Galler Rheintal

Sub-zone

St.Galler Rheintal is a named sub-zone of AOC St. Gallen in Switzerland across 28 communes.

The left bank of the Rhine from Lake Constance up to Sevelen, with the hills of Rorschach and Goldach. Over half of the canton's vines, most of them on steep south and south-east slopes at Berneck, Thal, Balgach and Altstätten.
