AOC Appenzell Ausserrhoden

Appellation

AOC Appenzell Ausserrhoden is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

The vines are in the Vorderland above the Rhine valley and Lake Constance, at Lutzenberg, Walzenhausen and Wolfhalden. The St. Gallen agriculture centre runs the harvest control.
