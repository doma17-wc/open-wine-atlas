Distretto di Riviera

Sub-zone

Distretto di Riviera is a named sub-zone of Sopraceneri in Switzerland in Biasca and Riviera. It lies 4.5 km south-east of Biasca and 16 km north of Bellinzona.

The Ticino valley between Bellinzona and the mouth of the Blenio and the Leventina. Biasca holds most of the vines. Merlot makes up 83% of the area, the highest share of any district.
