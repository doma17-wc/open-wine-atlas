AOC Thunersee

Appellation

AOC Thunersee is an appellation of AOC Bern in Switzerland across 8 communes.

Small vineyards on the shores of Lake Thun below the Alps, at Spiez, Oberhofen, Hilterfingen and Sigriswil. The recommended grapes include Blauburgunder, Riesling-Silvaner and fungus-resistant varieties such as Regent, Divona and Souvignier gris. The KUBR annex lists ten named sites.
