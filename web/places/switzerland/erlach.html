Erlach

Sub-zone

Erlach is a named sub-zone of AOC Bielersee in Switzerland in Erlach. It lies 16 km south-west of Biel/Bienne and 14 km east of Neuchâtel.

Small town on the south-west shore of Lake Biel near the St. Petersinsel. Vines on molasse on the slopes of the Jolimont.
