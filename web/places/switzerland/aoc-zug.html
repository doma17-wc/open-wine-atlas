AOC Zug

Appellation

AOC Zug is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

About two thirds of the vines are white. Swiss Wine counts vineyards in all seven communes of the Zug valley floor, the largest at Meisterswil in Hünenberg.
