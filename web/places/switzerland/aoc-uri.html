AOC Uri

Appellation

AOC Uri is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

Vines in the Reuss valley at Altdorf, Bürglen and Silenen, with small plots at Flüelen and Sisikon on Lake Uri. The Föhn wind ripens the grapes. Wine growing in Uri is first recorded in 1248; the canton has been in the Swiss vineyard register since 1995.
