Buchberg

Sub-zone

Buchberg is a named sub-zone of Reiat/Rhein in Switzerland in Buchberg. It lies 5.5 km north-east of Bülach and 15 km north-west of Winterthur.

In the southernmost part of the canton, on the Rhine. With Rüdlingen it forms the Buchberg-Rüdlingen area of Swiss Wine, about 33 ha.
