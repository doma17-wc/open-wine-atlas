Sottoceneri

Sub-zone

Sottoceneri is a named sub-zone of DOC Ticino in Switzerland across 59 communes.

The part of the canton south of the Monte Ceneri pass, around Lake Lugano and down to Chiasso. Swiss Wine describes it as warmer, with clay and limestone soils and gentler slopes, giving fuller Merlot often aged in barrique. It holds the majority of the vines, most of them in the Mendrisiotto.
