Distretto di Leventina

Sub-zone

Distretto di Leventina is a named sub-zone of Sopraceneri in Switzerland across 10 communes.

The lower Leventina along the Gotthard route, at Giornico, Bodio, Personico, Pollegio and Faido. Merlot makes up 70% of its 28.4 ha.
