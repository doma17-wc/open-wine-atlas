Ardon

Sub-zone

Ardon is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Ardon. It lies 5.9 km north-west of Conthey and 10 km west of Sion.

Between Vétroz and Chamoson at the mouth of the Lizerne. Sylvaner led the AOC harvest in 2025 (25%), ahead of Pinot Noir and Chasselas.
