AOC Neuchâtel

Appellation

AOC Neuchâtel is an appellation of Trois-Lacs in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Vineyards along Lake Neuchâtel at the foot of the Jura. Chasselas and Pinot Noir, and the pale rosé Oeil-de-Perdrix.
