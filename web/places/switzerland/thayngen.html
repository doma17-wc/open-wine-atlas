Thayngen

Sub-zone

Thayngen is a named sub-zone of Reiat/Rhein in Switzerland in Thayngen. It lies 7 km north-east of Schaffhausen and 11 km west of Singen.

The main town of the Reiat, known for its vineyards for over 300 years. The statistics still list the old villages Altdorf and Bibern apart.
