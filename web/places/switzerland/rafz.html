Rafz

Sub-zone

Rafz is a named sub-zone of Zürcher Unterland in Switzerland in Rafz. It lies 4.7 km south-west of Jestetten and 12 km south-west of Schaffhausen.

The main village of the Rafzerfeld, at the northern end of the Unterland.
