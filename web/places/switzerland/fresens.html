Fresens

Sub-zone

Fresens is a named sub-zone of La Béroche in Switzerland in Fresens. It lies 8.8 km north-west of Estavayer-le-Lac and 18 km south-west of Neuchâtel.

Small hill village of the Béroche, part of La Grande Béroche since 2018.
