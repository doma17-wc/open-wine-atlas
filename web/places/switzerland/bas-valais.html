Bas-Valais

Sub-zone

Bas-Valais is a named sub-zone of AOC Valais in Switzerland across 18 communes.

The western part, from Port-Valais on Lake Geneva up the Rhône to the bend at Martigny and on to Leytron. Fully, Leytron, Saillon and Saxon hold most of the vines. Around Fully the slopes are mainly granite, and the Combe d'Enfer is a terraced amphitheatre of Petite Arvine.

Promotion region of Swiss Wine Valais (the IVV); the OVV itself names no wine regions inside AOC Valais. Swiss Wine Valais runs it from Port-Valais to Leytron.
