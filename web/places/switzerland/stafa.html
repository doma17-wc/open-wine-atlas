Stäfa

Sub-zone

Stäfa is a named sub-zone of AOC Zürichsee in Switzerland in Stäfa. It lies 3.5 km west of Hombrechtikon and 8 km north-west of Rapperswil.

The largest wine commune on Lake Zürich, on the right shore. Older sources call it the largest wine commune of the canton.
