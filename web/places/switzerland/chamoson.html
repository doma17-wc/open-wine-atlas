Chamoson

Sub-zone

Chamoson is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Chamoson. It lies 8.1 km west of Conthey and 12 km west of Sion.

The largest wine commune of Valais, on the right bank between Leytron and Ardon. It is the home of Johannisberg: Sylvaner gave 31% of the AOC harvest in 2025, ahead of Pinot Noir and Chasselas. Its Grand Cru rules of 2011 were the first under the 2005 framework.
