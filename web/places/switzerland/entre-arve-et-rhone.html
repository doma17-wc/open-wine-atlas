Entre Arve et Rhône

Sub-zone

Entre Arve et Rhône is a named sub-zone of AOC Genève in Switzerland across 15 communes. It lies 2.3 km west of Onex and 6 km west of Geneva.

The country south-west of Geneva between the Arve and the Rhône, from Bernex and Lully to the Champagne genevoise around Soral, Laconnex and Avully, under the Salève.
