Grand Cru Ville de Sion

Grand cruAppellation

Grand Cru Ville de Sion is a grand cru appellation of Sion in Switzerland in Sion. It lies around the town of Sion and 65 km south-east of Lausanne.

Sion argued for years over the grape list before its rules came in 2012. The commission is run by the Association des Encaveurs de Sion with the city.
