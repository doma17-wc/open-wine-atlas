Soral

Sub-zone

Soral is a named sub-zone of Entre Arve et Rhône in Switzerland in Soral. It lies 3.1 km west of Saint-Julien-en-Genevois and 10 km south-west of Geneva.

In the Champagne genevoise near the French border, with La Feuillée, a premier cru shared with Laconnex.
