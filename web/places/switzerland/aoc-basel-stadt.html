AOC Basel-Stadt

Appellation

AOC Basel-Stadt is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. It lies around the town of Basel and 4 km south-west of Weil am Rhein.

The city canton has its vines in Riehen, on the slopes above the Rhine plain next to the German border. Weingut Riehen farms most of them.
