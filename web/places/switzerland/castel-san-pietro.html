Castel San Pietro

Sub-zone

Castel San Pietro is a named sub-zone of Distretto di Mendrisio in Switzerland in Castel San Pietro. It lies 3.3 km east of Mendrisio and 10 km north of Como.

A commune on the slopes at the mouth of the Valle di Muggio. Mendrisiotto Turismo called it the most planted wine commune of the canton in 2006, when the Merlot centenary trails were opened.
