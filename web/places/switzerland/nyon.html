Nyon

Sub-zone

Nyon is a named sub-zone of AOC La Côte in Switzerland across 19 communes. It lies around the town of Nyon and 24 km north-east of Geneva.

The western end of La Côte between the Sérine and the Geneva border, around Nyon, Founex and Coppet.
