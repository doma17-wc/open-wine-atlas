Warth-Weiningen

Sub-zone

Warth-Weiningen is a named sub-zone of Unteres Thurtal in Switzerland in Warth-Weiningen. It lies 4 km north of Frauenfeld and 14 km north-east of Winterthur.

Home of the Kartause Ittingen, a former Carthusian monastery whose monks helped spread wine in Thurgau. Several listed sites carry its name.
