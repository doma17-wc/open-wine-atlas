Epesses

Sub-zone

Epesses is a named sub-zone of AOC Lavaux in Switzerland in Epesses, Riex and Cully. It lies 5.9 km east of Pully and 8 km east of Lausanne.

The former communes of Epesses, Riex and Cully and part of Grandvaux, all now in Bourg-en-Lavaux, plus a corner of Puidoux. Cully and Riex have no lieu de production of their own: their wine is Epesses. Calamin Grand cru lies inside.
