Riehen

Sub-zone

Riehen is a named sub-zone of AOC Basel-Stadt in Switzerland in Riehen. It lies 2.7 km south-east of Weil am Rhein and 6 km east of Basel.

All the vines of Basel-Stadt are in Riehen, above the Rhine plain at the German border. Blauburgunder is half the area, with Riesling-Silvaner and Pinot Blanc the main whites.
