Churer Rheintal

Sub-zone

Churer Rheintal is a named sub-zone of Bündner Rheintal in Switzerland across 7 communes.

The vines south of the Herrschaft, from Landquart up past Chur to Bonaduz. Zizers and Trimmis hold most of them; Chur keeps a few hectares.
