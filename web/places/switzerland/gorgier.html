Gorgier

Sub-zone

Gorgier is a named sub-zone of La Béroche in Switzerland in Gorgier. It lies 12 km south-west of Peseux and 15 km west of Neuchâtel.

Village of the Béroche, part of La Grande Béroche since 2018. The decree also allows its lakeside locality Chez-le-Bart.
