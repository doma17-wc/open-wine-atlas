Spiez

Sub-zone

Spiez is a named sub-zone of AOC Thunersee in Switzerland in Spiez. It lies around the town of Spiez and 9 km south of Thun.

The best known Lake Thun vineyard, the Spiezer Rebberg. The village list also names Spiezwiler and Faulensee. The RebG names Spiez as a wine commune.
