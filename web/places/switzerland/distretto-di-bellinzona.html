Distretto di Bellinzona

Sub-zone

Distretto di Bellinzona is a named sub-zone of Sopraceneri in Switzerland across 7 communes.

The vines on the slopes of the Ticino valley around Bellinzona and on the edge of the Piano di Magadino. Gudo, Camorino, Sementina, Monte Carasso and Giubiasco, all quarters of Bellinzona since 2017, hold most of them. A wine cooperative was founded at Giubiasco in 1929.
