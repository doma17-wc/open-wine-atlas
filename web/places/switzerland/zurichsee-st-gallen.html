Zürichsee (St. Gallen)

Sub-zone

Zürichsee (St. Gallen) is a named sub-zone of AOC St. Gallen in Switzerland across 8 communes.

The St. Gallen shore of the upper Lake Zurich and the Linth plain. Rapperswil-Jona holds most of the vines and all the Räuschling of the canton. The law also names Rieden, Ernetschwil, Goldingen and St.Gallenkappel, which are now parts of Gommiswald and Eschenbach.
