AOC Zürich

Appellation

AOC Zürich is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Vineyards around Lake Zürich and in the Weinland north of the city. Blauburgunder (Pinot Noir) and Räuschling, an old local white grape.
