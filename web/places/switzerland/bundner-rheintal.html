Bündner Rheintal

Sub-zone

Bündner Rheintal is a named sub-zone of AOC Graubünden in Switzerland across 11 communes.

The Rhine valley from Fläsch on the St. Gallen border up to Bonaduz, with almost all the vines of the canton. Most of them lie on the right bank of the Rhine, on calcareous slate scree. The Bündner Herrschaft in the north is the heart of it, the Churer Rheintal around Chur the smaller rest.
