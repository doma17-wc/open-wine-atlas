AOC Bonvillars

Appellation

AOC Bonvillars is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland across 9 communes. It lies 9.1 km north-east of Yverdon-les-Bains and 29 km south-west of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

The north-west shore of Lake Neuchâtel below the Jura, from Grandson to Concise, the largest wine commune here. Pinot Noir is planted as much as Chasselas. The village of Champagne may put its name on the label only for wine wholly from the commune.
