Distretto di Locarno

Sub-zone

Distretto di Locarno is a named sub-zone of Sopraceneri in Switzerland across 19 communes.

The vines around Lake Maggiore: Locarno, Ascona, Losone, the Gambarogno shore, the Piano di Magadino edge and the mouths of the Centovalli and Verzasca. Bondola, the old red grape of the Ticino, keeps more ground here than in any other district.
