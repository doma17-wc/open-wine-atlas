AOC Côtes de l'Orbe

Appellation

AOC Côtes de l'Orbe is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland across 20 communes.

Vines at the foot of the Jura around Orbe, north of the watershed: the water here flows to the Rhine, not the Rhône. The only region of Vaud where Gamay, not Chasselas, leads.
