Aigle

Sub-zone

Aigle is a named sub-zone of AOC Chablais in Switzerland in Aigle. It lies around the town of Aigle and 33 km south-east of Lausanne.

The town of the Chablais, with its castle among the vines and the wine museum of Vaud.
