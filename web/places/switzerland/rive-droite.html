Rive droite

Sub-zone

Rive droite is a named sub-zone of AOC Genève in Switzerland across 9 communes.

The right bank of the Rhône north-west of Geneva, called the Mandement around Satigny, Dardagny and Russin. More than half of all Geneva vines. Céligny, an enclave between Vaud communes, belongs here too and may sell its wine as La Côte Céligny.
