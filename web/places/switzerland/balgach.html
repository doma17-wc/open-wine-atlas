Balgach

Sub-zone

Balgach is a named sub-zone of St.Galler Rheintal in Switzerland in Balgach. It lies 4.5 km south-west of Lustenau and 10 km west of Dornbirn.

Third of the Rheintal wine communes, between Berneck and Rebstein. Half the area is Blauburgunder.
