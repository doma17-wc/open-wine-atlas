Oberhofen

Sub-zone

Oberhofen is a named sub-zone of AOC Thunersee in Switzerland in Oberhofen am Thunersee. It lies 4.7 km south-east of Thun and 29 km south-east of Bern.

Village on the north shore of Lake Thun, named as a wine commune in the RebG. The KUBR uses Oberhofen Thunersee AOC as its example of a village name.
