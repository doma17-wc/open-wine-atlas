Marin

Sub-zone

Marin is a named sub-zone of Entre-deux-Lacs in Switzerland in La Tène. It lies 7.3 km east of Neuchâtel and 22 km south-west of Biel/Bienne.

Marin is a locality of the commune of La Tène. The designation keeps the old village name.
