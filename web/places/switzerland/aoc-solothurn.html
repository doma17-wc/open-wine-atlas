AOC Solothurn

Appellation

AOC Solothurn is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

Small scattered vineyards: in the Schwarzbubenland and Dorneck south of Basel (Hofstetten-Flüh, Dornach, Rodersdorf), at the Jura foot near Grenchen and Erlinsbach, and in the Bucheggberg. Fungus resistant hybrids such as Maréchal Foch and Cabernet Jura are a large part of the area.
