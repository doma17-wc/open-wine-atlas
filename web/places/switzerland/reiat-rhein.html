Reiat/Rhein

Sub-zone

Reiat/Rhein is a named sub-zone of AOC Schaffhausen in Switzerland across 7 communes.

The scattered vineyards east and south of the Klettgau: the Munot vineyard in the city, the Reiat around Thayngen, the Wolkenstein hills at Stein am Rhein and the Rhine bend at Buchberg and Rüdlingen.
