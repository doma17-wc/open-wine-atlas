Vin des Glaciers

Sub-zone

Vin des Glaciers is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Anniviers.

A white wine from the Sierre district carried up to cellars in the Val d'Anniviers and kept there for years. The 2004 text asked for larch casks in cellars at 1,200 m or higher for at least fifteen years; the 2026 text says only that it is raised in the valley following local tradition. The only Valais traditional name tied to how a wine is aged rather than to a grape.
