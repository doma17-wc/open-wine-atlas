Malans

Sub-zone

Malans is a named sub-zone of Bündner Herrschaft in Switzerland in Malans. It lies 3.8 km north-east of Landquart and 15 km north-east of Chur.

The village of the Completer. The grape was first written down here in 1321 and nearly died out in the 20th century; an old vine at the Completerhalde in Malans kept it alive. Its name comes from Compline, the last prayer of the day, after which the monks were allowed a glass.
