Unteres Thurtal

Sub-zone

Unteres Thurtal is a named sub-zone of AOC Thurgau in Switzerland across 12 communes.

The largest of the three production areas, in the west of the canton: the lower Thur valley around Uesslingen and Neunforn, the Seebachtal with Hüttwilen and Nussbaumen, the Rhine at Diessenhofen and the Lauche valley at Stettfurt. Swiss Wine counts these as four regions.
