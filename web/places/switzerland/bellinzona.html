Bellinzona

Sub-zone

Bellinzona is a named sub-zone of Distretto di Bellinzona in Switzerland in Bellinzona.

Since the 2017 merger the city of Bellinzona has about 148 ha of vines. They lie on the sunny right-bank slopes from Gudo to Monte Carasso and on the left bank at Camorino and Giubiasco.
