Fully Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Fully Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Fully in Switzerland in Fully. It lies around the town of Fully and 20 km west of Sion.

Created in 1996 and the first old regulation brought in line with the cantonal framework, in 2012. Petite Arvine is the flagship of the commune.
