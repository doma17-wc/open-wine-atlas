Lüscherz

Sub-zone

Lüscherz is a named sub-zone of AOC Bielersee in Switzerland in Lüscherz. It lies 11 km south-west of Biel/Bienne and 23 km north-west of Bern.

Lakeside village on the south shore of Lake Biel, a wine commune of the Bielersee region by the RebV.
