Dornach

Sub-zone

Dornach is a named sub-zone of AOC Solothurn in Switzerland in Dornach. It lies 3.5 km south-east of Reinach and 10 km south of Basel.

Vines in the lower Birs valley, mostly fungus resistant varieties.
