AOC Basel-Landschaft

Appellation

AOC Basel-Landschaft is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Vines on the south slopes of the Tafeljura and Faltenjura east and south of Basel. Blauburgunder is almost half the area, Riesling-Silvaner and Kerner lead the whites. Aesch, Maisprach, Sissach and Muttenz are the largest wine communes.

On the label
AOC Basel-Landschaft; Baselbieter Wein if the grapes come from the BL production area. Commune or registered regional name: at least 70% of the grapes from it, at most 30% from other communes of the same production area. Site (Lage): 100%, and the site must be in the cantonal Reblagenverzeichnis (§ 12).
Grapes planted

BL, BS and SO together grow 83 varieties; 18.6% of the area is fungus resistant (interspecific) varieties.
