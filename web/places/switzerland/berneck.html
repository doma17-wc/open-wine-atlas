Berneck

Sub-zone

Berneck is a named sub-zone of St.Galler Rheintal in Switzerland in Berneck. It lies 3.2 km west of Lustenau and 10 km west of Dornbirn.

The largest wine commune of the canton, with vines on the steep slopes above the Rhine valley and some 1,100 years of wine growing. About 30 varieties grow here. The Haus des St. Galler Weins in Berneck sells the wines of 19 estates.
