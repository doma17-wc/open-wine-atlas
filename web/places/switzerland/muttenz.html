Muttenz

Sub-zone

Muttenz is a named sub-zone of AOC Basel-Landschaft in Switzerland in Muttenz. It lies around the town of Muttenz and 7 km south-east of Basel.

Vines on the edge of Basel, the second commune of the canton by harvest in 2025. Sauvignon Blanc is the main white.
