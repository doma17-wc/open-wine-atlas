Schinznach

Sub-zone

Schinznach is a named sub-zone of Schenkenbergertal in Switzerland in Schinznach. It lies 8.9 km north-east of Aarau and 33 km north-west of Zürich.

The merged commune of Schinznach-Dorf and Oberflachs (2014) holds the most vines of any Aargau commune. Riesling-Silvaner is as important here as Pinot Noir.
