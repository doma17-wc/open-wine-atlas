Buus

Sub-zone

Buus is a named sub-zone of AOC Basel-Landschaft in Switzerland in Buus. It lies 6.4 km south of Möhlin and 8 km south-east of Rheinfelden.

A Tafeljura village next to Maisprach, with two thirds Blauburgunder and some Bacchus.
