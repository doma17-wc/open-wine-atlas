AOC Cheyres

Appellation

AOC Cheyres is an appellation of Fribourg / Freiburg in Switzerland in Cheyres, Châbles and Font. It lies 4.4 km south-west of Estavayer-le-Lac and 13 km east of Yverdon-les-Bains.

The only vineyard on the south shore of Lake Neuchâtel, in the Fribourg Broye. Vines between the lake and the Cheyres hillside, sheltered from the bise by the cliffs called Crottes de Cheyres. Pinot Noir covers almost half, then Chasselas.
