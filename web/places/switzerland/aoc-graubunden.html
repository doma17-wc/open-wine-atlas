AOC Graubünden

Appellation

AOC Graubünden is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Switzerland's Pinot Noir stronghold: two thirds of the vines are Blauburgunder. The warm Föhn wind from the south, called the grape cooker, gives long ripe autumns in a valley sheltered by the Rätikon. Four villages of the Bündner Herrschaft hold about 83% of the vineyards. The law names three wine areas: the Bündner Rheintal, the Misox south of the Alps and Brusio in the Valposchiavo.
