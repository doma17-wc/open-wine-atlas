Hallau

Sub-zone

Hallau is a named sub-zone of Klettgau in Switzerland in Hallau. It lies 7.7 km north-east of Wutöschingen and 14 km west of Schaffhausen.

The largest wine commune of German-speaking Switzerland. Blauburgunder covers 57% of its vines. The All Saints monastery of Schaffhausen owned vineyards here by 1100.
