Grône

Sub-zone

Grône is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Grône. It lies 9.5 km east of Sion and 72 km south-east of Lausanne.

On the left bank between Chalais and Bramois. Pinot Noir leads, with unusually large shares of Merlot and Gamaret.
