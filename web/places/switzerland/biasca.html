Biasca

Sub-zone

Biasca is a named sub-zone of Distretto di Riviera in Switzerland in Biasca. It lies around the town of Biasca and 20 km north of Bellinzona.

The main wine commune of the Riviera, where the Blenio and the Leventina meet the Ticino valley. 88% of its 21.8 ha are Merlot.
