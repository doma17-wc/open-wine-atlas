Distretto di Blenio

Sub-zone

Distretto di Blenio is a named sub-zone of Sopraceneri in Switzerland in Acquarossa, Blenio and Serravalle.

The lower Valle di Blenio, north of Biasca, mainly in the communes of Serravalle and Acquarossa. Almost 70% of the vines are Merlot, with a share of disease-resistant whites such as Sauvignac and Johanniter.
