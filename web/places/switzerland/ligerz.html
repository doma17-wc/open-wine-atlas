Ligerz

Sub-zone

Ligerz is a named sub-zone of AOC Bielersee in Switzerland in Ligerz. It lies 9.3 km west of Biel/Bienne and 24 km east of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

Steep terraced village between Schafis and Twann, with the hamlet of Schernelz above it. The land consolidation of Ligerz, Twann and Tüscherz-Alfermée covers 105.5 ha of vines.
