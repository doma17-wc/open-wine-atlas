Zürcher Weinland

Sub-zone

Zürcher Weinland is a named sub-zone of AOC Zürich in Switzerland across 20 communes.

The largest wine region of the canton, in the Andelfingen district north of Winterthur, from the Irchel and the Kohlfirst to the Stammheim valley. Main wine villages are Stammheim, Ossingen, Benken and Trüllikon.
