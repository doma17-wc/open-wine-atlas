Vully fribourgeois

Sub-zone

Vully fribourgeois is a named sub-zone of AOC Vully in Switzerland in Mont-Vully. It lies 4.8 km north-west of Murten/Morat and 11 km south-east of Neuchâtel.

The Fribourg side of the Mont Vully, two thirds of the appellation. Vines on molasse benches facing Lake Morat. Chasselas leads, then Pinot Noir. Freiburger and Traminer are local specialities. The commune of Mont-Vully replaced Bas-Vully and Haut-Vully in the ordinance text.
