Ossingen

Sub-zone

Ossingen is a named sub-zone of Zürcher Weinland in Switzerland in Ossingen. It lies 12 km north of Winterthur and 12 km south-east of Schaffhausen.

A wine village of the Weinland. It founded the first wine growers' cooperative of the canton in 1897, when it still had over 90 ha of vines.
