Stein am Rhein

Sub-zone

Stein am Rhein is a named sub-zone of Reiat/Rhein in Switzerland in Stein am Rhein. It lies 10 km south of Singen and 12 km south-west of Radolfzell.

The small town where the Rhine leaves the Untersee. The vines lie on the Wolkenstein hills toward Hemishofen.
