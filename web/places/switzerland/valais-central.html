Valais central

Sub-zone

Valais central is a named sub-zone of AOC Valais in Switzerland across 19 communes.

The heart of the canton from Chamoson to Sierre, with about two thirds of the Valais vines. The biggest wine communes of Switzerland are here: Chamoson, Sion, Conthey, Savièse, Sierre and Noble-Contrée. Soils change from alluvial fans and limestone to moraine and schist.

Promotion region of Swiss Wine Valais (the IVV); the OVV itself names no wine regions inside AOC Valais. Swiss Wine Valais runs it from Chamoson to Sierre.

Outline: Union of the communes Chamoson, Ardon, Vétroz, Conthey, Nendaz, Sion, Savièse, Grimisuat, Ayent, Vex, Mont-Noble, Saint-Léonard, Grône, Chalais, Chippis, Sierre, Noble-Contrée, Crans-Montana, Lens. The vines are only part of it: the vineyard layer of the map shows where they are.
