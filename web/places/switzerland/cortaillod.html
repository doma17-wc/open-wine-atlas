Cortaillod

Sub-zone

Cortaillod is a named sub-zone of AOC Neuchâtel in Switzerland in Cortaillod. It lies 8 km south-west of Neuchâtel and 17 km south of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

Wine commune on the lake south of Boudry. The decree names Cortaillod as a designation.
