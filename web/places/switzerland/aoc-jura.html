AOC Jura

Appellation

AOC Jura is an appellation of Trois-Lacs in Switzerland.

The youngest Swiss AOC, created for the 2016 vintage. The vineyards were planted from the 1980s on, about half a dozen producers work them, and many grow fungus resistant varieties bred in the region by Valentin Blattner. The largest estate is the Clos des Cantons at Buix in the Ajoie, with 7 ha.
