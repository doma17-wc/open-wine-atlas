Mendrisio

Sub-zone

Mendrisio is a named sub-zone of Distretto di Mendrisio in Switzerland in Mendrisio. It lies around the town of Mendrisio and 11 km north-west of Como.

The centre of the Mendrisiotto and seat of the Cantina Sociale Mendrisio, a cooperative founded in 1949 with about 350 member growers. The merged commune includes the wine villages of Monte San Giorgio, Besazio, Tremona, Meride and Arzo, as well as Rancate, Ligornetto, Genestrerio, Salorino and Capolago, each listed on its own in the cantonal statistics.
