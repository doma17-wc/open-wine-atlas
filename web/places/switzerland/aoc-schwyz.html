AOC Schwyz

Appellation

AOC Schwyz is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland.

Most vines are on the slopes of the Höfe and the March above the upper Lake Zurich, which may also carry the regional AOC Zürichsee. Smaller plots lie at Küssnacht and Immensee. The five main varieties in 2019 were Blauburgunder, Riesling-Silvaner, Cabernet Soyhières, Chardonnay and Räuschling, together about 28 ha. The area was 50 ha in 1886 and 7 ha in 1960.

On the label
AOC Schwyz covers the whole canton. Commune name: at least 60% from the commune, the rest from AOC Schwyz; region, hamlet or site: 100%. Approved extra names include Altendorf (Schloss, St. Johann), Wangen (Eggenbüel, Sonnenrain, Nuolen), Schübelbach (Buttikon), Tuggen (Bürg), Freienbach (Leutschen Klostergut, Insel Ufnau, Joch, Tal), Wollerau (Studenbühl) and Küssnacht (Immensee Sunnehof).
