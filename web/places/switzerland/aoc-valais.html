AOC Valais

Appellation

AOC Valais is an appellation of Valais in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

Switzerland's largest wine canton, in the dry upper Rhône valley protected by the Alps. Chasselas (called Fendant here) and Pinot Noir lead, with a wealth of native varieties: Petite Arvine, Amigne, Cornalin, Humagne Rouge and Heida.
