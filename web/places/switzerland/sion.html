Sion

Sub-zone

Sion is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Sion. It lies around the town of Sion and 65 km south-east of Lausanne.

The cantonal capital and the second wine commune, with vines on the right bank slope. Chasselas led the AOC harvest in 2025 (25%), then Pinot Noir. The commune now includes Bramois, Salins and Les Agettes.
