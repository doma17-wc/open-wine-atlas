Klettgau

Sub-zone

Klettgau is a named sub-zone of AOC Schaffhausen in Switzerland across 9 communes. It lies 3.6 km south-east of Stühlingen and 11 km north-west of Schaffhausen.

The western part of the canton along the German border. It holds about four fifths of the canton's vines, mostly Blauburgunder, with Hallau, Wilchingen with Osterfingen, and Oberhallau the largest wine communes.
