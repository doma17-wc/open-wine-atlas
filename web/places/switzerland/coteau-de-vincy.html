Coteau de Vincy

Sub-zone

Coteau de Vincy is a named sub-zone of AOC La Côte in Switzerland in Gilly. It lies 5.6 km north-east of Gland and 18 km north-west of Thonon-les-Bains.

The rest of Gilly, with the hamlet of Vincy.
