Fribourg / Freiburg

Appellation

Fribourg / Freiburg is an appellation of Trois-Lacs in Switzerland.

Two small vineyards on the Three Lakes. The Vully on the Mont Vully between Lake Morat and Lake Neuchâtel, shared with Vaud, and Cheyres on the south shore of Lake Neuchâtel. Chasselas is the first grape, then Pinot Noir. The Freiburger, a cross also called Freisamer, is a Vully speciality.

Key facts

Type
Cantonal AOC
Vineyard area
116.6 ha Statistiques viticoles fribourgeoises 2025 102.9 ha in the Vully and 13.7 ha at Cheyres, cantonal vineyard cadastre 2025; 56% white. BLW gives 116.5 ha for 2025.
On the label
Two AOCs: Vully Appellation d'origine contrôlée and Cheyres Appellation d'origine contrôlée; the mention AOC must be on the label (art. 13). Single-variety whites other than Chasselas must name the grape, blends say Assemblage de cépages (art. 20a). Sweetening of AOC wine is banned since 1 July 2015.
Grapes planted
Chasselas 32.8%, Pinot Noir 24.4%, Pinot Gris 6.3%, Gamaret 5.5%, Freiburger (Freisamer) 4.2%, Garanoir 3.6%, Gewürztraminer 3.2%, Sauvignon Blanc 3%, Divico 2.2%, Merlot 2.1% Share of the 116.6 ha planted, Statistiques viticoles fribourgeoises 2025. Chasselas 38.3 ha, Pinot Noir 28.5 ha, Pinot Gris 7.3 ha, Gamaret 6.4 ha, Freiburger (Freisamer) 4.9 ha, Garanoir 4.2 ha, Gewürztraminer 3.7 ha, Sauvignon Blanc 3.5 ha, Divico 2.6 ha, Merlot 2.5 ha.
Production rules
Vully minimum sugar: Chasselas 64 °Oe, other whites 70 °Oe, Pinot Noir 75 °Oe, other reds 70 °Oe (art. 17). Yields are set each year by the Direction; for 2024 Vully 1.1 kg/m² (Pinot Noir 1.0), Cheyres 1.25 kg/m² white and 1.1 kg/m² red, Cheyres minimum 62 °Oe white and 70 °Oe red.
