Unteres Aaretal

Sub-zone

Unteres Aaretal is a named sub-zone of AOC Aargau in Switzerland across 5 communes. It lies 6.7 km north of Obersiggenthal and 29 km north-west of Zürich.

The lower Aare and the Surbtal toward the Rhine, mostly in the Zurzach district. Red wine country, with Tegerfelden, the largest wine village of the canton, and Döttingen.
