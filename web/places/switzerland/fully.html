Fully

Sub-zone

Fully is a named sub-zone of Bas-Valais in Switzerland in Fully. It lies around the town of Fully and 20 km west of Sion.

Steep granite slopes on the right bank below the Grand Chavalard, just past the Rhône bend at Martigny. Gamay (22%) and Chasselas lead the harvest, but Fully is known for Petite Arvine, 17% of the AOC grapes in 2025. The Combe d'Enfer is a terraced amphitheatre with about 5 km of dry-stone walls, recorded since 1602.
