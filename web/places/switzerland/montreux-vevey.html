Montreux-Vevey

Sub-zone

Montreux-Vevey is a named sub-zone of AOC Lavaux in Switzerland across 5 communes. It lies around the town of Montreux and 24 km south-east of Lausanne.

The eastern end of Lavaux, from Vevey past Montreux to Chillon. The law writes it as Montreux or Vevey.
