Fricktal

Sub-zone

Fricktal is a named sub-zone of AOC Aargau in Switzerland across 9 communes.

The largest wine region of the canton by Swiss Wine, from the Basel-Landschaft border to the Bözberg and Staffelegg passes, with a balance of red and white. In 2024 the Aargau harvest began here with Solaris on 30 August.
