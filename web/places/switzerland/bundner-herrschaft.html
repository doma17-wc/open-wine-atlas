Bündner Herrschaft

Sub-zone

Bündner Herrschaft is a named sub-zone of Bündner Rheintal in Switzerland across 4 communes. It lies 6.9 km north of Landquart and 18 km north of Chur.

Four villages under the Falknis and the Rätikon, the best known Pinot Noir address of Switzerland. Pinot Noir came from Burgundy around 1630, probably with Bündner mercenaries, and Schloss Salenegg in Maienfeld has made wine since 1068. The Föhn, the warm dry wind from the south, ripens the grapes late into autumn, and the vines sit at about 500 to 650 m on calcareous slate scree. Malans keeps the Completer, a white grape first written down there in 1321.
