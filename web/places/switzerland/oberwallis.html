Oberwallis

Sub-zone

Oberwallis is a named sub-zone of AOC Valais in Switzerland across 19 communes.

The German-speaking upper valley from Salgesch to Brig. Salgesch, Varen and Leuk make most of its wine, mainly Pinot Noir. Visperterminen in the side valley above Visp has vines above 1,000 m, and Steg keeps a Humagne Blanche vine planted in 1750, said to be the oldest in Switzerland.
