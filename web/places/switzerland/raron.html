Raron

Sub-zone

Raron is a named sub-zone of Oberwallis in Switzerland in Raron. It lies 9.1 km north of Visp and 46 km south of Thun.

On the north side of the Rhône west of Visp, with steep vines below the castle hill. Pinot Noir, Chasselas and Heida lead the harvest.
