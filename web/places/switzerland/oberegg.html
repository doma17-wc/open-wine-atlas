Oberegg

Sub-zone

Oberegg is a named sub-zone of AOC Appenzell Innerrhoden in Switzerland in Oberegg. It lies 4.5 km north of Altstätten and 13 km east of Sankt Gallen.

The Bezirk Oberegg holds all the vines of Appenzell Innerrhoden, mostly Solaris.
