Ettingen

Sub-zone

Ettingen is a named sub-zone of AOC Basel-Landschaft in Switzerland in Ettingen. It lies 4.1 km south-west of Reinach and 9 km south-west of Basel.

In the Leimental next to Aesch, with Riesling-Silvaner and Johanniter beside Blauburgunder.
