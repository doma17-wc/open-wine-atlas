Dézaley Grand cru

Grand cruAppellation

Dézaley Grand cru is a grand cru appellation of AOC Lavaux in Switzerland in Puidoux. It lies 6.6 km north-west of Vevey and 11 km south-east of Lausanne.

The steepest and most famous slope of Lavaux, slopes of over 30% falling straight to the lake between Epesses and Rivaz. Cistercian monks of the Haut-Crêt abbey cleared it in the 12th century, and the City of Lausanne has owned the Clos des Moines here since 1802. Almost all Chasselas.

Vintage on the label, no blending, at most 10% from other Lavaux sites, higher minimum sugar. The wines may also be sold as AOC Lavaux Puidoux.
