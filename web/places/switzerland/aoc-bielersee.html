AOC Bielersee

Appellation

AOC Bielersee is an appellation of AOC Bern in Switzerland across 11 communes.

Vines on the steep south-east facing shore of Lake Biel from Vingelz through Tüscherz, Twann and Ligerz to Schafis and La Neuveville, and on the Jolimont around Erlach, Tschugg, Gampelen and Ins. Limestone on the left shore, molasse at Erlach and the St. Petersinsel. A papal letter of 866 records wine here. Chasselas and Pinot Noir lead.

On the label
Bielersee AOC or Lac de Bienne AOC: 100% from the region (KUBR art. 3). Village or hamlet name: 85%. Lage name: 100%, written for example Marnin Schafis Bielersee AOC.
Grapes planted
Pinot Noir 35.4%, Chasselas 27.5%, Pinot Gris 6.4%, Chardonnay 5.5%, Sauvignon Blanc 5.1% Hectares 2025 read from the Lake Biel column of BLW, Das Weinjahr 2025, tables 1.2 and 1.3.
Known producers
More than 60 winegrowers (Bielersee Wein)
Production rules
2025: maximum yield 1.2 kg/m² for Chasselas and Riesling-Silvaner, 1.0 kg/m² for all others. Minimum sugar 63 °Oe for Chasselas, 77 °Oe for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, 81 °Oe for Gewürztraminer and Freisamer. Recommended grapes: Pinot Noir, Diolinoir, Gamaret, Garanoir, Chardonnay, Chasselas, Freisamer, Gewürztraminer, Pinot Blanc, Pinot Gris, Riesling-Silvaner, Sauvignon Blanc, Sylvaner.
