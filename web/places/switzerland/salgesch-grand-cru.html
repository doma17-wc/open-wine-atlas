Salgesch Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Salgesch Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Salgesch in Switzerland in Salgesch. It lies 3.9 km north-east of Sierre and 19 km east of Sion.

The oldest communal grand cru of Switzerland, created by the growers of Salgesch in 1988 with strict limits on the crop per vine. Pinot Noir is the main wine. Salgesch checks bottles against an analytical profile taken at tank tasting, a method used only here.
