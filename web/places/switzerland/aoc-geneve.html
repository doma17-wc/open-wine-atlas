AOC Genève

Appellation

AOC Genève is an appellation of Genève in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

The first AOC of Switzerland, created in 1988, and the third wine canton. Three areas: the Rive droite or Mandement with Satigny, the largest wine commune of the country, Entre Arve et Rhône and Entre Arve et Lac. Gamay, not Chasselas, is the first grape. Half the vineyard is in 23 premiers crus, defined parcels of at least about 1 ha with lower yields.
