Giubiasco

Sub-zone

Giubiasco is a named sub-zone of Bellinzona in Switzerland in Giubiasco. It lies 2 km south of Bellinzona and 19 km north-east of Lugano.

14.9 ha in 2024. A wine cooperative was founded here in 1929.
