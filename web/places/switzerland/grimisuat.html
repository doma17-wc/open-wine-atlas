Grimisuat

Sub-zone

Grimisuat is a named sub-zone of Valais central in Switzerland in Grimisuat. It lies 3.8 km north-east of Sion and 64 km south-east of Lausanne.

On the slope north of Sion between Savièse and Ayent, with Champlan. Pinot Noir and Chasselas lead.
