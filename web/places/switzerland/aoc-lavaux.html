AOC Lavaux

Appellation

AOC Lavaux is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland across 18 communes.

Steep stone terraces over Lake Geneva for about 30 km from the edge of Lausanne to Chillon, built from the 11th and 12th centuries under Benedictine and Cistercian monasteries. A UNESCO World Heritage site since 2007: the listed area holds 702 ha of vines. Chasselas is two thirds of the planting.
