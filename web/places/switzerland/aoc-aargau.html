AOC Aargau

Appellation

AOC Aargau is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

About 380 ha spread over many small villages, mostly on south facing slopes of the main and side valleys. Almost 600 growers, most of them part time on under one hectare. Blauburgunder is about half the area. In the 1880s the canton had 2,700 ha of vines. Since 2025 the law has a Grand Cru Aargau for Pinot Noir.

On the label
The whole canton is one production area (§ 14 Abs. 1). Ortschaft or commune name: at least 60% from there; region name: at least 90%; Lage name: at least 90% (§ 14 Abs. 2 to 4). Other blends are AOC Aargau. The Liebegg publishes the approved Lage names: 77 sites in 37 communes (Feb 2025).

Grand cru
Grand Cru Aargau (since 2025): Blauburgunder only, from approved parcels scoring 140 of 180 points, vines at least 15 years old, at most 0.6 kg/m², at least 95 °Oechsle, 12 months in barrique, no chaptalisation, 18 of 20 points at tasting, release from 1 June of the second year (SAR 915.712 §§ 19b to 19i)
