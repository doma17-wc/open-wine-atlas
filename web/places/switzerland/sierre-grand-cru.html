Sierre Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Sierre Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Valais central in Switzerland across 8 communes. It lies around the town of Sierre and 12 km north-east of Sion.

The only regional grand cru: Sierre with seven neighbours, now the communes of Sierre, Lens, Crans-Montana (without Mollens) and Noble-Contrée. Only parcels in sectors rated particularly well suited may enter. In 2015 about 100 of the 1,000 ha in the perimeter were expected to qualify.
