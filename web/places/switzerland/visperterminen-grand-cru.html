Visperterminen Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Visperterminen Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Visperterminen in Switzerland in Visperterminen. It lies around the town of Visp and 44 km east of Sion.

A single-grape grand cru for Heida from the high terraces, sold by St. Jodern Kellerei as Heida Grand Cru.
