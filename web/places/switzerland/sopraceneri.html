Sopraceneri

Sub-zone

Sopraceneri is a named sub-zone of DOC Ticino in Switzerland across 49 communes.

The part of the canton north of the Monte Ceneri pass: the Bellinzonese, the Locarnese and the alpine valleys. Swiss Wine describes its soils as mainly granite and slate, with cooler alpine nights and lighter Merlot. Bondola, the old red grape of the Ticino, survives mostly here.
