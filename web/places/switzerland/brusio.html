Brusio

Sub-zone

Brusio is a named sub-zone of AOC Graubünden in Switzerland in Brusio. It lies 5.4 km north of Tirano and 80 km north of Brescia.

A quarter of a hectare in the Valposchiavo, the smallest wine area of the canton. Much more wine is made in Poschiavo from grapes grown just over the border in the Valtellina, many of those vineyards owned by Swiss families, but that wine is Italian by origin.
