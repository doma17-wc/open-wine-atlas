Gudo

Sub-zone

Gudo is a named sub-zone of Bellinzona in Switzerland in Gudo. It lies 5.7 km west of Bellinzona and 19 km north of Lugano.

The largest wine quarter of Bellinzona, on the right bank of the Ticino where the valley opens onto the Piano di Magadino. 30.7 ha in 2024, almost all of it DOC.
