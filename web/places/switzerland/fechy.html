Féchy

Sub-zone

Féchy is a named sub-zone of AOC La Côte in Switzerland in Féchy and Bougy-Villars. It lies 10 km west of Morges and 14 km north-west of Thonon-les-Bains.

Féchy and Bougy-Villars, plus the Curzilles area of Aubonne and part of Perroy north of the route de l'Etraz. One of the best known Chasselas names of La Côte.
