Visperterminen

Sub-zone

Visperterminen is a named sub-zone of Oberwallis in Switzerland in Visperterminen. It lies around the town of Visp and 44 km east of Sion.

High in the Vispertal above Visp, with terraces from about 650 m to over 1,000 m, the highest vineyards of Switzerland and among the highest of Europe. Heida (Savagnin Blanc) made 55% of the AOC harvest in 2025, and the name Heyda is written here in 1586. Almost all growers deliver to the St. Jodern cooperative, founded in 1979 with 120 members and now over 500.
