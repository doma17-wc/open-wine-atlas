AOC Vully

Appellation

AOC Vully is an appellation of AOC Vaud in Switzerland in Vully-les-Lacs and Mont-Vully. It lies 10 km south-east of Neuchâtel and 16 km north-west of Fribourg.

The Mont Vully between Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Morat, an appellation shared by Vaud and Fribourg. Vines on molasse facing the Lake of Morat, with the Fribourg villages Môtier, Praz and Lugnorre on the other side of the cantonal line.
