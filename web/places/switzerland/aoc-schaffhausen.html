AOC Schaffhausen

Appellation

AOC Schaffhausen is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

The Blauburgunderland: Pinot Noir covers over half the vines. The law splits the canton into two production areas, the Klettgau in the west with about four fifths of the vines, and Reiat/Rhein around the city, Stein am Rhein and Buchberg. Hallau is the largest wine commune of German-speaking Switzerland. The All Saints monastery in Schaffhausen owned vines in Hallau by 1100.
