Schenkenbergertal

Sub-zone

Schenkenbergertal is a named sub-zone of AOC Aargau in Switzerland in Schinznach, Villnachern and Thalheim (AG). It lies 6.8 km north-east of Aarau and 35 km north-west of Zürich.

A side valley of the Aare west of Brugg. Swiss Wine calls it historically white wine country; in Schinznach Riesling-Silvaner still covers more than Pinot Noir.
