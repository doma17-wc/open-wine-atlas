Corcelles-Cormondrèche

Sub-zone

Corcelles-Cormondrèche is a named sub-zone of AOC Neuchâtel in Switzerland in Corcelles-Cormondrèche. It lies 1.8 km west of Peseux and 5 km west of Neuchâtel.

Former commune above Auvernier, part of the commune of Neuchâtel since 1 January 2021. The wine designation remains.
