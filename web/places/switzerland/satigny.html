Satigny

Sub-zone

Satigny is a named sub-zone of Rive droite in Switzerland in Satigny. It lies 4 km west of Vernier and 9 km west of Geneva.

The largest wine commune of Switzerland, a third of all Geneva vines in one place. Its hamlets give their names to premiers crus: Peissy, Bourdigny, Choully and Peney.
