Stammheim

Sub-zone

Stammheim is a named sub-zone of Zürcher Weinland in Switzerland in Stammheim. It lies 15 km south-west of Singen and 15 km north-east of Winterthur.

The Stammheim valley at the northeast tip of the canton, on the Thurgau border. Since 2019 one commune, formed from Oberstammheim, Unterstammheim and Waltalingen.
