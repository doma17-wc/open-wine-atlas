AOC Thurgau

Appellation

AOC Thurgau is an appellation of Deutschschweiz in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

A small wine canton in the land of apples, with about 240 ha left from more than 2,000 ha two centuries ago. Hermann Müller, born in Thurgau, bred the Müller-Thurgau grape in 1882 and it carries the canton's name. The law groups the vines in three production areas: Unteres Thurtal, Oberes Thurtal and Untersee. Swiss Wine counts six smaller regions.
