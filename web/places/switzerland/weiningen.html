Weiningen

Sub-zone

Weiningen is a named sub-zone of Limmattal in Switzerland in Weiningen (ZH). It lies 3.2 km north-east of Dietikon and 11 km north-west of Zürich.

The main wine village of the Limmattal. In 2024 the canton's first grapes were picked here, Solaris on 27 August.
