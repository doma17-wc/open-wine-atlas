Peseux

Sub-zone

Peseux is a named sub-zone of AOC Neuchâtel in Switzerland in Peseux. It lies 3.1 km west of Neuchâtel and 13 km south of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

Former commune west of the city, part of the commune of Neuchâtel since 1 January 2021. The wine designation remains.
