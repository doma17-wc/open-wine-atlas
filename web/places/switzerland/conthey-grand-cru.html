Conthey Grand Cru

Grand cruAppellation

Conthey Grand Cru is a grand cru appellation of Conthey in Switzerland in Conthey. It lies 7.7 km north-west of Sion and 57 km south-east of Lausanne.

Grand cru since 1999, now under rules of 2018. Each grape has its own sectors on the Coteau de Conthey: Cornalin only sector A, Chasselas A, B, C and K, Pinot Noir most of the slope.
