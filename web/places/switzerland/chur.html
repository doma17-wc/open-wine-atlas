Chur

Sub-zone

Chur is a named sub-zone of Churer Rheintal in Switzerland in Chur. It lies around the town of Chur and 42 km south of Feldkirch.

The cantonal capital still has a few hectares of vines. The outline is the whole commune, which since 2021 includes Haldenstein.
