Calamin Grand cru

Grand cruAppellation

Calamin Grand cru is a grand cru appellation of AOC Lavaux in Switzerland in Epesses. It lies 7.7 km north-west of Vevey and 10 km south-east of Lausanne.

The smallest appellation of Vaud, a single slope below the village of Epesses between Cully and Dézaley. Practically all Chasselas.
