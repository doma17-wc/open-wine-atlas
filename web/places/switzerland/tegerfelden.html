Tegerfelden

Sub-zone

Tegerfelden is a named sub-zone of Unteres Aaretal in Switzerland in Tegerfelden. It lies 8.3 km south-east of Waldshut-Tiengen and 30 km north-west of Zürich.

The largest wine village of the canton, in the Surbtal. Over half of it is Blauburgunder. It has seven approved site names, as many as Döttingen and Würenlingen.
