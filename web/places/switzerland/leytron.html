Leytron

Sub-zone

Leytron is a named sub-zone of Bas-Valais in Switzerland in Leytron. It lies 7.1 km north-east of Fully and 16 km west of Sion.

On the right bank between Saillon and Chamoson, below Ovronnaz. Chasselas, Pinot Noir and Gamay lead, and Leytron is one of the homes of Humagne Rouge. The cantonal estate Domaine du Grand Brûlé, which studies the native varieties, is here.
