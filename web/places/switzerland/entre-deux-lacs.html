Entre-deux-Lacs

Sub-zone

Entre-deux-Lacs is a named sub-zone of AOC Neuchâtel in Switzerland across 5 communes. It lies 7.1 km north-east of Neuchâtel and 16 km south-east of La Chaux-de-Fonds.

The east end of the Neuchâtel vineyard, on the plain and slopes between Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Biel. One of the two regions the AOC decree names. Le Landeron alone held about a tenth of the canton's vines in the early 2000s.
