Hüttwilen

Sub-zone

Hüttwilen is a named sub-zone of Unteres Thurtal in Switzerland in Hüttwilen. It lies 8.8 km north-west of Frauenfeld and 15 km north-east of Winterthur.

The heart of the Seebachtal, between the Seerücken hills and the Thur valley. The commune includes Nussbaumen and Uerschhausen. Hüttwilen has three Lagenwein sites, among them the Stadtschryber.
