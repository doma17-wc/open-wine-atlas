AOC Vaud

Appellation

AOC Vaud is an appellation of Vaud in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Chasselas country on the shores of Lake Geneva. The terraces of Lavaux are a UNESCO World Heritage site since 2007, with Dézaley and Calamin as grand cru appellations. Also La Côte and Chablais.
