Wilchingen

Sub-zone

Wilchingen is a named sub-zone of Klettgau in Switzerland in Wilchingen. It lies 8.5 km east of Wutöschingen and 12 km west of Schaffhausen.

The second wine commune of the canton. Since 2005 it includes Osterfingen, which the statistics still list on its own. Mostly Blauburgunder.
