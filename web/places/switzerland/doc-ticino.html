DOC Ticino

Appellation

DOC Ticino is an appellation of Ticino in Switzerland. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

The wine canton of Italian-speaking Switzerland, and Merlot country: about three quarters of the vines are Merlot. It came after phylloxera, when the first cuttings were handed out in 1906 and Bondola, the old local red, was replaced. The Monte Ceneri pass splits the canton into the Sopraceneri and the Sottoceneri, and the Mendrisio district alone has over a third of the vines. Nearly a third of the Merlot is now pressed as white wine.

On the label
'Ticino' plus a grape variety: at least 90% of that variety. Blends: 'Rosso, Bianco or Rosato del Ticino', naming the main variety and others above 10%, never a single variety. 'Denominazione di origine controllata' in full on the main label, right after the name. Part of canton, district, commune, frazione or land register name may be added if on the production and harvest control certificates; not on non-vintage DOC in 1, 0.5 or 0.2 l bottles with crown or screw cap. 'Castello' only for land with that name in the land register for 20 years. Riserva needs the vintage (Direttive 2024, 3.1, 3.10, 3.13, 3.14).
Grapes planted
Merlot 73.6%, Chardonnay 4.3%, Merlot (registered for Blanc de Noir) 3.9%, American and hybrid red varieties 3.8%, Sauvignon Blanc 1.9%, Cabernet Franc 1.6%, Cabernet Sauvignon 1.4%, Pinot Noir 1%, Johanniter 0.9%, Gamaret 0.8%, Bondola 0.7%, Souvignier Gris 0.6%, Viognier 0.5%, Isabella 0.4%, Kerner 0.4% Canton Ticino 2024, total 1,144.8 ha planted (Sezione dell'agricoltura, Rapporto vendemmia 2024, Allegato 3). BLW gives 832.9 ha of Merlot for 2025.
Known producers
Gambero Rosso Tre Bicchieri 2021: Paolo Basso Wine (Lugano), Angelo Delea (Losone). The Cantina Sociale Mendrisio, founded 1949, has about 350 member growers (ticino.ch).
Nostrano
Nostrano is a traditional name of the IGT tier, not DOC. Any variety registered in the vineyard cadastre of Ticino or the Mesolcina may be used, including the American and hybrid grapes that are banned from DOC blending and from other IGT wine (RViti 2024 art. 18; Direttive 2024 point 5 and annex 1). Swiss Wine notes Bondola is often blended into Nostrano.
Americana
Americana is the local name for American grapes and direct-producer hybrids such as Isabella, Clinton and Noah. Annex 1 of the 2024 directives excludes them from DOC and from IGT, except as Nostrano. The 2024 statistics count 42.9 ha of red American and hybrid varieties plus 4.8 ha of Isabella in the canton, most of it in the Locarnese, the Luganese and the Valle Maggia.
Bianco di Merlot
Much Merlot is pressed as a white wine. In 2023, 1,491,354 kg of Merlot, 29% of the Merlot harvest, was vinified white; in 2024, 43.4 ha were registered as Merlot for Blanc de Noir. The law has no separate category for it: it is DOC Ticino like any other white, and growers must declare red grapes vinified white (RViti 2024 art. 11).
Production rules
DOC grapes may come from Ticino and the Mesolcina (GR) and must be vinified in Ticino or the Mesolcina. Minimum natural sugar 18.0 Brix for red and 16.0 Brix for white; maximum yield 1,000 g/m2 red and 1,200 g/m2 white. Up to 10% blending, never with red or rosé wine from outside the canton (Direttive 2024, 3.3 to 3.8).
Growers
Growers in Ticino 2023, including 111 from outside the canton with vines in Ticino; plus 93 in the Mesolcina. 185 wineries vinified grapes in 2023 (Rapporto vendemmia 2023).
Harvest
2025: 37,903 hl of wine in Ticino, 14,003 hl white and 23,900 hl red; 10,240 hl of the white was made from red grapes (BLW, Das Weinjahr 2025).

Legal basis
