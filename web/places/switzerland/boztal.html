Böztal

Sub-zone

Böztal is a named sub-zone of Fricktal in Switzerland in Böztal. It lies 8.3 km north-west of Brugg and 25 km south-east of Rheinfelden.

Since 2022 one commune formed from Bözen, Effingen, Elfingen and Hornussen in the Sissle valley. Together these villages make one of the largest wine communes of the canton.
