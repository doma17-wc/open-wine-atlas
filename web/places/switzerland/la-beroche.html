La Béroche

Sub-zone

La Béroche is a named sub-zone of AOC Neuchâtel in Switzerland in La Grande Béroche. It lies 13 km south-west of Peseux and 16 km west of Neuchâtel.

The west end of the Neuchâtel vineyard toward the Vaud border. One of the two regions the AOC decree names. Since 2018 its villages form the commune of La Grande Béroche with Bevaix.
