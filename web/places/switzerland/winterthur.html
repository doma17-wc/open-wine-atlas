Winterthur

Sub-zone

Winterthur is a named sub-zone of AOC Zürich in Switzerland across 19 communes.

The city of Winterthur and the wine villages around it. Neftenbach is the largest wine commune. Winterthur has the oldest wine growers' association of the canton.
