Misox

Sub-zone

Misox is a named sub-zone of AOC Graubünden in Switzerland across 6 communes.

The Graubünden valley south of the San Bernardino pass, Italian speaking and open to the Ticino. Here it is Merlot country, not Pinot: Ticino rules apply to grapes, yields and sugar, and Ticino runs the controls. San Vittore, at 279 m the lowest point of the canton, sits on the vineyard border with Lumino in the Ticino.
