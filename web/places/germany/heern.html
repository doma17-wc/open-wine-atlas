Heßern

Vineyard site

Heßern is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 3.1 ha in St.Goarshausen. It makes up 1.6% of the Großlage Loreleyfelsen. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km south-east of Boppard and 24 km south of Koblenz.
