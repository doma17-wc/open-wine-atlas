Kehr

Vineyard site

Kehr is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 176.3 ha in Weinolsheim. It makes up 4.8% of the Großlage Gutes Domtal. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
