Steinacker

Vineyard site

Steinacker is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 51.5 ha in Nieder-Ingelheim. It makes up 1.5% of the Großlage Kaiserpfalz. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 1.7 km east of Ingelheim am Rhein and 17 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
