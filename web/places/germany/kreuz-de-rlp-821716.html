Kreuz

Vineyard site

Kreuz is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 129.3 ha in Kirchheim. It makes up 4.0% of the Großlage Schwarzerde. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.9 km south of Grünstadt and 12 km west of Frankenthal.
