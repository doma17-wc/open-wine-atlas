Backofen

Vineyard site

Backofen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 29.6 ha in Kaub. It makes up 41.1% of the Großlage Herrenberg. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
