Katzenkopf

Vineyard site

Katzenkopf is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 4 ha in Alf. It makes up 0.4% of the Großlage Grafschaft. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
