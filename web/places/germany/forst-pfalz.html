Forst

Vineyard site

Forst is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 176.4 ha in Edesheim. It makes up 13.3% of the Großlage Ordensgut. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.2 km south-east of Edenkoben and 9 km south of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
