Gärkammer

Vineyard site

Gärkammer is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Ahr in Germany, covering 0.6 ha in Ahrweiler. It makes up 0.1% of the Großlage Klosterberg. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 2.3 km west of Bad Neuenahr-Ahrweiler and 22 km south of Bonn.
