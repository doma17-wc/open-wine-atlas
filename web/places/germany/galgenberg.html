Galgenberg

Vineyard site

Galgenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 96.5 ha in Badenheim. It makes up 2.2% of the Großlage St. Rochuskapelle. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.4 km east of Bad Kreuznach and 33 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
