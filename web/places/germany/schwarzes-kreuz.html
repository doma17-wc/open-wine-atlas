Schwarzes Kreuz

Vineyard site

Schwarzes Kreuz is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 94.1 ha in Freinsheim. It makes up 5.1% of the Großlage Kobnert. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.2 km north-east of Bad Dürkheim and 11 km west of Frankenthal.
