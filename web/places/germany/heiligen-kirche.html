Heiligen Kirche

Vineyard site

Heiligen Kirche is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 26.2 ha in Großbockenheim. It makes up 2.1% of the Großlage Grafenstück. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
