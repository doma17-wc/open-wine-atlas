Bruderschaft

Vineyard site

Bruderschaft is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 320.2 ha in Klöerath. It makes up 13.7% of the Großlage Sankt Michael. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.1 km east of Schweich and 19 km east of Trier.
