Liebfrauenmorgen

Sub-zone

Liebfrauenmorgen is a named sub-zone of Rheinhessen in Germany. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.6 km west of Worms and 11 km north of Frankenthal.
