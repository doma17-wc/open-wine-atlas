Goldberg

Vineyard site

Goldberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 313 ha in Freinsheim, Weisenheim am Sand. It makes up 27.3% of the Großlage Rosenbühl. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
