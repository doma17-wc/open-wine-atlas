Falklay

Vineyard site

Falklay is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 97.8 ha in Reil. It makes up 15.6% of the Großlage Vom heißen Stein. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
