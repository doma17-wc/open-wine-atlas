Hasensprung

Vineyard site

Hasensprung is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 57.8 ha in Jugenheim. It makes up 1.7% of the Großlage Kaiserpfalz. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 8 km west of Nieder-Olm and 16 km south-west of Mainz.
