Schloßkapelle

Sub-zone

Schloßkapelle is a named sub-zone of Nahe in Germany. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.8 km north of Bad Kreuznach and 35 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
