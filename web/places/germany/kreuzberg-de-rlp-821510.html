Kreuzberg

Vineyard site

Kreuzberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 96.4 ha in Einselthum, Niefernheim, Zell. It makes up 18.3% of the Großlage Schnepfenflug vom Zellertal. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
