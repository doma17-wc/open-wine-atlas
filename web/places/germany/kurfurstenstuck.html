Kurfürstenstück

Sub-zone

Kurfürstenstück is a named sub-zone of Rheinhessen in Germany. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.3 km west of Wörrstadt and 13 km east of Bad Kreuznach.
