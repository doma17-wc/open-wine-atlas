Saale-Unstrut

Wine region

Saale-Unstrut is a wine region in Germany across 51 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

One of the northernmost quality wine regions of Europe, along the Saale and Unstrut rivers, on shell limestone.
