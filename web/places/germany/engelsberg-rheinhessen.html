Engelsberg

Vineyard site

Engelsberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 560.8 ha in Offstein. It makes up 25.7% of the Großlage Domblick. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.9 km north-east of Heidesheim and 9 km west of Worms.
