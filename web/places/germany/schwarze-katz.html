Schwarze Katz

Sub-zone

Schwarze Katz is a named sub-zone of Mosel in Germany. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 8 km north-east of Traben-Trarbach and 48 km south-west of Koblenz.
