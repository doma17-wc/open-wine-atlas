Treppchen

Vineyard site

Treppchen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 529 ha in Niederemmel, Piesport. It makes up 29.4% of the Großlage Michelsberg. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 13 km south of Wittlich and 23 km north-east of Trier.
