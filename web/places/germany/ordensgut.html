Ordensgut

Sub-zone

Ordensgut is a named sub-zone of Pfalz in Germany. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.2 km south-east of Edenkoben and 9 km south of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
