Rheingrafenberg

Vineyard site

Rheingrafenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 75.6 ha in Frei-Laubersheim. It makes up 2.3% of the Großlage Rheingrafenstein. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 4.4 km south of Bad Kreuznach and 40 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
