Übereltzer

Vineyard site

Übereltzer is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 24.4 ha in Moselkern. It makes up 2.3% of the Großlage Goldbäumchen. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 16 km west of Boppard and 24 km south-west of Koblenz.
