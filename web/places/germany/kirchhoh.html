Kirchhöh

Vineyard site

Kirchhöh is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 241.7 ha in Dierbach. It makes up 12.2% of the Großlage Guttenberg. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
