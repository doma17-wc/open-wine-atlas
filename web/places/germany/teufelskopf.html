Teufelskopf

Vineyard site

Teufelskopf is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 36.3 ha in Ludwigshöhe. It makes up 9.6% of the Großlage Vögelsgärten. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 6.1 km south of Nierstein and 19 km south of Mainz.
