Carlsfelsen

Vineyard site

Carlsfelsen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 80.8 ha in Palzem. It makes up 5.9% of the Großlage Gipfel. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
