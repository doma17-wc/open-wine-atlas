Bleidenberg

Vineyard site

Bleidenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 29.1 ha in Alken. It makes up 3.6% of the Großlage Weinhex. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 13 km west of Lahnstein and 15 km south-west of Koblenz.
