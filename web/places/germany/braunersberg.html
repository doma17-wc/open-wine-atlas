Bräunersberg

Vineyard site

Bräunersberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 7.1 ha in Ottersheim. It makes up 1.3% of the Großlage Schnepfenflug vom Zellertal. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 8.3 km north of Grünstadt and 18 km west of Worms.
