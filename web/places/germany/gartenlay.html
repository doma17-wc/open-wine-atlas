Gartenlay

Vineyard site

Gartenlay is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 73.1 ha in Leutesdorf. It makes up 35.9% of the Großlage Burg Hammerstein. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.9 km north of Andernach and 7 km north-west of Neuwied.
