Herrlich

Sub-zone

Herrlich is a named sub-zone of Pfalz in Germany. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.2 km west of Landau in der Pfalz and 21 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
