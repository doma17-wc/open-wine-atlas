Hölle

Vineyard site

Hölle is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 254.4 ha in Wöllstein. It makes up 7.9% of the Großlage Rheingrafenstein. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.4 km south-east of Bad Kreuznach and 35 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
