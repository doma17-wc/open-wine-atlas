Kronenberg

Vineyard site

Kronenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 70.1 ha in Kallstadt. It makes up 3.8% of the Großlage Kobnert. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
