Sonnenberg

Vineyard site

Sonnenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 288.5 ha in Aspisheim. It makes up 6.7% of the Großlage St. Rochuskapelle. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
