Klostergarten

Vineyard site

Klostergarten is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 86 ha in Filzen. It makes up 3.5% of the Großlage Kurfürstlay. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.8 km west of Bernkastel-Kues and 29 km east of Trier.
