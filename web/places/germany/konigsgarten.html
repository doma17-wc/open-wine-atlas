Königsgarten

Sub-zone

Königsgarten is a named sub-zone of Pfalz in Germany. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 4 km west of Landau in der Pfalz and 18 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
