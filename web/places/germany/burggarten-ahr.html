Burggarten

Vineyard site

Burggarten is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Ahr in Germany, covering 15.2 ha in Heimersheim, Lohrsdorf. It makes up 1.4% of the Großlage Klosterberg. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 4.6 km east of Bad Neuenahr-Ahrweiler and 14 km south of Königswinter.
