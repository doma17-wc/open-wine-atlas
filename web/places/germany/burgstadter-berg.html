Bürgstadter Berg

Vineyard site

Bürgstadter Berg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Franken in Germany in Bürgstadt. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.2 km north-east of Miltenberg and 30 km south of Aschaffenburg.
