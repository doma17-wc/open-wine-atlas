Brückstück

Vineyard site

Brückstück is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 8.9 ha in Winningen. It makes up 1.1% of the Großlage Weinhex. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.3 km south-west of Koblenz and 13 km south of Neuwied.
