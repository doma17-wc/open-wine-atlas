Goldberg

Vineyard site

Goldberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 88.5 ha in Monzernheim. It makes up 3.1% of the Großlage Pilgerpfad. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.5 km south-east of Alzey and 14 km north-west of Worms.
