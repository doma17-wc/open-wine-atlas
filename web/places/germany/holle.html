Hölle

Vineyard site

Hölle is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 7.7 ha in Niederhammerstein. It makes up 3.8% of the Großlage Burg Hammerstein. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
