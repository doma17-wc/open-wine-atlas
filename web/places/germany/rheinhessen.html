Rheinhessen

Wine region

Rheinhessen is a wine region in Germany across 133 communes. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Germany's largest wine region, in rolling hills inside the bend of the Rhine. The Roter Hang at Nierstein and the limestone hills around Westhofen give its top wines.
