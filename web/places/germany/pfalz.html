Pfalz

Wine region

Pfalz is a wine region in Germany across 142 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Warm, sunny region along the Haardt mountains, the northern continuation of Alsace. Famous sites around Forst, Deidesheim and Wachenheim.
