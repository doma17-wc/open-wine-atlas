Maximiner Prälat

Vineyard site

Maximiner Prälat is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 9.6 ha in Kastel-Staadt. It makes up 0.5% of the Großlage Scharzberg. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.8 km south of Saarburg and 16 km north of Merzig.
