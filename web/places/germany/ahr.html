Ahr

Wine region

Ahr is a wine region in Germany across 7 communes. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

One of the northernmost red wine regions in the world, Spätburgunder on steep slate in a narrow valley. Badly hit by the floods of July 2021.
