Sonne

Vineyard site

Sonne is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 29.3 ha in Oberheimbach. It makes up 6.0% of the Großlage Schloß Reichenstein. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
