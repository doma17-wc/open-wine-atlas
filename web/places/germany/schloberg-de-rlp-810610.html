Schloßberg

Vineyard site

Schloßberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 92.4 ha in Rhodt, Rhodt-3.Mittelhainger.. It makes up 7.0% of the Großlage Ordensgut. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.5 km west of Edenkoben and 9 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
