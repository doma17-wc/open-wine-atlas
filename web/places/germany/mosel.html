Mosel

Wine region

Mosel is a wine region in Germany across 131 communes. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.

Riesling on steep slate slopes along the Mosel, Saar and Ruwer. Some of the steepest vineyards in Europe. The light, low-alcohol style of Kabinett and Spätlese is world-famous.
