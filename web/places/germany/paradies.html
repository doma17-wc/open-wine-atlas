Paradies

Vineyard site

Paradies is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 182.9 ha in Kröv. It makes up 47.9% of the Großlage Nacktarsch. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 6.7 km north of Bernkastel-Kues and 40 km north-east of Trier.
