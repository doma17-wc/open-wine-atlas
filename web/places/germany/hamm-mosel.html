Hamm

Vineyard site

Hamm is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 14.9 ha in Winningen. It makes up 1.8% of the Großlage Weinhex. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.1 km south-west of Koblenz and 14 km south of Neuwied.
