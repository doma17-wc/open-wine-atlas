Schloß

Vineyard site

Schloß is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 411.5 ha in Dienheim, Oppenheim. It makes up 10.1% of the Großlage Krötenbrunnen. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
