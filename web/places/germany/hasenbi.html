Hasenbiß

Vineyard site

Hasenbiß is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 70.7 ha in Osthofen. It makes up 2.5% of the Großlage Pilgerpfad. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
