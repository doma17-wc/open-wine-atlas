Schloß

Vineyard site

Schloß is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 4.8 ha in Edesheim. It makes up 0.4% of the Großlage Ordensgut. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.5 km south of Edenkoben and 10 km south of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
