Kurfürst

Vineyard site

Kurfürst is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 101 ha in Mußbach. It makes up 9.3% of the Großlage Meerspinne. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.6 km east of Neustadt an der Weinstraße and 22 km west of Ludwigshafen am Rhein.
