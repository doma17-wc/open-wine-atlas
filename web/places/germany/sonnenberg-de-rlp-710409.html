Sonnenberg

Vineyard site

Sonnenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 322.4 ha in Vendersheim. It makes up 14.5% of the Großlage Kurfürstenstück. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.1 km north-west of Wörrstadt and 15 km east of Bad Kreuznach.
