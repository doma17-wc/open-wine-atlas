Fuchsmantel

Vineyard site

Fuchsmantel is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 131.1 ha in Bad Dürkheim, Seebach, Wachenheim. It makes up 21.7% of the Großlage Schenkenböhl. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.6 km west of Bad Dürkheim and 11 km north of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
