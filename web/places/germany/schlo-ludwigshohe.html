Schloß Ludwigshöhe

Sub-zone

Schloß Ludwigshöhe is a named sub-zone of Pfalz in Germany. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies next to Edenkoben and 7 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
