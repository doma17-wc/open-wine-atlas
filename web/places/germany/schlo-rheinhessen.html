Schloß

Vineyard site

Schloß is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 214.7 ha in Uelversheim. It makes up 5.3% of the Großlage Krötenbrunnen. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.2 km south-west of Nierstein and 18 km south of Mainz.
