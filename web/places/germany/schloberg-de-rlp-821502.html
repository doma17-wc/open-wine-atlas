Schloßberg

Vineyard site

Schloßberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 3.5 ha in Bolanden. It makes up 0.7% of the Großlage Schnepfenflug vom Zellertal. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.5 km south of Kirchheimbolanden and 28 km north-east of Kaiserslautern.
