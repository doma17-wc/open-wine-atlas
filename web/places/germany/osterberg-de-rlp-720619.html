Osterberg

Vineyard site

Osterberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 300 ha in Spiesheim. It makes up 9.1% of the Großlage Petersberg. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.6 km north-east of Alzey and 21 km south-west of Mainz.
