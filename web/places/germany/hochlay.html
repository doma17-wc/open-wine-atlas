Hochlay

Vineyard site

Hochlay is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 3.7 ha in Sehl. It makes up 0.4% of the Großlage Goldbäumchen. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies next to Cochem and 38 km south-west of Koblenz.
