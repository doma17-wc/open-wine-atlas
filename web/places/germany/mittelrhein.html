Mittelrhein

Wine region

Mittelrhein is a wine region in Germany across 51 communes. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

Steep slate terraces in the Rhine gorge between Bingen and Bonn, part of the UNESCO Upper Middle Rhine Valley.
