Römerberg

Vineyard site

Römerberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 666.5 ha in Alsheim, Dorn-Dürkheim. It makes up 41.1% of the Großlage Rheinblick. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
