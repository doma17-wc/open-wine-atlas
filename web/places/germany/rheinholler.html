Rheinhöller

Vineyard site

Rheinhöller is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 20.6 ha in Linz a. Rhein. It makes up 10.1% of the Großlage Burg Hammerstein. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 3.6 km north-east of Sinzig and 13 km south of Königswinter.
