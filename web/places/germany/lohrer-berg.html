Löhrer Berg

Vineyard site

Löhrer Berg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 45.8 ha in Langenlonsheim. It makes up 1.4% of the Großlage Schloßkapelle. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
