Brückchen

Vineyard site

Brückchen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 14.3 ha in Nierstein. It makes up 1.4% of the Großlage Spiegelberg. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies next to Nierstein and 14 km south of Mainz.
