Goldstückchen

Vineyard site

Goldstückchen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 349.3 ha in Armsheim. It makes up 8.6% of the Großlage Adelberg. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
