Schwarzer Letten

Vineyard site

Schwarzer Letten is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 41.8 ha in Edenkoben. It makes up 5.6% of the Großlage Schloß Ludwigshöhe. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies next to Edenkoben and 8 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
