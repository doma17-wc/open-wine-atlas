Pechstein

Vineyard site

Pechstein is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 18.8 ha in Forst. It makes up 4.3% of the Großlage Mariengarten. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Forster Pechstein: site named after the dark basalt in its soil, from an old volcanic outcrop above Forst.
