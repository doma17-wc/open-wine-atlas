Alte Lay

Vineyard site

Alte Lay is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Ahr in Germany, covering 19.1 ha in Ahrweiler. It makes up 1.8% of the Großlage Klosterberg. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
