Kaiserberg

Vineyard site

Kaiserberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 46.2 ha in Nußdorf. It makes up 2.4% of the Großlage Bischofskreuz. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 1.9 km north of Landau in der Pfalz and 15 km south of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
