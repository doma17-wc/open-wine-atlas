Baden

Wine region

Baden is a wine region in Germany across 400 communes. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Germany's warmest and most southerly region, from Lake Constance to Heidelberg. Strong in Pinot varieties, especially in the Kaiserstuhl.
