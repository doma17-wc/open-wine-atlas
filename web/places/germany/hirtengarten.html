Hirtengarten

Vineyard site

Hirtengarten is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 54.6 ha in Oberbillig. It makes up 4.0% of the Großlage Gipfel. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5 km west of Konz and 12 km south-west of Trier.
