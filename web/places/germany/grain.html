Grain

Vineyard site

Grain is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 22.6 ha in Neustadt. It makes up 3.3% of the Großlage Rebstöckel. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.1 km south-east of Neustadt an der Weinstraße and 19 km west of Speyer.
