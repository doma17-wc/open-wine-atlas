Großer Herrgott

Vineyard site

Großer Herrgott is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 173.6 ha in Wintrich. It makes up 7.0% of the Großlage Kurfürstlay. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
