Kallenberg

Vineyard site

Kallenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 167.1 ha in Bubenheim. It makes up 5.0% of the Großlage Kaiserpfalz. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 6.2 km south of Ingelheim am Rhein and 16 km west of Mainz.
