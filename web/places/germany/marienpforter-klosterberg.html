Marienpforter Klosterberg

Vineyard site

Marienpforter Klosterberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 25.4 ha in Waldböckelheim. It makes up 2.4% of the Großlage Burgweg. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
