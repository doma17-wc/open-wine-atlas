Königshof

Vineyard site

Königshof is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 7.4 ha in Vallendar. It makes up 6.1% of the Großlage Marksburg. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.2 km south-east of Bendorf and 6 km north-east of Koblenz.
