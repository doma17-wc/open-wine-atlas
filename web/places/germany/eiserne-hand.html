Eiserne Hand

Vineyard site

Eiserne Hand is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 67.6 ha in Guntersblum. It makes up 1.7% of the Großlage Krötenbrunnen. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
