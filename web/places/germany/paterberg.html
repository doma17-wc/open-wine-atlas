Paterberg

Vineyard site

Paterberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 204.3 ha in Nierstein. It makes up 20.3% of the Großlage Spiegelberg. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
