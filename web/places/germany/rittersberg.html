Rittersberg

Vineyard site

Rittersberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 406.9 ha in Ilbesheim. It makes up 13.1% of the Großlage Herrlich. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.1 km west of Landau in der Pfalz and 20 km south-west of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
