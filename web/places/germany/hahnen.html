Hahnen

Vineyard site

Hahnen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 122.2 ha in Weisenheim am Sand. It makes up 10.7% of the Großlage Rosenbühl. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.6 km east of Herxheim am Berg and 8 km west of Frankenthal.
