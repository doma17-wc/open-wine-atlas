Weinkammer

Vineyard site

Weinkammer is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 22.9 ha in Enkirch. It makes up 1.4% of the Großlage Schwarzlay. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
