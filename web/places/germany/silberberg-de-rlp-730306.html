Silberberg

Vineyard site

Silberberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 773.7 ha in Mölsheim, Monsheim. It makes up 35.4% of the Großlage Domblick. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
