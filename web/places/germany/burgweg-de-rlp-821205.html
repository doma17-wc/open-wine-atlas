Burgweg

Vineyard site

Burgweg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 189.2 ha in Lambsheim, Weisenheim am Sand. It makes up 16.5% of the Großlage Rosenbühl. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 4.1 km north of Maxdorf and 6 km west of Frankenthal.
