Gollenberg

Vineyard site

Gollenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 53.1 ha in Bellheim, Knittelsheim. It makes up 1.8% of the Großlage Trappenberg. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
