Kirchberg

Vineyard site

Kirchberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 110.1 ha in Veldenz. It makes up 4.4% of the Großlage Kurfürstlay. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.5 km west of Bernkastel-Kues and 32 km east of Trier.
