Ritterpfad

Vineyard site

Ritterpfad is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 29.8 ha in Kanzem, Wawern. It makes up 1.7% of the Großlage Scharzberg. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
