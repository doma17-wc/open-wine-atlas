Backöfchen

Vineyard site

Backöfchen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 226.9 ha in Wallhausen. It makes up 26.8% of the Großlage Pfarrgarten. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
