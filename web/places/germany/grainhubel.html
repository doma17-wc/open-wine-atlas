Grainhübel

Vineyard site

Grainhübel is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 8.4 ha in Deidesheim. It makes up 1.9% of the Großlage Mariengarten. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.8 km north-east of Neustadt an der Weinstraße and 22 km west of Mannheim.
