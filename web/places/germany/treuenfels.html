Treuenfels

Vineyard site

Treuenfels is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 5.4 ha in Altenbamberg. It makes up 0.5% of the Großlage Burgweg. Regional bedrock: acid volcanic rocks. It lies 7 km south-west of Bad Kreuznach and 45 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
