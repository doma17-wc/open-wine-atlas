Goldtröpfchen

Vineyard site

Goldtröpfchen is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 84.8 ha in Dhron, Niederemmel, Piesport. It makes up 4.7% of the Großlage Michelsberg. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 11 km south of Wittlich and 23 km north-east of Trier.

Piesporter Goldtröpfchen: south-facing amphitheatre of slate around Piesport, giving rich, fruity Riesling.
