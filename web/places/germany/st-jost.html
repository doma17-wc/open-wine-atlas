St. Jost

Vineyard site

St. Jost is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 12.8 ha in Bacharach, Steeg. It makes up 3.5% of the Großlage Schloß Stahleck. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
