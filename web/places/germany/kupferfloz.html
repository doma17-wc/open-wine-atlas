Kupferflöz

Vineyard site

Kupferflöz is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mittelrhein in Germany, covering 4.5 ha in Dörscheid, Kaub. It makes up 6.2% of the Großlage Herrenberg. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 18 km north-west of Bingen am Rhein and 35 km west of Wiesbaden.
