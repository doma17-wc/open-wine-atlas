Lerchenberg

Vineyard site

Lerchenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 50.4 ha in Kapsweyer. It makes up 2.5% of the Großlage Guttenberg. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.2 km east of Wissembourg and 18 km south-west of Landau in der Pfalz.
