Schnepp

Vineyard site

Schnepp is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 17.6 ha in Obersülzen. It makes up 0.5% of the Großlage Schwarzerde. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
