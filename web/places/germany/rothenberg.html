Rothenberg

Vineyard site

Rothenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 89.2 ha in Rümmelsheim. It makes up 2.7% of the Großlage Schloßkapelle. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 5.4 km south-west of Bingen am Rhein and 10 km north of Bad Kreuznach.
