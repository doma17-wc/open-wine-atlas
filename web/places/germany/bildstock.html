Bildstock

Vineyard site

Bildstock is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 96.8 ha in Nierstein, Schwabsburg. It makes up 9.6% of the Großlage Spiegelberg. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 1.7 km south-west of Nierstein and 14 km south of Mainz.
