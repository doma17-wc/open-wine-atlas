Morstein

Vineyard site

Morstein is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 179.1 ha in Westhofen. It makes up 6.6% of the Großlage Bergkloster. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Westhofener Morstein: limestone-rich site in the hills of southern Rheinhessen, famous for powerful dry Riesling.
