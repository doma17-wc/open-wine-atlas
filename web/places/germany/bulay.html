Bußlay

Vineyard site

Bußlay is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 134.1 ha in Erden. It makes up 8.3% of the Großlage Schwarzlay. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.4 km east of Wittlich and 37 km north-east of Trier.
