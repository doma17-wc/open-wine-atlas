Marbach

Vineyard site

Marbach is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Nahe in Germany, covering 17.5 ha in Sobernheim. It makes up 1.4% of the Großlage Paradiesgarten. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 2 km west of Bad Sobernheim and 18 km west of Bad Kreuznach.
