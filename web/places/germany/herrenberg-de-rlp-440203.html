Herrenberg

Vineyard site

Herrenberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Mosel in Germany, covering 11.3 ha in Alf. It makes up 1.2% of the Großlage Grafschaft. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
