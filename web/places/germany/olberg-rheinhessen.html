Ölberg

Vineyard site

Ölberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 63.4 ha in Grolsheim. It makes up 1.5% of the Großlage St. Rochuskapelle. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 9.1 km north-east of Bad Kreuznach and 30 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
