Osterberg

Vineyard site

Osterberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 218 ha in Eßingen. It makes up 7.4% of the Großlage Trappenberg. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.8 km north-east of Landau in der Pfalz and 12 km south of Neustadt an der Weinstraße.
