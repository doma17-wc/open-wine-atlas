Letten

Vineyard site

Letten is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Pfalz in Germany, covering 141.4 ha in Deidesheim. It makes up 21.9% of the Großlage Schnepfenflug an der Weinstraße. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
