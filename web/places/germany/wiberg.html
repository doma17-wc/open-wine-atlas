Wißberg

Vineyard site

Wißberg is a registered vineyard site (Einzellage) of Rheinhessen in Germany, covering 301 ha in Sprendlingen. It makes up 7.9% of the Großlage Abtey. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 9.8 km east of Bad Kreuznach and 31 km south-west of Wiesbaden.
