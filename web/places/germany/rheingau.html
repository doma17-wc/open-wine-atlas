Rheingau

Wine region

Rheingau is a wine region in Germany across 11 communes. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

A single south-facing slope along the Rhine where it flows west for about 30 km. Riesling dominates. Schloss Johannisberg and Kloster Eberbach shaped German wine history.
