Corton-Charlemagne

Grand cruAppellation

Corton-Charlemagne is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 71.9 ha in Aloxe-Corton, Ladoix-Serrigny and Pernand-Vergelesses. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.5 km north of Beaune and 29 km south-west of Dijon.

White grand cru on the upper slopes of the hill of Corton, planted with Chardonnay. Tradition links it to the emperor Charlemagne, who is said to have owned vines here.
