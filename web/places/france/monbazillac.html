Monbazillac

Appellation

Monbazillac is an appellation in France, covering 4,827.1 ha across 5 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
