Côtes d'Auvergne Chanturgue

Sub-zone

Côtes d'Auvergne Chanturgue is a named sub-zone of Côtes d'Auvergne in France, covering 75.9 ha in Cebazat and Clermont-Ferrand. That is 2.5% of the Côtes d'Auvergne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.5 km north-east of Clermont-Ferrand and 71 km south of Montluçon.
