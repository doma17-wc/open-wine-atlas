Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Altenberg de Bergheim is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 33.9 ha in Bergheim. The rules allow white wine, based on Riesling. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 9 km south-west of Sélestat and 14 km north of Colmar.
