Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Morey-Saint-Denis in France, covering 85.2 ha in Morey-Saint-Denis. That is 56.6% of the Morey-Saint-Denis appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Aligoté. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
