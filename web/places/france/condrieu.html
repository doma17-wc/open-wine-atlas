Condrieu

Appellation

Condrieu is an appellation in France, covering 323.2 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Viognier. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 6.4 km north-west of Roussillon and 20 km south-east of Saint-Chamond.

Northern Rhône appellation for white wine from Viognier only, grown on steep granite terraces. Château-Grillet is a separate appellation inside it.
