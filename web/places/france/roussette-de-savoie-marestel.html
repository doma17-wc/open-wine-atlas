Roussette de Savoie Marestel

Sub-zone

Roussette de Savoie Marestel is a named sub-zone of Roussette de Savoie in France, covering 68.3 ha in Jongieux and Lucey. That is 1.1% of the Roussette de Savoie appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Altesse. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 11 km north-west of Aix-les-Bains and 22 km north of Chambéry.
