Mazoyères-Chambertin

Grand cruAppellation

Mazoyères-Chambertin is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 18.6 ha in Gevrey-Chambertin. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 9.8 km south-west of Chenôve and 12 km south-west of Dijon.

Grand cru below Latricières. Its wines may be labelled Charmes-Chambertin, so the Mazoyères name is rarely seen.
