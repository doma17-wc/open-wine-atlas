Saint-Julien

Appellation

Saint-Julien is an appellation in France, covering 1,048.9 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Compact Médoc commune between Pauillac and Margaux, with no first growth but a remarkable run of second growths, such as the three Léovilles and Ducru-Beaucaillou.
