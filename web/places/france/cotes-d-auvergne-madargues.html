Côtes d'Auvergne Madargues

Sub-zone

Côtes d'Auvergne Madargues is a named sub-zone of Côtes d'Auvergne in France, covering 32 ha in Riom. That is 1.1% of the Côtes d'Auvergne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
