Chablis premier cru Les Beauregards

Premier cruVineyard site

Chablis premier cru Les Beauregards is a premier cru climat of Chablis in France, covering 21.3 ha in Courgis. That is 0.4% of the Chablis appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km south-east of Auxerre and 64 km south-west of Troyes.
