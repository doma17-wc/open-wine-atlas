Gevrey-Chambertin

Appellation

Gevrey-Chambertin is an appellation in France, covering 540.9 ha in Brochon and Gevrey-Chambertin. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 8.3 km south-west of Chenôve and 11 km south-west of Dijon.

The largest village of the Côte de Nuits, home to nine grands crus. Its reds are often the most structured of the Côte, with Clos Saint-Jacques as the most famous premier cru.
