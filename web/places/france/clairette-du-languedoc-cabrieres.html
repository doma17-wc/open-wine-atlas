Clairette du Languedoc Cabrières

Sub-zone

Clairette du Languedoc Cabrières is a named sub-zone of Clairette du Languedoc in France, covering 2,794.7 ha in Cabrieres. That is 33.1% of the Clairette du Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Clairette. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 15 km north of Pézenas and 29 km north-east of Béziers.
