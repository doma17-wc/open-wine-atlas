Viré-Clessé complété par une dénomination de climat

Sub-zone

Viré-Clessé complété par une dénomination de climat is a named sub-zone of Viré-Clessé in France, covering 519.2 ha across 4 communes. That is 94.7% of the Viré-Clessé appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north of Mâcon and 38 km south of Chalon-sur-Saône.
