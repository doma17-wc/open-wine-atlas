Côtes de Provence la Londe

Sub-zone

Côtes de Provence la Londe is a named sub-zone of Côtes de Provence in France, covering 3,338 ha across 4 communes. That is 5.2% of the Côtes de Provence appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
