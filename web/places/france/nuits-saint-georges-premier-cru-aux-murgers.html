Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Aux Murgers

Premier cruVineyard site

Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Aux Murgers is a premier cru climat of Nuits-Saint-Georges in France, covering 4.9 ha in Nuits-Saint-Georges. That is 1.5% of the Nuits-Saint-Georges appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 19 km south-west of Dijon.
