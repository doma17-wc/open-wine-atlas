Alsace Klevener de Heiligenstein

Sub-zone

Alsace Klevener de Heiligenstein is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 91.2 ha across 5 communes. That is 0.5% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
