Saint-Emilion grand cru

Grand cruAppellation

Saint-Emilion grand cru is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 7,102.9 ha across 9 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km south-east of Libourne and 38 km east of Bordeaux.

Right-bank appellation around the medieval town of Saint-Émilion, dominated by Merlot and Cabernet Franc on limestone plateau, slopes and sand. It has its own classification, revised about every ten years.
