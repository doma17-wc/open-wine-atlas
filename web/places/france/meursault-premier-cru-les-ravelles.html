Meursault premier cru Les Ravelles

Premier cruVineyard site

Meursault premier cru Les Ravelles is a premier cru climat of Meursault in France, covering 1.2 ha in Meursault. That is 0.3% of the Meursault appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.8 km north of Chagny and 22 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
