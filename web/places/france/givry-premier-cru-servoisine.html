Givry premier cru Servoisine

Premier cruVineyard site

Givry premier cru Servoisine is a premier cru climat of Givry in France, covering 7.4 ha in Givry. That is 2.0% of the Givry appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
