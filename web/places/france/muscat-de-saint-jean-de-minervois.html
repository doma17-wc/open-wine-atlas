Muscat de Saint-Jean-de-Minervois

Appellation

Muscat de Saint-Jean-de-Minervois is an appellation in France, covering 409 ha in Saint-Jean-De-Minervois. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
