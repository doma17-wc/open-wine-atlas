Mercurey

Appellation

Mercurey is an appellation in France, covering 790.5 ha in Mercurey and Saint-Martin-Sous-Montaigu. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.8 km north-west of Châtenoy-le-Royal and 11 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
