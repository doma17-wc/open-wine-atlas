Beaune

Appellation

Beaune is an appellation in France, covering 473.7 ha in Beaune. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Wine capital of Burgundy and home of the Hospices de Beaune auction. A large number of premiers crus, many owned by the old négociant houses.
