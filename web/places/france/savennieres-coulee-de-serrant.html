Savennières Coulée de Serrant

Appellation

Savennières Coulée de Serrant is an appellation in France in Savennières. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.

Monopole inside Savennières owned by the Joly family. Nicolas Joly is one of the pioneers of biodynamic viticulture.
