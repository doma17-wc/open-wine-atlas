Côtes de Millau

Appellation

Côtes de Millau is an appellation in France, covering 1,014 ha across 17 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Syrah and Chenin. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
