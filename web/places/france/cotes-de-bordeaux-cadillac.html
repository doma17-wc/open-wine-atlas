Côtes de Bordeaux Cadillac

Sub-zone

Côtes de Bordeaux Cadillac is a named sub-zone of Côtes de Bordeaux in France, covering 12,002.8 ha across 38 communes. That is 33.4% of the Côtes de Bordeaux appellation area. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
