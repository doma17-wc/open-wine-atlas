Vin de Corse Calvi ou Corse Calvi

Sub-zone

Vin de Corse Calvi ou Corse Calvi is a named sub-zone of Vin de Corse in France, covering 8,649.8 ha across 28 communes. That is 12.2% of the Vin de Corse appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
