Saint-Aubin premier cru Les Combes au Sud

Premier cruVineyard site

Saint-Aubin premier cru Les Combes au Sud is a premier cru climat of Saint-Aubin in France, covering 7.8 ha in Saint-Aubin. That is 3.3% of the Saint-Aubin appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
