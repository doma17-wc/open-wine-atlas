Pouilly-Loché premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Pouilly-Loché premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Pouilly-Loché in France, covering 7.1 ha in Macon. That is 20.2% of the Pouilly-Loché appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.4 km south-west of Mâcon and 36 km north-west of Bourg-en-Bresse.
