Volnay premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Volnay premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Volnay in France, covering 143.3 ha in Meursault and Volnay. That is 59.3% of the Volnay appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
