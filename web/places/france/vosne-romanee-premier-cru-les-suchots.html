Vosne-Romanée premier cru Les Suchots

Premier cruVineyard site

Vosne-Romanée premier cru Les Suchots is a premier cru climat of Vosne-Romanée in France, covering 13.1 ha in Vosne-Romanee. That is 5.6% of the Vosne-Romanée appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.2 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 17 km south-west of Dijon.
