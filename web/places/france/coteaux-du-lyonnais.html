Coteaux du Lyonnais

Appellation

Coteaux du Lyonnais is an appellation in France, covering 7,692.8 ha across 48 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Aligoté and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
