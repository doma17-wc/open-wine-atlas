Vin de Corse

Appellation

Vin de Corse is an appellation in France, covering 71,056.5 ha across 146 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
