Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Clisson

Sub-zone

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Clisson is a named sub-zone of Muscadet Sèvre et Maine in France, covering 298.4 ha across 9 communes. That is 2.6% of the Muscadet Sèvre et Maine appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Melon. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies around the town of Clisson and 27 km south-east of Nantes.
