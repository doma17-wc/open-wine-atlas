Pouilly-Fumé

Appellation

Pouilly-Fumé is an appellation in France, covering 2,026.5 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Sauvignon and Chasselas. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km south of Cosne-Cours-sur-Loire and 48 km east of Bourges.

Sauvignon Blanc from the right bank of the Loire facing Sancerre. Flint-rich soils are often credited for its smoky note.
