Pacherenc du Vic-Bilh

Appellation

Pacherenc du Vic-Bilh is an appellation in France, covering 6,394.5 ha across 36 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Courbu, Gros Manseng, Petit Courbu and Petit Manseng. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
