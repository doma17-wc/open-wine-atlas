Languedoc Saint-Saturnin

Sub-zone

Languedoc Saint-Saturnin is a named sub-zone of Languedoc in France, covering 814.4 ha across 4 communes. That is 0.3% of the Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.7 km north-west of Gignac and 32 km north-west of Montpellier.
