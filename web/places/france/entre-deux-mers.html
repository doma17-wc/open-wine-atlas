Entre-deux-Mers

Appellation

Entre-deux-Mers is an appellation in France, covering 65,426.5 ha across 131 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
