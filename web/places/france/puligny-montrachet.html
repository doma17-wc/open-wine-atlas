Puligny-Montrachet

Appellation

Puligny-Montrachet is an appellation in France, covering 236.7 ha in Puligny-Montrachet. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Village shared with Chassagne for Montrachet and Bâtard-Montrachet, and home to Chevalier-Montrachet and Bienvenues. Its whites are known for precision and floral tension.
