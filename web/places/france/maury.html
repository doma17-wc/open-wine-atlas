Maury

Appellation

Maury is an appellation in France, covering 4,324.7 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 16 km north of Ille-sur-Têt and 26 km north-west of Perpignan.
