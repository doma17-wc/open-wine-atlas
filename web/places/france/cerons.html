Cérons

Appellation

Cérons is an appellation in France, covering 1,817.7 ha in Cerons, Illats and Podensac. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 12 km north-west of Langon and 30 km south-east of Bordeaux.
