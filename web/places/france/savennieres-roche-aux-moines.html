Savennières Roche aux Moines

Appellation

Savennières Roche aux Moines is an appellation in France, covering 34.6 ha in Savennieres. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 4.3 km south-west of Bouchemaine and 11 km south-west of Angers.
