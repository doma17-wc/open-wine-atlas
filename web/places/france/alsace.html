Alsace

Appellation

Alsace is an appellation in France, covering 20,108 ha across 117 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Alsace lies in the rain shadow of the Vosges, one of the driest regions of France. Wines are labelled by grape variety, and the 51 grand cru sites each have their own appellation.
