Anjou Villages

Appellation

Anjou Villages is an appellation in France, covering 12,300.1 ha across 25 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc and Cabernet Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
