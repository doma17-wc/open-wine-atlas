Saint-Mont

Appellation

Saint-Mont is an appellation in France, covering 10,794.5 ha across 46 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Tannat, Cabernet Sauvignon, Fer and Gros Manseng. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
