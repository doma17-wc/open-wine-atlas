Vin de Corse ou Corse Figari

Sub-zone

Vin de Corse ou Corse Figari is a named sub-zone of Vin de Corse in France, covering 4,952.7 ha in Figari, Monacia-D'Aullene and Pianottoli-Caldarello. That is 7.0% of the Vin de Corse appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 15 km west of Porto-Vecchio and 54 km south-east of Ajaccio.
