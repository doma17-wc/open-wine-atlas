Quarts de Chaume

Appellation

Quarts de Chaume is an appellation in France, covering 48.8 ha in Rochefort-Sur-Loire. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km south-west of Bouchemaine and 18 km south-west of Angers.
