Pommard

Appellation

Pommard is an appellation in France, covering 336.1 ha in Pommard. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 3.5 km west of Beaune and 26 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.

Côte de Beaune village for firm, dark reds. Rugiens and Épenots are its most famous premiers crus, and growers are pushing for grand cru status.
