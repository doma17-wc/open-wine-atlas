Crozes-Hermitage

Appellation

Crozes-Hermitage is an appellation in France, covering 4,226.4 ha across 11 communes. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Syrah, Marsanne and Roussanne. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 1.8 km north-east of Tournon-sur-Rhône and 18 km north of Valence.
