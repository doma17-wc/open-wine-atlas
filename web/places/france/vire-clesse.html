Viré-Clessé

Appellation

Viré-Clessé is an appellation in France, covering 548.1 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north of Mâcon and 38 km south of Chalon-sur-Saône.
