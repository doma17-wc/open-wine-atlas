Coteaux du Loir

Appellation

Coteaux du Loir is an appellation in France, covering 1,874.9 ha across 18 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Pineau D'Aunis and Chenin. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
