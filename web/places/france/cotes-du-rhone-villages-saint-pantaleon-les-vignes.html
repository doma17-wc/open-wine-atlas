Côtes du Rhône Villages Saint-Pantaléon-les-Vignes

Sub-zone

Côtes du Rhône Villages Saint-Pantaléon-les-Vignes is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Rhône Villages in France, covering 622.6 ha in Saint-Pantaléon-Les-Vignes. That is 1.4% of the Côtes du Rhône Villages appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
