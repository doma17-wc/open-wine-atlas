Cornas

Appellation

Cornas is an appellation in France, covering 541.6 ha in Cornas. The rules allow red wine, based on Syrah. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.

Northern Rhône appellation for red wine from Syrah only, on steep granite slopes in a sheltered amphitheatre.
