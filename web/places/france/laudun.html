Laudun

Appellation

Laudun is an appellation in France, covering 1,148.1 ha in Laudun-L'Ardoise, Saint-Victor-La-Coste and Tresques. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.4 km south of Laudun-l'Ardoise and 14 km west of Orange.
