Alsace Côtes de Barr

Sub-zone

Alsace Côtes de Barr is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 49.4 ha in Barr. That is 0.2% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies around the town of Barr and 29 km south-west of Strasbourg.
