Mâcon

Appellation

Mâcon is an appellation in France, covering 17,699.8 ha across 88 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
