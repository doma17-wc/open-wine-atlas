Quincy

Appellation

Quincy is an appellation in France, covering 814.1 ha in Brinay and Quincy. The rules allow white wine, based on Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.4 km west of Mehun-sur-Yèvre and 21 km north-west of Bourges.
