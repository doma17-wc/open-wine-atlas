Saint-Aubin premier cru Les Perrières

Premier cruVineyard site

Saint-Aubin premier cru Les Perrières is a premier cru climat of Saint-Aubin in France, covering 5.3 ha in Saint-Aubin. That is 2.2% of the Saint-Aubin appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
