Régnié

Appellation

Régnié is an appellation in France, covering 748.1 ha in Lantignie and Regnie-Durette. The rules allow red wine, based on Gamay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 9.4 km north-west of Belleville and 23 km south-west of Mâcon.
