Loupiac

Appellation

Loupiac is an appellation in France, covering 618.9 ha in Loupiac. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
