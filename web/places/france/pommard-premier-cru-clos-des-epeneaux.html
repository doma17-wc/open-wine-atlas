Pommard premier cru Clos des Epeneaux

Premier cruVineyard site

Pommard premier cru Clos des Epeneaux is a premier cru climat of Pommard in France, covering 5.2 ha in Pommard. That is 1.5% of the Pommard appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.8 km west of Beaune and 26 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
