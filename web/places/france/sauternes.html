Sauternes

Appellation

Sauternes is an appellation in France, covering 3,056.7 ha across 5 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Sweet white wine from grapes affected by noble rot, helped by autumn mists from the Ciron river. Sémillon, Sauvignon Blanc and Muscadelle. Château d'Yquem was the only Premier Cru Supérieur in 1855.
