Savigny-lès-Beaune premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Savigny-lès-Beaune premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Savigny-lès-Beaune in France, covering 144.5 ha in Savigny-Les-Beaune. That is 38.0% of the Savigny-lès-Beaune appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Beaune and 31 km south-west of Dijon.
