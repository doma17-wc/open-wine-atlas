Côtes du Roussillon Villages Caramany

Sub-zone

Côtes du Roussillon Villages Caramany is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Roussillon Villages in France, covering 2,179.1 ha in Belesta, Caramany and Cassagnes. That is 5.8% of the Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 8.2 km north of Ille-sur-Têt and 24 km north-west of Perpignan.
