Clairette du Languedoc Aspiran

Sub-zone

Clairette du Languedoc Aspiran is a named sub-zone of Clairette du Languedoc in France, covering 1,328.1 ha in Aspiran. That is 15.7% of the Clairette du Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Clairette. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north-east of Pézenas and 33 km west of Montpellier.
