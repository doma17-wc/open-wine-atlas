Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Chassagne-Montrachet in France, covering 172.3 ha in Chassagne-Montrachet. That is 48.5% of the Chassagne-Montrachet appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.2 km north-west of Chagny and 19 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
