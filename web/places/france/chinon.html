Chinon

Appellation

Chinon is an appellation in France, covering 6,781.7 ha across 26 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Cabernet Franc and Chenin. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Touraine appellation, mainly red from Cabernet Franc, on gravel near the Vienne river and on limestone slopes inland.
