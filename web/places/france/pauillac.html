Pauillac

Appellation

Pauillac is an appellation in France, covering 1,636.3 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies around the town of Pauillac and 42 km north of Bordeaux.

Médoc commune with three of the five first growths of the 1855 classification: Lafite Rothschild, Latour and Mouton Rothschild. Cabernet Sauvignon on deep gravel gives powerful, long-lived wines.
