Vosne-Romanée premier cru En Orveaux

Premier cruVineyard site

Vosne-Romanée premier cru En Orveaux is a premier cru climat of Vosne-Romanée in France, covering 1.9 ha in Flagey-Echezeaux. That is 0.8% of the Vosne-Romanée appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 4 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 16 km south-west of Dijon.
