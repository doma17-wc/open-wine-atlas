Givry premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Givry premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Givry in France, covering 163.5 ha in Dracy-Le-Fort, Givry and Jambles. That is 44.4% of the Givry appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
