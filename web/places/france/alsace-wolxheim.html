Alsace Wolxheim

Sub-zone

Alsace Wolxheim is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 64.1 ha in Dahlenheim and Wolxheim. That is 0.3% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.6 km north-east of Molsheim and 18 km west of Strasbourg.
