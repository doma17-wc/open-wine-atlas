Volnay

Appellation

Volnay is an appellation in France, covering 241.6 ha in Meursault and Volnay. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.1 km south-west of Beaune and 24 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.

Côte de Beaune village known for delicate, perfumed reds. Premiers crus such as Clos des Chênes, Caillerets and Taillepieds are on lighter, limestone-rich soils.
