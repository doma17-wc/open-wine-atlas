Vosne-Romanée premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Vosne-Romanée premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Vosne-Romanée in France, covering 133.2 ha in Flagey-Echezeaux and Vosne-Romanee. That is 56.9% of the Vosne-Romanée appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
