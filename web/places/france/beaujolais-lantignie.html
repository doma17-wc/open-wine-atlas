Beaujolais Lantignié

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Lantignié is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 496.6 ha in Lantignie. That is 1.3% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: basic volcanic rocks. It lies 12 km north-west of Belleville and 25 km south-west of Mâcon.
