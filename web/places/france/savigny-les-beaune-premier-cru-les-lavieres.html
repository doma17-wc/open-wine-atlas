Savigny-lès-Beaune premier cru Les Lavières

Premier cruVineyard site

Savigny-lès-Beaune premier cru Les Lavières is a premier cru climat of Savigny-lès-Beaune in France, covering 17.9 ha in Savigny-Les-Beaune. That is 4.7% of the Savigny-lès-Beaune appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Beaune and 31 km south-west of Dijon.
