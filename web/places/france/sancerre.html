Sancerre

Appellation

Sancerre is an appellation in France, covering 3,624.6 ha across 14 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Upper Loire appellation for Sauvignon Blanc, with some Pinot Noir. Three main soil types: terres blanches (Kimmeridgian marl), caillottes (limestone pebbles) and silex (flint).
