Coteaux du Layon Saint-Aubin-de-Luigné ou Saint-Aubin

Sub-zone

Coteaux du Layon Saint-Aubin-de-Luigné ou Saint-Aubin is a named sub-zone of Coteaux du Layon in France, covering 646.8 ha in Val-Du-Layon. That is 10.4% of the Coteaux du Layon appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
