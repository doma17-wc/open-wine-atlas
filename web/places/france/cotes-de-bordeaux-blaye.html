Côtes de Bordeaux Blaye

Sub-zone

Côtes de Bordeaux Blaye is a named sub-zone of Côtes de Bordeaux in France, covering 17,354.7 ha across 38 communes. That is 48.3% of the Côtes de Bordeaux appellation area. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
