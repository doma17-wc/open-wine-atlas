Montravel

Appellation

Montravel is an appellation in France, covering 4,012.3 ha across 15 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 15 km south-west of Montpon-Ménestérol and 53 km east of Bordeaux.
