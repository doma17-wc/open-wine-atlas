Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Les Poulettes

Premier cruVineyard site

Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Les Poulettes is a premier cru climat of Nuits-Saint-Georges in France, covering 2.1 ha in Nuits-Saint-Georges. That is 0.7% of the Nuits-Saint-Georges appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 22 km south-west of Dijon.
