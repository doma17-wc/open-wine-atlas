Gaillac premières côtes

Appellation

Gaillac premières côtes is an appellation in France, covering 3,619.7 ha across 10 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Len De L'El, Mauzac, Mauzac Rose and Muscadelle. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Gaillac and 20 km west of Albi.
