Ladoix

Appellation

Ladoix is an appellation in France, covering 134.1 ha in Ladoix-Serrigny. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.9 km north-east of Beaune and 28 km south-west of Dijon.
