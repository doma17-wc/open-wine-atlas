Clairette du Languedoc Adissan

Sub-zone

Clairette du Languedoc Adissan is a named sub-zone of Clairette du Languedoc in France, covering 362.9 ha in Adissan. That is 4.3% of the Clairette du Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Clairette. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
