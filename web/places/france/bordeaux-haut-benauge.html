Bordeaux Haut-Benauge

Sub-zone

Bordeaux Haut-Benauge is a named sub-zone of Bordeaux in France, covering 6,716.9 ha across 8 communes. That is 2.9% of the Bordeaux appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
