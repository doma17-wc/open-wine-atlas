Roussette de Savoie Monterminod

Sub-zone

Roussette de Savoie Monterminod is a named sub-zone of Roussette de Savoie in France, covering 15.9 ha in Saint-Alban-Leysse. That is 0.3% of the Roussette de Savoie appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Altesse. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.9 km east of Chambéry and 49 km north-east of Grenoble.
