Bourgogne Côtes du Couchois

Sub-zone

Bourgogne Côtes du Couchois is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 940.1 ha across 6 communes. That is 2.3% of the Bourgogne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.5 km west of Chagny and 21 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
