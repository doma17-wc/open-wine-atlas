Fixin

Appellation

Fixin is an appellation in France, covering 153.8 ha in Brochon and Fixin. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
