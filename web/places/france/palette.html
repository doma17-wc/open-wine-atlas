Palette

Appellation

Palette is an appellation in France, covering 1,858.2 ha in Aix-En-Provence, Le Tholonet and Meyreuil. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.8 km south of Aix-en-Provence and 24 km north-east of Marseille.
