Les Baux de Provence

Appellation

Les Baux de Provence is an appellation in France, covering 3,290.2 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.8 km north-east of Saint-Martin-de-Crau and 19 km east of Arles.
