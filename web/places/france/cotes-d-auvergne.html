Côtes d'Auvergne

Appellation

Côtes d'Auvergne is an appellation in France, covering 2,997.5 ha across 59 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
