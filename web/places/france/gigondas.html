Gigondas

Appellation

Gigondas is an appellation in France, covering 1,736 ha in Gigondas. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Grenache, Mourvèdre and Syrah. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Southern Rhône red at the foot of the Dentelles de Montmirail, mostly Grenache with Syrah and Mourvèdre, from higher, cooler vineyards than the valley floor.
