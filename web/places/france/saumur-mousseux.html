Saumur (mousseux)

Sub-zone

Saumur (mousseux) is a named sub-zone of Saumur in France, covering 346 ha in Brissac Loire Aubance and Saint-Macaire-Du-Bois. That is 2.1% of the Saumur appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
