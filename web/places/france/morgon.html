Morgon

Appellation

Morgon is an appellation in France, covering 1,341.8 ha in Villie-Morgon. The rules allow red wine, based on Gamay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 7.8 km north-west of Belleville and 21 km south-west of Mâcon.
