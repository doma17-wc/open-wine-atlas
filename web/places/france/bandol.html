Bandol

Appellation

Bandol is an appellation in France, covering 9,630.1 ha across 8 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.4 km east of Saint-Cyr-sur-Mer and 12 km north-west of La Seyne-sur-Mer.

Provence appellation on terraces above the Mediterranean. Reds and rosés built on Mourvèdre, which needs the warmth and sea exposure of this coast.
