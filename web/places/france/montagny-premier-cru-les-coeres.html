Montagny premier cru Les Coères

Premier cruVineyard site

Montagny premier cru Les Coères is a premier cru climat of Montagny in France, covering 29.7 ha in Jully-Les-Buxy, Montagny-Les-Buxy and Saint-Vallerin. That is 6.9% of the Montagny appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 16 km south-west of Chalon-sur-Saône and 44 km north of Mâcon.
