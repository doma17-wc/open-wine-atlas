Mâcon Montbellet

Sub-zone

Mâcon Montbellet is a named sub-zone of Mâcon in France, covering 103.9 ha in Montbellet. That is 0.6% of the Mâcon appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 11 km south-west of Tournus and 18 km north of Mâcon.
