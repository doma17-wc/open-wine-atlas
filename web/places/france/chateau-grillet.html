Château-Grillet

Appellation

Château-Grillet is an appellation in France, covering 3.8 ha in Saint-Michel-Sur-Rhone and Vérin. The rules allow white wine, based on Viognier. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 9.9 km north of Roussillon and 13 km south-west of Vienne.
