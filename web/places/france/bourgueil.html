Bourgueil

Appellation

Bourgueil is an appellation in France, covering 3,436 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Cabernet Franc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 17 km east of Saumur and 44 km west of Tours.
