Montlouis-sur-Loire

Appellation

Montlouis-sur-Loire is an appellation in France, covering 1,502.9 ha in Lussault-Sur-Loire, Montlouis-Sur-Loire and Saint-Martin-Le-Beau. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
