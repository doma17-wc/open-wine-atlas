Côtes d'Auvergne Corent

Sub-zone

Côtes d'Auvergne Corent is a named sub-zone of Côtes d'Auvergne in France, covering 201.1 ha across 4 communes. That is 6.7% of the Côtes d'Auvergne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
