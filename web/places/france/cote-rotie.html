Côte Rôtie

Appellation

Côte Rôtie is an appellation in France, covering 505.2 ha in Ampuis, Saint-Cyr-Sur-Le-Rhone and Tupin-Et-Semons. The rules allow red wine, based on Syrah. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 6.4 km west of Vienne and 28 km south of Lyon.

Steep terraces on the right bank of the Rhône, the northernmost cru of the Rhône valley. Syrah, which may be co-fermented with a small share of Viognier. The Côte Brune and Côte Blonde are its most famous slopes.
