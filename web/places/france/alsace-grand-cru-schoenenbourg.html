Alsace grand cru Schoenenbourg

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Schoenenbourg is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 52.9 ha in Riquewihr and Zellenberg. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 10 km north of Colmar and 57 km south-west of Strasbourg.
