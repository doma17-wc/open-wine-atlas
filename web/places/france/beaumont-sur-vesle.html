Beaumont-sur-Vesle

Grand cruSub-zone

Beaumont-sur-Vesle is a grand cru sub-zone of Montagne de Reims in France in Beaumont-sur-Vesle. It lies 11 km south-east of Cormontreuil and 15 km south-east of Reims.
