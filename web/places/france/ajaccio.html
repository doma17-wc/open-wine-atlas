Ajaccio

Appellation

Ajaccio is an appellation in France, covering 23,217.8 ha across 36 communes. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Barbaroux, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
