Maranges premier cru Le Clos des Loyères

Premier cruVineyard site

Maranges premier cru Le Clos des Loyères is a premier cru climat of Maranges in France, covering 11.4 ha in Sampigny-Les-Maranges. That is 4.9% of the Maranges appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.9 km west of Chagny and 20 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
