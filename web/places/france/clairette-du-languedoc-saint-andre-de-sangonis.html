Clairette du Languedoc Saint-André-de-Sangonis

Sub-zone

Clairette du Languedoc Saint-André-de-Sangonis is a named sub-zone of Clairette du Languedoc in France, covering 145.5 ha in Saint-Andre-De-Sangonis. That is 1.7% of the Clairette du Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Clairette. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.5 km west of Gignac and 31 km west of Montpellier.
