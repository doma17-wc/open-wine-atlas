Pommard premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Pommard premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Pommard in France, covering 124.9 ha in Pommard. That is 37.2% of the Pommard appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.9 km west of Beaune and 26 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
