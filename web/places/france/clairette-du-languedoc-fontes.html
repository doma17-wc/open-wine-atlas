Clairette du Languedoc Fontès

Sub-zone

Clairette du Languedoc Fontès is a named sub-zone of Clairette du Languedoc in France, covering 803.5 ha in Fontes. That is 9.5% of the Clairette du Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Clairette. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.8 km north of Pézenas and 27 km north-east of Béziers.
