Clairette de Die

Appellation

Clairette de Die is an appellation in France, covering 6,626.8 ha across 30 communes. The rules allow rosé and white wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs, Muscat À Petits Grains Rouges and Clairette. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
