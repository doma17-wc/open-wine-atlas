Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru Les Vergers

Premier cruVineyard site

Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru Les Vergers is a premier cru climat of Chassagne-Montrachet in France, covering 9.5 ha in Chassagne-Montrachet. That is 2.7% of the Chassagne-Montrachet appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
