Pomerol

Appellation

Pomerol is an appellation in France, covering 963.9 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 3.2 km east of Libourne and 31 km east of Bordeaux.

Small right-bank plateau of clay and gravel, mostly planted with Merlot. Pomerol has no official classification, yet it is home to Pétrus and Le Pin.
