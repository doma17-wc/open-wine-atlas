Tavel

Appellation

Tavel is an appellation in France, covering 1,158.1 ha in Roquemaure and Tavel. The rules allow rosé wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.8 km north-east of Rochefort-du-Gard and 11 km north-west of Avignon.
