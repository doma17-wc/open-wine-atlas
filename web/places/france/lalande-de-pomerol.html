Lalande-de-Pomerol

Appellation

Lalande-de-Pomerol is an appellation in France, covering 1,386.6 ha in Lalande-De-Pomerol and Neac. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Cot and Merlot. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
