Saumur-Champigny

Appellation

Saumur-Champigny is an appellation in France, covering 2,078.3 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.9 km south-east of Saumur and 50 km south-east of Angers.
