Bourgogne Côte Chalonnaise

Sub-zone

Bourgogne Côte Chalonnaise is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 5,836.6 ha across 44 communes. That is 14.4% of the Bourgogne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
