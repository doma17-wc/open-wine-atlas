Mercurey premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Mercurey premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Mercurey in France, covering 178 ha in Mercurey and Saint-Martin-Sous-Montaigu. That is 22.5% of the Mercurey appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.6 km north-west of Châtenoy-le-Royal and 11 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
