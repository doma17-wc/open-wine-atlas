Volnay premier cru Clos de l'Audignac

Premier cruVineyard site

Volnay premier cru Clos de l'Audignac is a premier cru climat of Volnay in France, covering 1.1 ha in Volnay. That is 0.5% of the Volnay appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.2 km west of Beaune and 25 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
