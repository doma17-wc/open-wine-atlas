Alsace grand cru Steinert

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Steinert is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 38.8 ha in Pfaffenheim and Westhalten. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.3 km north-east of Guebwiller and 12 km south-west of Colmar.
