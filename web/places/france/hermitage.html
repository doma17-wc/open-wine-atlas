Hermitage

Appellation

Hermitage is an appellation in France, covering 145.5 ha in Crozes-Hermitage, Larnage and Tain-L'Hermitage. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Syrah, Marsanne and Roussanne. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Granite hill above Tain-l'Hermitage facing south over the Rhône. Syrah for the reds, Marsanne and Roussanne for the whites. One of the oldest famous wine names in France.
