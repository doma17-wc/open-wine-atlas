Côtes d'Auvergne Chateaugay

Sub-zone

Côtes d'Auvergne Chateaugay is a named sub-zone of Côtes d'Auvergne in France, covering 182.8 ha in Cebazat, Chateaugay and Menetrol. That is 6.1% of the Côtes d'Auvergne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: basic volcanic rocks.
