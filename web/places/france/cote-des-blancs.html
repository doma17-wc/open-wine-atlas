Côte des Blancs

Sub-zone

Côte des Blancs is a named sub-zone of Champagne in France across 9 communes. The rules allow rosé and white wine.

An east-facing chalk slope running south from Épernay to Vertus. It is almost all Chardonnay. Six of the 17 grand cru villages are here.
