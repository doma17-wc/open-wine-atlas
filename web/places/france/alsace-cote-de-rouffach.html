Alsace Côte de Rouffach

Sub-zone

Alsace Côte de Rouffach is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 281.7 ha in Pfaffenheim, Rouffach and Westhalten. That is 1.4% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
