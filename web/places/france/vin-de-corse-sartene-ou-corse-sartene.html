Vin de Corse Sartène ou Corse Sartène

Sub-zone

Vin de Corse Sartène ou Corse Sartène is a named sub-zone of Vin de Corse in France, covering 8,780.7 ha across 14 communes. That is 12.4% of the Vin de Corse appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
