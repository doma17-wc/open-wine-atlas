Chambolle-Musigny

Appellation

Chambolle-Musigny is an appellation in France, covering 182.5 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Village famed for the most perfumed and silky reds of the Côte de Nuits. Limestone-rich soils with little clay. Les Amoureuses is its most celebrated premier cru.
