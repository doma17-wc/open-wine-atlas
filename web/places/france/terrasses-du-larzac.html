Terrasses du Larzac

Appellation

Terrasses du Larzac is an appellation in France, covering 6,213.3 ha across 32 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Mourvèdre and Syrah. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
