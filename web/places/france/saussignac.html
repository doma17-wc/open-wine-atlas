Saussignac

Appellation

Saussignac is an appellation in France, covering 3,150 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km west of Bergerac and 54 km south-west of Périgueux.
