Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Gorges

Sub-zone

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Gorges is a named sub-zone of Muscadet Sèvre et Maine in France, covering 224.6 ha across 4 communes. That is 2.0% of the Muscadet Sèvre et Maine appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Melon. Regional bedrock: basic plutonic rocks. It lies 3.7 km north-west of Clisson and 21 km south-east of Nantes.
