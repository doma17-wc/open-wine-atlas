Aloxe-Corton premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Aloxe-Corton premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Aloxe-Corton in France, covering 198.4 ha in Aloxe-Corton, Ladoix-Serrigny and Pernand-Vergelesses. That is 68.9% of the Aloxe-Corton appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.8 km north-east of Beaune and 29 km south-west of Dijon.
