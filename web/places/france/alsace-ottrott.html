Alsace Ottrott

Sub-zone

Alsace Ottrott is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 43.6 ha in Obernai and Ottrott. That is 0.2% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3 km west of Obernai and 26 km west of Strasbourg.
