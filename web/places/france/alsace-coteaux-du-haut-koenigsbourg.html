Alsace Coteaux du Haut Koenigsbourg

Sub-zone

Alsace Coteaux du Haut Koenigsbourg is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 126.1 ha across 4 communes. That is 0.6% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 5.2 km west of Sélestat and 18 km north of Colmar.
