Meursault

Appellation

Meursault is an appellation in France, covering 443.4 ha in Meursault. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.3 km south-west of Beaune and 22 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.

The largest white-wine village of the Côte de Beaune. No grand cru, but top premiers crus such as Perrières, Charmes and Genevrières give rich, nutty Chardonnay.
