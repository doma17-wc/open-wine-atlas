Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Derrière la Grange

Premier cruVineyard site

Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Derrière la Grange is a premier cru climat of Chambolle-Musigny in France, covering 0.5 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. That is 0.3% of the Chambolle-Musigny appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
