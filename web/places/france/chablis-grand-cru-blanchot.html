Chablis Grand Cru Blanchot

Grand cruVineyard site

Chablis Grand Cru Blanchot is a grand cru climat of Chablis Grand Cru in France, covering 13.1 ha in Chablis. That is 12.3% of the Chablis Grand Cru appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km west of Tonnerre and 18 km east of Auxerre.
