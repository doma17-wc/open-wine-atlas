Coteaux du Layon Faye-d'Anjou ou Faye

Sub-zone

Coteaux du Layon Faye-d'Anjou ou Faye is a named sub-zone of Coteaux du Layon in France, covering 616.8 ha in Bellevigne-En-Layon. That is 9.9% of the Coteaux du Layon appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
