Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru La Grande Borne

Premier cruVineyard site

Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru La Grande Borne is a premier cru climat of Chassagne-Montrachet in France, covering 1.7 ha in Chassagne-Montrachet. That is 0.5% of the Chassagne-Montrachet appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.9 km north-west of Chagny and 19 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
