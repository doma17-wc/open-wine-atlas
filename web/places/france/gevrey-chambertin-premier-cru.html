Gevrey-Chambertin premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Gevrey-Chambertin premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Gevrey-Chambertin in France, covering 172.6 ha in Gevrey-Chambertin. That is 31.9% of the Gevrey-Chambertin appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 8.3 km south-west of Chenôve and 11 km south-west of Dijon.
