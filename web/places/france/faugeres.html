Faugères

Appellation

Faugères is an appellation in France, covering 5,986.9 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.9 km south of Bédarieux and 22 km north of Béziers.
