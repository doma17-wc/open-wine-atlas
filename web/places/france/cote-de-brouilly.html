Côte de Brouilly

Appellation

Côte de Brouilly is an appellation in France, covering 405.8 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Gamay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 7.2 km west of Belleville and 14 km north of Villefranche-sur-Saône.
