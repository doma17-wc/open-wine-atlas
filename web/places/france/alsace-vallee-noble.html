Alsace Vallée Noble

Sub-zone

Alsace Vallée Noble is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 161.1 ha in Soultzmatt and Westhalten. That is 0.8% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
