Beaujolais Jullié

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Jullié is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 296.5 ha in Jullie. That is 0.7% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: acid volcanic rocks. It lies 14 km south-west of Mâcon and 28 km north of Villefranche-sur-Saône.
