Beaujolais Le Perréon

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Le Perréon is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 801.5 ha in Le Perreon. That is 2.0% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 13 km north-west of Villefranche-sur-Saône and 40 km north of Lyon.
