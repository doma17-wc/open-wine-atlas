Pessac-Léognan

Appellation

Pessac-Léognan is an appellation in France, covering 4,487.3 ha across 10 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.2 km south of Villenave-d'Ornon and 10 km south-east of Pessac.

Appellation created in 1987 from the northern part of the Graves, south of Bordeaux city. Home of Château Haut-Brion, the only first growth outside the Médoc in 1855, and of fine dry whites.
