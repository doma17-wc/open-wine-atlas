Valençay

Appellation

Valençay is an appellation in France, covering 2,607.5 ha across 12 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Cot, Gamay, Pinot Noir and Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
