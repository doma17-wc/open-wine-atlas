Saint-Romain

Appellation

Saint-Romain is an appellation in France, covering 147 ha in Saint-Romain. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.6 km west of Beaune and 26 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
