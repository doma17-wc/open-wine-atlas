Saint-Pourçain

Appellation

Saint-Pourçain is an appellation in France, covering 2,832.7 ha across 19 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
