Canon Fronsac

Appellation

Canon Fronsac is an appellation in France, covering 392.4 ha in Fronsac and Saint-Michel-De-Fronsac. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.3 km north-west of Libourne and 25 km east of Bordeaux.
