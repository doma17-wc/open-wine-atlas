Chablis

Appellation

Chablis is an appellation in France, covering 5,282.3 ha across 17 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km east of Auxerre and 60 km south-west of Troyes.

Chardonnay grown around the town of Chablis in the Yonne, the northern outpost of Burgundy. The best sites lie on Kimmeridgian marl with fossil oyster shells, which many link to the wine's saline, chalky edge.
