Moselle

Appellation

Moselle is an appellation in France, covering 678.7 ha across 18 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Auxerrois, Mueller Thurgau and Pinot Gris. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
