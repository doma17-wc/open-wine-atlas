Givry premier cru Le Vigron

Premier cruVineyard site

Givry premier cru Le Vigron is a premier cru climat of Givry in France, covering 3.3 ha in Givry. That is 0.9% of the Givry appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 10 km west of Chalon-sur-Saône and 64 km south-west of Dijon.
