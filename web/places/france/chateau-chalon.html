Château-Chalon

Appellation

Château-Chalon is an appellation in France, covering 94.3 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Savagnin Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Jura appellation for vin jaune only: Savagnin aged at least six years under a veil of yeast, without topping up, and sold in the 62 cl clavelin bottle.
