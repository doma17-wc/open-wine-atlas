Alsace grand cru Froehn

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Froehn is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 14.3 ha in Zellenberg. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.9 km north of Colmar and 56 km south-west of Strasbourg.
