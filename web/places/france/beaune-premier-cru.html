Beaune premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Beaune premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Beaune in France, covering 335.5 ha in Beaune. That is 70.8% of the Beaune appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies around the town of Beaune and 35 km south-west of Dijon.
