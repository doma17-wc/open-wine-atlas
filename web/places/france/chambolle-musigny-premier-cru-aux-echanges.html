Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Aux Echanges

Premier cruVineyard site

Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Aux Echanges is a premier cru climat of Chambolle-Musigny in France, covering 0.9 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. That is 0.5% of the Chambolle-Musigny appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.6 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 15 km south-west of Dijon.
