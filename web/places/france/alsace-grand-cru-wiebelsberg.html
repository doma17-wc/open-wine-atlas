Alsace grand cru Wiebelsberg

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Wiebelsberg is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 12.2 ha in Andlau. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 2.6 km south-west of Barr and 32 km south-west of Strasbourg.
