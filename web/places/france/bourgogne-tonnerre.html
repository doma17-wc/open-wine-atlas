Bourgogne Tonnerre

Sub-zone

Bourgogne Tonnerre is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 838.1 ha across 6 communes. That is 2.1% of the Bourgogne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Tonnerre and 46 km south of Troyes.
