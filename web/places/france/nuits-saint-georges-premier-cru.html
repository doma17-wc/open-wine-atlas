Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Nuits-Saint-Georges in France, covering 147.2 ha in Nuits-Saint-Georges and Premeaux-Prissey. That is 45.6% of the Nuits-Saint-Georges appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 24 km south-west of Dijon.
