Cadillac

Appellation

Cadillac is an appellation in France, covering 7,661.1 ha across 22 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
