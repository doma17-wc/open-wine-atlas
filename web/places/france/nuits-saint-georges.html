Nuits-Saint-Georges

Appellation

Nuits-Saint-Georges is an appellation in France, covering 323 ha in Nuits-Saint-Georges and Premeaux-Prissey. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

The town that gives the Côte de Nuits its name. No grand cru, but a long list of premiers crus, Les Saint-Georges being the most famous. Reds tend to be firm and earthy.
