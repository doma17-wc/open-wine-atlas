Bouzeron

Appellation

Bouzeron is an appellation in France, covering 155.9 ha in Bouzeron and Chassey-Le-Camp. The rules allow white wine, based on Aligoté. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3 km south-west of Chagny and 15 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
