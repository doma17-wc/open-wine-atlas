Monthélie

Appellation

Monthélie is an appellation in France, covering 140.6 ha in Monthelie. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.4 km west of Beaune and 25 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
