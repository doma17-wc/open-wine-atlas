Pouilly-Loché premier cru les Mûres

Premier cruVineyard site

Pouilly-Loché premier cru les Mûres is a premier cru climat of Pouilly-Loché in France, covering 7.1 ha in Macon. That is 20.2% of the Pouilly-Loché appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
