Premières Côtes de Bordeaux

Appellation

Premières Côtes de Bordeaux is an appellation in France, covering 12,002.8 ha across 38 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
