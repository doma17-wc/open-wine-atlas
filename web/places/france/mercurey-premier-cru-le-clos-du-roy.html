Mercurey premier cru Le Clos du Roy

Premier cruVineyard site

Mercurey premier cru Le Clos du Roy is a premier cru climat of Mercurey in France, covering 10.8 ha in Mercurey. That is 1.4% of the Mercurey appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
