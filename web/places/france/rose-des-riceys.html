Rosé des Riceys

Appellation

Rosé des Riceys is an appellation in France in Les Riceys. The rules allow rosé wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 21 km north-west of Châtillon-sur-Seine and 40 km south of Troyes.
