Cour-Cheverny

Appellation

Cour-Cheverny is an appellation in France, covering 4,131.1 ha across 11 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Romorantin. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.4 km east of Vineuil and 11 km east of Blois.
