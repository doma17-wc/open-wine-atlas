Muscadet Sèvre et Maine

Appellation

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine is an appellation in France, covering 11,483.4 ha across 30 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Melon. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

Melon de Bourgogne near Nantes, at the western end of the Loire. Often aged on its lees, 'sur lie'. Named crus communaux such as Clisson and Gorges are among its sub-zones.
