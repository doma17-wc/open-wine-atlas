Roussette de Savoie Frangy

Sub-zone

Roussette de Savoie Frangy is a named sub-zone of Roussette de Savoie in France, covering 119.7 ha in Chaumont, Desingy and Frangy. That is 1.9% of the Roussette de Savoie appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Altesse. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 12 km south of Bellegarde-sur-Valserine and 22 km north-west of Annecy.
