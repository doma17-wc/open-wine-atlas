Muscat de Lunel

Appellation

Muscat de Lunel is an appellation in France, covering 555.1 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Lunel and 21 km east of Montpellier.
