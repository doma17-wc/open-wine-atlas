Puligny-Montrachet premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Puligny-Montrachet premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Puligny-Montrachet in France, covering 122.1 ha in Puligny-Montrachet. That is 51.6% of the Puligny-Montrachet appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
