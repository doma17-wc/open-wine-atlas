Mercurey premier cru La Mission

Premier cruVineyard site

Mercurey premier cru La Mission is a premier cru climat of Mercurey in France, covering 1.9 ha in Mercurey. That is 0.2% of the Mercurey appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.6 km north-west of Châtenoy-le-Royal and 11 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
