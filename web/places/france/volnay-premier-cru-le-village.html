Volnay premier cru Le Village

Premier cruVineyard site

Volnay premier cru Le Village is a premier cru climat of Volnay in France, covering 3 ha in Volnay. That is 1.2% of the Volnay appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 4.9 km west of Beaune and 25 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
