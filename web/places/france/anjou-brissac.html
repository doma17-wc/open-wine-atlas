Anjou Brissac

Appellation

Anjou Brissac is an appellation in France, covering 2,508.1 ha across 7 communes. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.9 km south of Bouchemaine and 12 km south-west of Angers.
