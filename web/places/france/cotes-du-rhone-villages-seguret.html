Côtes du Rhône Villages Séguret

Sub-zone

Côtes du Rhône Villages Séguret is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Rhône Villages in France, covering 1,898.1 ha in Seguret. That is 4.4% of the Côtes du Rhône Villages appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.5 km south-west of Vaison-la-Romaine and 21 km east of Orange.
