Languedoc (Rouge et Rosé)

Sub-zone

Languedoc (Rouge et Rosé) is a named sub-zone of Languedoc in France, covering 689.1 ha in Montagnac. That is 0.3% of the Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
