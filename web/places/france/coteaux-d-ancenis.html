Coteaux d'Ancenis

Appellation

Coteaux d'Ancenis is an appellation in France, covering 1,635.8 ha across 14 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Pinot Gris. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
