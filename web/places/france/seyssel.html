Seyssel

Appellation

Seyssel is an appellation in France, covering 1,837.7 ha in Corbonod and Seyssel. The rules allow white wine, based on Altesse, Chasselas and Molette. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north-west of Rumilly and 23 km north-west of Annecy.
