Touraine Mesland

Sub-zone

Touraine Mesland is a named sub-zone of Touraine in France, covering 1,440.5 ha across 4 communes. That is 3.7% of the Touraine appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
