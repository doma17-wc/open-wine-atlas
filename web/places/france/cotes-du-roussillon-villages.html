Côtes du Roussillon Villages

Appellation

Côtes du Roussillon Villages is an appellation in France, covering 37,791.6 ha across 32 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
