Chambolle-Musigny premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Chambolle-Musigny premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Chambolle-Musigny in France, covering 85.5 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. That is 46.8% of the Chambolle-Musigny appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.8 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 15 km south-west of Dijon.
