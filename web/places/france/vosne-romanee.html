Vosne-Romanée

Appellation

Vosne-Romanée is an appellation in France, covering 233.9 ha in Flagey-Echezeaux and Vosne-Romanee. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Village with the highest concentration of grands crus in Burgundy, including Romanée-Conti and La Tâche. Its premiers crus, such as Cros Parantoux, are among the most sought-after in the region.
