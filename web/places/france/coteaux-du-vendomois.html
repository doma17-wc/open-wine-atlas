Coteaux du Vendômois

Appellation

Coteaux du Vendômois is an appellation in France, covering 2,760.9 ha across 26 communes. The rules allow rosé, red and white wine, based on Pineau D'Aunis and Chenin. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
