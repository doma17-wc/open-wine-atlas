Saint-Estèphe

Appellation

Saint-Estèphe is an appellation in France, covering 1,554.9 ha in Saint-Estephe. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

The northernmost of the great Médoc communes. More clay in the soil than further south gives firmer wines, with Cos d'Estournel and Montrose as leading estates.
