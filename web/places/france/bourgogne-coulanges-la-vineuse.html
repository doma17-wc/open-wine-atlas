Bourgogne Coulanges-la-Vineuse

Sub-zone

Bourgogne Coulanges-la-Vineuse is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 707.7 ha across 7 communes. That is 1.8% of the Bourgogne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
