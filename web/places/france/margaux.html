Margaux

Appellation

Margaux is an appellation in France, covering 1,873.8 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 9.9 km north of Parempuyre and 22 km north of Bordeaux.

Médoc appellation covering five communes, with more classified growths from 1855 than any other. Château Margaux is the first growth. The wines are known for fragrance and finesse.
