Côtes de Bordeaux Castillon

Sub-zone

Côtes de Bordeaux Castillon is a named sub-zone of Côtes de Bordeaux in France, covering 5,572 ha across 9 communes. That is 15.5% of the Côtes de Bordeaux appellation area. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 15 km south-east of Libourne and 39 km east of Bordeaux.
