Touraine Oisly

Sub-zone

Touraine Oisly is a named sub-zone of Touraine in France, covering 1,283.4 ha across 9 communes. That is 3.3% of the Touraine appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 22 km south of Blois and 47 km east of Tours.
