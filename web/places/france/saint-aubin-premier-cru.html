Saint-Aubin premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Saint-Aubin premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Saint-Aubin in France, covering 157.3 ha in Saint-Aubin. That is 66.0% of the Saint-Aubin appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.6 km north of Chagny and 21 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
