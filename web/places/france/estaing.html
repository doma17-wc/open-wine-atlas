Estaing

Appellation

Estaing is an appellation in France, covering 992.4 ha in Coubisou, Estaing and Sebrazac. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Fer, Gamay and Chenin. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 24 km north-east of Rodez and 47 km south of Aurillac.
