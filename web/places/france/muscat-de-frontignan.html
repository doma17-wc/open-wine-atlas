Muscat de Frontignan

Appellation

Muscat de Frontignan is an appellation in France, covering 2,009.5 ha in Frontignan and Vic-La-Gardiole. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Frontignan and 7 km north-east of Sète.
