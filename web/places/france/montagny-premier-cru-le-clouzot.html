Montagny premier cru Le Clouzot

Premier cruVineyard site

Montagny premier cru Le Clouzot is a premier cru climat of Montagny in France, covering 2.4 ha in Buxy. That is 0.6% of the Montagny appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 15 km west of Chalon-sur-Saône and 46 km north of Mâcon.
