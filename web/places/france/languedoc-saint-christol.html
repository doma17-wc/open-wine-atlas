Languedoc Saint-Christol

Sub-zone

Languedoc Saint-Christol is a named sub-zone of Languedoc in France, covering 586.5 ha in Entre-Vignes. That is 0.2% of the Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.3 km north of Lunel and 22 km north-east of Montpellier.
