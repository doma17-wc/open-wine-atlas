Alsace Scherwiller

Sub-zone

Alsace Scherwiller is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 112.4 ha in Scherwiller. That is 0.6% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.9 km north of Sélestat and 24 km north-east of Colmar.
