Vouvray

Appellation

Vouvray is an appellation in France, covering 3,280.5 ha across 8 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.1 km north-east of Tours and 12 km north-east of Joué-lès-Tours.

Chenin Blanc east of Tours on tuffeau limestone, made from dry to sweet and as sparkling wine. Many producers age wine in caves dug into the tuffeau.
