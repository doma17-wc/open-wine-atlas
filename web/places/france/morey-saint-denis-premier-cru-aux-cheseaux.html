Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Aux Cheseaux

Premier cruVineyard site

Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Aux Cheseaux is a premier cru climat of Morey-Saint-Denis in France, covering 1.5 ha in Morey-Saint-Denis. That is 1.0% of the Morey-Saint-Denis appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Aligoté. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 10 km south-west of Chenôve and 13 km south-west of Dijon.
