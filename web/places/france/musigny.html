Musigny

Grand cruAppellation

Musigny is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 10.9 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.4 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 16 km south-west of Dijon.

Grand cru of Chambolle-Musigny and a benchmark for silky, perfumed Pinot Noir. One of very few grands crus of the Côte de Nuits that also produces white wine, in tiny quantities from Chardonnay.
