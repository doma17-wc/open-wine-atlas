Côte de Sézanne

Sub-zone

Côte de Sézanne is a named sub-zone of Champagne in France across 12 communes. The rules allow rosé and white wine.

Slopes around Sézanne in the south of the Marne. Chardonnay leads. The houses' union counts it in a wider Côte des Blancs.
