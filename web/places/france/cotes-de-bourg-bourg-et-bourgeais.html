Côtes de Bourg, Bourg et Bourgeais

Appellation

Côtes de Bourg, Bourg et Bourgeais is an appellation in France, covering 6,988.9 ha across 15 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 13 km north-east of Parempuyre and 25 km north of Bordeaux.
