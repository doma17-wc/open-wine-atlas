Beaune premier cru Le Bas des Teurons

Premier cruVineyard site

Beaune premier cru Le Bas des Teurons is a premier cru climat of Beaune in France, covering 6.7 ha in Beaune. That is 1.4% of the Beaune appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Beaune and 35 km south-west of Dijon.
