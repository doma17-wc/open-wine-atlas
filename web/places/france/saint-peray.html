Saint-Péray

Appellation

Saint-Péray is an appellation in France, covering 1,831.9 ha in Saint-Peray and Toulaud. The rules allow white wine, based on Marsanne and Roussanne. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 4.4 km west of Guilherand-Granges and 7 km west of Valence.
