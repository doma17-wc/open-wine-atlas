Alsace Rodern

Sub-zone

Alsace Rodern is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 33.5 ha in Rodern and Saint-Hippolyte. That is 0.2% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 7.8 km west of Sélestat and 16 km north of Colmar.
