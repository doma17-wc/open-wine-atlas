Beaujolais Quincié-en-Beaujolais

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Quincié-en-Beaujolais is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 1,096.8 ha in Quincie-En-Beaujolais. That is 2.8% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: basic volcanic rocks. It lies 9.1 km west of Belleville and 15 km north of Villefranche-sur-Saône.
