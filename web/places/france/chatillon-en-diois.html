Châtillon-en-Diois

Appellation

Châtillon-en-Diois is an appellation in France, covering 1,341.3 ha across 11 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Aligoté and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 29 km south-east of Crest and 47 km south-east of Valence.
