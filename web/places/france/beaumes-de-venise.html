Beaumes de Venise

Appellation

Beaumes de Venise is an appellation in France, covering 1,318.9 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Grenache. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.8 km north of Carpentras and 19 km east of Orange.
