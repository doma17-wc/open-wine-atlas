Côtes de Provence Sainte-Victoire

Sub-zone

Côtes de Provence Sainte-Victoire is a named sub-zone of Côtes de Provence in France, covering 5,109.5 ha across 9 communes. That is 8.0% of the Côtes de Provence appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
