Bourgogne Côte d'Or

Sub-zone

Bourgogne Côte d'Or is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 8,490.1 ha across 40 communes. That is 21.0% of the Bourgogne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
