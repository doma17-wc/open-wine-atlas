Montagny premier cru

Premier cruSub-zone

Montagny premier cru is a premier cru sub-zone of Montagny in France, covering 243 ha across 4 communes. That is 56.7% of the Montagny appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
