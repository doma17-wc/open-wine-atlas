Tursan

Appellation

Tursan is an appellation in France, covering 5,273.7 ha across 36 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Tannat, Baroque and Gros Manseng. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
