Alsace grand cru Steinklotz

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Steinklotz is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 40.9 ha in Marlenheim. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.3 km south-east of Wasselonne and 20 km north-west of Strasbourg.
