Givry

Appellation

Givry is an appellation in France, covering 367.9 ha in Dracy-Le-Fort, Givry and Jambles. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
