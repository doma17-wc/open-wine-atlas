Côtes du Roussillon Villages Lesquerde

Sub-zone

Côtes du Roussillon Villages Lesquerde is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Roussillon Villages in France, covering 921 ha in Lansac, Lesquerde and Rasigueres. That is 2.4% of the Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
