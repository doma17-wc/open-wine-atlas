Côtes du Roussillon Villages Les Aspres

Sub-zone

Côtes du Roussillon Villages Les Aspres is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Roussillon Villages in France, covering 3,160.7 ha across 19 communes. That is 8.4% of the Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
