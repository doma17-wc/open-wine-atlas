Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru Blanchot dessus

Premier cruVineyard site

Chassagne-Montrachet premier cru Blanchot dessus is a premier cru climat of Chassagne-Montrachet in France, covering 1.2 ha in Chassagne-Montrachet. That is 0.3% of the Chassagne-Montrachet appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.8 km north of Chagny and 20 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
