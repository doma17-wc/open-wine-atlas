Romanée-Conti

Grand cruAppellation

Romanée-Conti is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 1.8 ha in Vosne-Romanee. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.7 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 18 km south-west of Dijon.

Grand cru monopole of about 1.8 ha owned by the Domaine de la Romanée-Conti. Named after the Prince de Conti, who bought it in 1760. Its wine is among the most sought-after in the world.
