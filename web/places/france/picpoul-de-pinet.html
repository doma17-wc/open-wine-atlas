Picpoul de Pinet

Appellation

Picpoul de Pinet is an appellation in France, covering 2,387.3 ha across 6 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Piquepoul Blanc. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.6 km south-east of Pézenas and 14 km west of Sète.
