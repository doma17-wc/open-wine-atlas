Bourgogne La Chapelle Notre-Dame

Sub-zone

Bourgogne La Chapelle Notre-Dame is a named sub-zone of Bourgogne in France, covering 4.5 ha in Ladoix-Serrigny. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.3 km north-east of Beaune and 29 km south-west of Dijon.
