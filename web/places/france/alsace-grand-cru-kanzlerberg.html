Alsace grand cru Kanzlerberg

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Kanzlerberg is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 3.3 ha in Bergheim. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.6 km south-west of Sélestat and 14 km north of Colmar.
