Vin de Corse Porto-Vecchio ou Corse Porto-Vecchio

Sub-zone

Vin de Corse Porto-Vecchio ou Corse Porto-Vecchio is a named sub-zone of Vin de Corse in France, covering 3,116.7 ha across 8 communes. That is 4.4% of the Vin de Corse appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
