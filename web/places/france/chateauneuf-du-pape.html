Châteauneuf-du-Pape

Appellation

Châteauneuf-du-Pape is an appellation in France, covering 3,737.3 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Southern Rhône appellation where Baron Le Roy drafted rules in the 1920s that inspired the French AOC system. A long list of permitted varieties, Grenache leading, on soils that range from large round stones to sand and limestone.
