Coteaux du Layon premier cru Chaume

Premier cruVineyard site

Coteaux du Layon premier cru Chaume is a premier cru climat of Coteaux du Layon in France, covering 177.6 ha in Rochefort-Sur-Loire. That is 2.9% of the Coteaux du Layon appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
