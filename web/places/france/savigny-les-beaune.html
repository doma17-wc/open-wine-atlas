Savigny-lès-Beaune

Appellation

Savigny-lès-Beaune is an appellation in France, covering 380.6 ha in Savigny-Les-Beaune. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
