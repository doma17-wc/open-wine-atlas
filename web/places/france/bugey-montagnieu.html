Bugey Montagnieu

Sub-zone

Bugey Montagnieu is a named sub-zone of Bugey in France, covering 123.9 ha in Briord, Montagnieu and Seillonnaz. That is 3.3% of the Bugey appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 15 km west of Belley and 50 km east of Lyon.
