Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Les Ruchots

Premier cruVineyard site

Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Les Ruchots is a premier cru climat of Morey-Saint-Denis in France, covering 2.6 ha in Morey-Saint-Denis. That is 1.7% of the Morey-Saint-Denis appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Aligoté. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 6.4 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 14 km south-west of Dijon.
