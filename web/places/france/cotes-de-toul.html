Côtes de Toul

Appellation

Côtes de Toul is an appellation in France, covering 587.3 ha across 8 communes. The rules allow rosé, red and white wine, based on Gamay, Pinot Noir, Aubin and Auxerrois. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
