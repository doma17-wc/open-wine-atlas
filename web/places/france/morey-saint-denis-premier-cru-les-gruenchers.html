Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Les Gruenchers

Premier cruVineyard site

Morey-Saint-Denis premier cru Les Gruenchers is a premier cru climat of Morey-Saint-Denis in France, covering 0.5 ha in Morey-Saint-Denis. That is 0.3% of the Morey-Saint-Denis appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Aligoté. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 13 km south-west of Dijon.
