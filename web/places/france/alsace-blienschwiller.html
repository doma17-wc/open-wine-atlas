Alsace Blienschwiller

Sub-zone

Alsace Blienschwiller is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 29.9 ha in Blienschwiller. That is 0.1% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
