Collioure

Appellation

Collioure is an appellation in France, covering 3,642.3 ha across 4 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 1.9 km west of Banyuls de la Marenda and 29 km south-east of Perpignan.
