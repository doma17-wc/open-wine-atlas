Bugey Cerdon

Sub-zone

Bugey Cerdon is a named sub-zone of Bugey in France, covering 859.3 ha across 10 communes. That is 23.0% of the Bugey appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 13 km south-east of Bourg-en-Bresse and 57 km north-east of Lyon.
