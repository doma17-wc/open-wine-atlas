Sainte-Croix-du-Mont

Appellation

Sainte-Croix-du-Mont is an appellation in France, covering 614.4 ha in Sainte-Croix-Du-Mont. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscadelle, Sauvignon, Sauvignon Gris and Semillon. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 5.3 km north of Langon and 36 km south-east of Bordeaux.
