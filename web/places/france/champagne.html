Champagne

Appellation

Champagne is an appellation in France across 312 communes. The rules allow rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Sparkling wine made by second fermentation in bottle, from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Meunier on chalk and marl. The historic échelle des crus ranks 17 villages as grand cru and 42 as premier cru.
