Pouilly-Fuissé premier cru Vers Cras

Premier cruVineyard site

Pouilly-Fuissé premier cru Vers Cras is a premier cru climat of Pouilly-Fuissé in France, covering 23.2 ha in Fuisse and Solutre-Pouilly. That is 2.9% of the Pouilly-Fuissé appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
