Languedoc Cabrières

Sub-zone

Languedoc Cabrières is a named sub-zone of Languedoc in France, covering 1,019.4 ha in Cabrieres. That is 0.4% of the Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north of Pézenas and 27 km north-east of Béziers.
