Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil

Appellation

Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil is an appellation in France, covering 1,248.8 ha in Saint-Nicolas-De-Bourgueil. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Cabernet Franc. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 16 km east of Saumur and 45 km west of Tours.
