Touraine Chenonceaux

Sub-zone

Touraine Chenonceaux is a named sub-zone of Touraine in France, covering 3,642.6 ha across 26 communes. That is 9.4% of the Touraine appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
