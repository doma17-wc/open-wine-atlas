Muscat de Mireval

Appellation

Muscat de Mireval is an appellation in France, covering 703.2 ha in Mireval and Vic-La-Gardiole. The rules allow white wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
