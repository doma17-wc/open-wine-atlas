Côtes d'Auvergne Boudes

Sub-zone

Côtes d'Auvergne Boudes is a named sub-zone of Côtes d'Auvergne in France, covering 180.1 ha in Boudes, Chalus and Saint-Herent. That is 6.0% of the Côtes d'Auvergne appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 11 km south-west of Issoire and 37 km south of Clermont-Ferrand.
