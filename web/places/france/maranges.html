Maranges

Appellation

Maranges is an appellation in France, covering 233.1 ha in Cheilly-Les-Maranges, Dezize-Les-Maranges and Sampigny-Les-Maranges. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.7 km west of Chagny and 20 km north-west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
