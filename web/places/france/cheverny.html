Cheverny

Appellation

Cheverny is an appellation in France, covering 6,376.6 ha across 23 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Sauvignon and Sauvignon Gris. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
