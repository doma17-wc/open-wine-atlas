Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Aux Chaignots

Premier cruVineyard site

Nuits-Saint-Georges premier cru Aux Chaignots is a premier cru climat of Nuits-Saint-Georges in France, covering 5.8 ha in Nuits-Saint-Georges. That is 1.8% of the Nuits-Saint-Georges appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
