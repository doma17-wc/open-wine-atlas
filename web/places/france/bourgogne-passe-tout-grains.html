Bourgogne Passe-tout-grains

Appellation

Bourgogne Passe-tout-grains is an appellation in France, covering 54,879.4 ha across 272 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Gamay and Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
