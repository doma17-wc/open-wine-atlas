Crémant de Loire

Appellation

Crémant de Loire is an appellation in France, covering 77,257.1 ha across 222 communes. The rules allow rosé and white wine, based on Chardonnay, Chenin and Orbois. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
