Anjou-Coteaux de la Loire

Appellation

Anjou-Coteaux de la Loire is an appellation in France, covering 1,428.3 ha across 8 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
