Beaujolais Odenas

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Odenas is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 671.3 ha in Odenas. That is 1.7% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks. It lies 8.6 km west of Belleville and 14 km north of Villefranche-sur-Saône.
