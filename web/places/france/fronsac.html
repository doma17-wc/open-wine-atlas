Fronsac

Appellation

Fronsac is an appellation in France, covering 1,730 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.3 km north-west of Libourne and 25 km east of Bordeaux.
