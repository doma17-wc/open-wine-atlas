Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf

Grand cruAppellation

Alsace grand cru Kaefferkopf is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 73.7 ha in Ammerschwihr. The rules allow white wine, based on Gewürztraminer. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 7.9 km north-west of Colmar and 45 km north-west of Freiburg.
