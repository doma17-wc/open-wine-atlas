Saint-Aubin

Appellation

Saint-Aubin is an appellation in France, covering 238.5 ha in Saint-Aubin. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.7 km north of Chagny and 22 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
