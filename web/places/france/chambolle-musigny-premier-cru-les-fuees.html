Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Les Fuées

Premier cruVineyard site

Chambolle-Musigny premier cru Les Fuées is a premier cru climat of Chambolle-Musigny in France, covering 4.4 ha in Chambolle-Musigny. That is 2.4% of the Chambolle-Musigny appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Noir. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.8 km north of Nuits-Saint-Georges and 15 km south-west of Dijon.
