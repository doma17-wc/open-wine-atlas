Morey-Saint-Denis

Appellation

Morey-Saint-Denis is an appellation in France, covering 150.5 ha in Morey-Saint-Denis. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc and Aligoté. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Village between Gevrey and Chambolle with five grands crus, among them two monopoles, Clos de Tart and almost all of Clos des Lambrays.
