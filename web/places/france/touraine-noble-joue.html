Touraine Noble Joué

Appellation

Touraine Noble Joué is an appellation in France, covering 351.2 ha across 5 communes. The rules allow rosé wine, based on Meunier. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.8 km north-east of Veigné and 11 km south of Tours.
