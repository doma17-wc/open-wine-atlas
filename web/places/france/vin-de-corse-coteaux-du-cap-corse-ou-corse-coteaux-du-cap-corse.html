Vin de Corse Coteaux du Cap Corse ou Corse Coteaux du Cap Corse

Sub-zone

Vin de Corse Coteaux du Cap Corse ou Corse Coteaux du Cap Corse is a named sub-zone of Vin de Corse in France, covering 4,124.2 ha across 11 communes. That is 5.8% of the Vin de Corse appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Nielluccio, Sciacarello and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.
