Montagne-Saint-Emilion

Appellation

Montagne-Saint-Emilion is an appellation in France, covering 2,375.3 ha in Montagne. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Cot and Merlot. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.9 km east of Libourne and 37 km east of Bordeaux.
