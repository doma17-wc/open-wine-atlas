Saint-Aubin premier cru En la Ranché

Premier cruVineyard site

Saint-Aubin premier cru En la Ranché is a premier cru climat of Saint-Aubin in France, covering 4 ha in Saint-Aubin. That is 1.7% of the Saint-Aubin appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.8 km north of Chagny and 22 km north of Chalon-sur-Saône.
