Côtes du Vivarais

Appellation

Côtes du Vivarais is an appellation in France, covering 5,225.9 ha across 14 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Grenache, Syrah and Grenache Blanc. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
