Givry premier cru Clos du Cellier aux Moines

Premier cruVineyard site

Givry premier cru Clos du Cellier aux Moines is a premier cru climat of Givry in France, covering 13.3 ha in Givry. That is 3.6% of the Givry appellation area. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.6 km west of Châtenoy-le-Royal and 9 km west of Chalon-sur-Saône.
