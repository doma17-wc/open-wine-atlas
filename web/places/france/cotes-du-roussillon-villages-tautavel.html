Côtes du Roussillon Villages Tautavel

Sub-zone

Côtes du Roussillon Villages Tautavel is a named sub-zone of Côtes du Roussillon Villages in France, covering 2,749.7 ha in Tautavel and Vingrau. That is 7.3% of the Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation area. The rules allow red wine, based on Carignan, Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
