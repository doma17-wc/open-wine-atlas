Coteaux de l'Aubance

Appellation

Coteaux de l'Aubance is an appellation in France, covering 1,180.6 ha across 7 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Chenin. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.3 km south of Bouchemaine and 11 km south-west of Angers.
