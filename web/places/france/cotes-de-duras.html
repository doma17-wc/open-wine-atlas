Côtes de Duras

Appellation

Côtes de Duras is an appellation in France, covering 11,214.3 ha across 15 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 23 km south-west of Bergerac and 70 km south-east of Bordeaux.
