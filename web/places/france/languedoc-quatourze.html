Languedoc Quatourze

Sub-zone

Languedoc Quatourze is a named sub-zone of Languedoc in France, covering 540.6 ha in Narbonne. That is 0.2% of the Languedoc appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
