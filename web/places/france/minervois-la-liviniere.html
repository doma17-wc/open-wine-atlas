Minervois-la-Livinière

Appellation

Minervois-la-Livinière is an appellation in France across 6 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Grenache, Lledoner Pelut, Mourvèdre and Syrah. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
