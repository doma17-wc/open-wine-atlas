Bellet

Appellation

Bellet is an appellation in France in Nice. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Brachet, Fuella Nera and Vermentino. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 2 km north-west of Nice and 9 km east of Cagnes-sur-Mer.
