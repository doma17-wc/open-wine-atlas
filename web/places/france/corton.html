Corton

Grand cruAppellation

Corton is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 160.5 ha in Aloxe-Corton, Ladoix-Serrigny and Pernand-Vergelesses. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.8 km north-east of Beaune and 29 km south-west of Dijon.

The largest grand cru of Burgundy and the only red grand cru of the Côte de Beaune, on the hill of Corton shared by Aloxe-Corton, Ladoix-Serrigny and Pernand-Vergelesses. A climat name can be added, such as Corton Bressandes or Corton Clos du Roi.
