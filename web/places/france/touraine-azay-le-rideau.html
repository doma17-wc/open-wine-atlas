Touraine Azay-le-Rideau

Sub-zone

Touraine Azay-le-Rideau is a named sub-zone of Touraine in France, covering 2,153.1 ha across 6 communes. That is 5.5% of the Touraine appellation area. The rules allow rosé, white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 20 km south-west of Fondettes and 26 km west of Tours.
