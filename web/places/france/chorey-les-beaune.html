Chorey-lès-Beaune

Appellation

Chorey-lès-Beaune is an appellation in France, covering 152.3 ha in Chorey-Les-Beaune. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies around the town of Beaune and 32 km south-west of Dijon.
