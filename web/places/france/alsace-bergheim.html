Alsace Bergheim

Sub-zone

Alsace Bergheim is a named sub-zone of Alsace in France, covering 94.7 ha in Bergheim and Ribeauville. That is 0.5% of the Alsace appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.
