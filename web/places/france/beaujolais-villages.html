Beaujolais Villages

Sub-zone

Beaujolais Villages is a named sub-zone of Beaujolais in France, covering 15,955.9 ha across 38 communes. That is 40.2% of the Beaujolais appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.
