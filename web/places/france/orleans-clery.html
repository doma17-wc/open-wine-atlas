Orléans-Cléry

Appellation

Orléans-Cléry is an appellation in France, covering 855.7 ha across 5 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.3 km west of Olivet and 10 km south-west of Orléans.
