La Romanée

Grand cruAppellation

La Romanée is a grand cru appellation in France, covering 0.8 ha in Vosne-Romanee.

Often cited as the smallest appellation in France, under one hectare. A monopole of the Comte Liger-Belair, just above Romanée-Conti.
