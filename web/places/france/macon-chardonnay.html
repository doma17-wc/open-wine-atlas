Mâcon Chardonnay

Sub-zone

Mâcon Chardonnay is a named sub-zone of Mâcon in France, covering 1,012.5 ha across 4 communes. That is 5.7% of the Mâcon appellation area. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Gamay, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 7.7 km south-west of Tournus and 21 km north of Mâcon.
