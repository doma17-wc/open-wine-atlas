Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Le Pallet

Sub-zone

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Le Pallet is a named sub-zone of Muscadet Sèvre et Maine in France, covering 213.4 ha in La Chapelle-Heulin and Le Pallet. That is 1.9% of the Muscadet Sèvre et Maine appellation area. The rules allow white wine, based on Melon. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks. It lies 5.7 km south-west of Vallet and 20 km south-east of Nantes.
