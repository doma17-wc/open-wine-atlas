Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise

Appellation

Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise is an appellation in France, covering 1,263 ha in Aubignan and Beaumes-De-Venise. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine, based on Muscat À Petits Grains Blancs and Muscat À Petits Grains Rouges. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
