Botticino

Appellation

Botticino is an appellation in Italy in Botticino, Brescia and Rezzato. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Marzemino, Sangiovese and Schiava Gentile. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 3.4 km east of Brescia and 58 km west of Verona.
