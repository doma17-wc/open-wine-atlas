Alezio

Appellation

Alezio is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Malvasia Nera Di Lecce, Montepulciano, Negro Amaro and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.5 km east of Gallipoli and 36 km south-west of Lecce.
