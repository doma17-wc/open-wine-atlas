Capalbio

Appellation

Capalbio is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine, based on Trebbiano Toscano, Vermentino, Sangiovese and Cabernet Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
