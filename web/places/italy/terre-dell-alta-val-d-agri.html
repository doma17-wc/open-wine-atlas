Terre dell'Alta Val d'Agri

Appellation

Terre dell'Alta Val d'Agri is an appellation in Italy in Grumento Nova, Moliterno and Viggiano. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Malvasia Bianca Di Basilicata. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
