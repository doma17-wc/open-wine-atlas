Vin Santo del Chianti

Appellation

Vin Santo del Chianti is an appellation in Italy across 91 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Malvasia Bianca Lunga, Trebbiano Toscano and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
