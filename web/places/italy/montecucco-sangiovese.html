Montecucco Sangiovese

DOCGAppellation

Montecucco Sangiovese is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Montecucco Sangiovese is grown on the lower slopes of Monte Amiata in the province of Grosseto, between Montalcino and the Maremma. It was raised from the Montecucco DOC to DOCG in 2011. The Consorzio reports that over 90% of production is certified organic.
