Valdinievole

Appellation

Valdinievole is an appellation in Italy across 12 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Trebbiano Toscano, Canaiolo Nero and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Valdinievole covers the valley of the Nievole between Pistoia and Lucca, around Montecatini Terme. Very little wine is claimed under it.
