Serralunga d'Alba

Sub-zone

Serralunga d'Alba is a commune of Barolo in Italy in Serralunga d'Alba. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 9.8 km south-west of Alba and 36 km south-west of Asti.

Whole commune in the zone (art. 3). Just over 353 ha of Nebbiolo da Barolo in 2018 and 39 MGA. The Enoteca Regionale del Barolo places it in the Serravallian (Elveziano) zone of compact grey-brown marls, giving structured wines for long ageing; Vietti puts Lazzarito on the Lequio formation.
