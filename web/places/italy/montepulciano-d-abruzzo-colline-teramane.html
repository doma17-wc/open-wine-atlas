Montepulciano d'Abruzzo Colline Teramane

DOCGAppellation

Montepulciano d'Abruzzo Colline Teramane is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 31 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Montepulciano and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
