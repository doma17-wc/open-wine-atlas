Ruchè di Castagnole Monferrato

DOCGAppellation

Ruchè di Castagnole Monferrato is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Brachetto and Ruchè. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 11 km north-east of Asti and 24 km north-west of Alessandria.

Ruchè di Castagnole Monferrato is a red DOCG from the aromatic Ruchè grape, grown in Castagnole Monferrato and six nearby communes in the province of Asti. The wine is perfumed, with floral and spice notes. It is one of the smallest DOCGs of Piedmont.
