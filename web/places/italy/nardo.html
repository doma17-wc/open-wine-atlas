Nardò

Appellation

Nardò is an appellation in Italy in Nardò and Porto Cesareo. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Malvasia Nera Di Brindisi, Malvasia Nera Di Lecce, Montepulciano and Negro Amaro. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
