Candia dei Colli Apuani

Appellation

Candia dei Colli Apuani is an appellation in Italy in Carrara, Massa and Montignoso. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.6 km north of Massa and 3 km south-east of Carrara.

A small DOC on the terraced slopes of the Apuan Alps above Massa and Carrara, in the north-west corner of Tuscany. Vermentino is the main grape.
