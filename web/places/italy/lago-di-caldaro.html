Lago di Caldaro

Appellation

Lago di Caldaro is an appellation in Italy across 19 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Schiava Gentile, Schiava Grigia and Schiava Grossa. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
