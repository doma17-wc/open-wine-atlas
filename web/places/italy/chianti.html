Chianti

DOCGAppellation

Chianti is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 91 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Large Tuscan appellation surrounding Chianti Classico, mainly Sangiovese, with sub-zones such as Rufina and Colli Senesi.
