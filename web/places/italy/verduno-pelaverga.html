Verduno Pelaverga

Appellation

Verduno Pelaverga is an appellation in Italy in La Morra, Roddi and Verduno. The rules allow red wine, based on Pelaverga Piccolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.1 km west of Alba and 35 km south-west of Asti.
