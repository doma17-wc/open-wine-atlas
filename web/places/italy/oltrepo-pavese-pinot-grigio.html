Oltrepò Pavese Pinot grigio

Appellation

Oltrepò Pavese Pinot grigio is an appellation in Italy across 41 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Pinot Grigio and Pinot Nero. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
