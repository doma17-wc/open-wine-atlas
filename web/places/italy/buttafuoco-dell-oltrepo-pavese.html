Buttafuoco dell'Oltrepò Pavese

Appellation

Buttafuoco dell'Oltrepò Pavese is an appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Croatina, Uva Rara and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 3.9 km west of Stradella and 17 km south of Pavia.
