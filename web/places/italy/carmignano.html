Carmignano

DOCGAppellation

Carmignano is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Carmignano and Poggio a Caiano. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.7 km west of Poggio A Caiano and 11 km south-west of Prato.

Carmignano lies on the Montalbano ridge west of Florence, in the province of Prato. It was one of the four zones delimited by Cosimo III de' Medici in 1716. Its rules have long required Cabernet in the blend with Sangiovese.
