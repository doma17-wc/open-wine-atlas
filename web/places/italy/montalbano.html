Montalbano

Sub-zone

Montalbano is a sub-zone of Chianti in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese.

Montalbano covers the Montalbano ridge west of Florence, in the provinces of Florence and Pistoia. It surrounds the Carmignano DOCG.
