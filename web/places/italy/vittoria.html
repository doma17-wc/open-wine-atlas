Italy · DOP (DOC/DOCG)

Vittoria

Appellation

Vittoria is an appellation in Italy across 14 communes. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Frappato, Nero D'Avola and Ansonica. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
