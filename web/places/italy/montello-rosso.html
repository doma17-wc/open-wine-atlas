Montello Rosso

DOCGAppellation

Montello Rosso is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 18 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Carmenere and Merlot. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
