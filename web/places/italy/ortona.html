Ortona

Appellation

Ortona is an appellation in Italy in Ortona. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Trebbiano Abruzzese, Trebbiano Toscano and Montepulciano. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies around the town of Ortona and 13 km north of Lanciano.
