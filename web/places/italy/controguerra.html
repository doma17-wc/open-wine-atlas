Controguerra

Appellation

Controguerra is an appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 8.7 km west of Martinsicuro and 13 km south-west of San Benedetto del Tronto.
