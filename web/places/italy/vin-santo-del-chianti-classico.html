Vin Santo del Chianti Classico

Appellation

Vin Santo del Chianti Classico is an appellation in Italy across 8 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Malvasia Bianca, Trebbiano Toscano and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

The Vin Santo DOC of the Chianti Classico zone, made from dried grapes aged in caratelli.
