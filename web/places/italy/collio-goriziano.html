Collio Goriziano

Appellation

Collio Goriziano is an appellation in Italy across 8 communes. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.4 km north-east of Cormons and 11 km north-west of Gorizia.
