Valdadige Terradeiforti

Appellation

Valdadige Terradeiforti is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Pinot Grigio, Lambrusco A Foglia Frastagliata and Casetta. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
