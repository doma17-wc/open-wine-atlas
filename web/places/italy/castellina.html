Castellina

Sub-zone

Castellina is a UGA of Chianti Classico in Italy in Castellina in Chianti. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 9.1 km east of Poggibonsi and 17 km north of Siena.

Castellina covers the whole commune of Castellina in Chianti in the centre-west of the zone. Vines grow mainly at 300 to 500 m on the Sillano Formation, with alberese east of the provincial road. Lake deposits, clayey near Lilliano and stony elsewhere, lie at 200 to 300 m.
