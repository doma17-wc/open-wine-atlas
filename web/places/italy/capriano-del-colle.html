Capriano del Colle

Appellation

Capriano del Colle is an appellation in Italy in Capriano Del Colle and Poncarale. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 3.3 km north-west of Bagnolo Mella and 10 km south-west of Brescia.
