Dolcetto di Diano d'Alba

DOCGAppellation

Dolcetto di Diano d'Alba is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Diano d'Alba. The rules allow red wine, based on Dolcetto. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Alba and 31 km south-west of Asti.

This 100% Dolcetto DOCG covers only the commune of Diano d'Alba, on the ridge between the Barolo zone and Alba. Its vineyard sites are recognised as sorì. A Superiore needs about 10 months of ageing.
