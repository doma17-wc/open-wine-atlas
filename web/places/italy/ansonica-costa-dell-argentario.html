Ansonica Costa dell'Argentario

Appellation

Ansonica Costa dell'Argentario is an appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Ansonica. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

A white wine from Ansonica grown on the Monte Argentario promontory, the Isola del Giglio and the nearby coast in the south of the province of Grosseto. Ansonica is the Tuscan name of the Sicilian Inzolia.
