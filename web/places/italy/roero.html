Roero

DOCGAppellation

Roero is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 19 communes. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Nebbiolo and Arneis. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Sandy hills north of the Tanaro river opposite the Langhe, for Nebbiolo reds and Arneis whites.
