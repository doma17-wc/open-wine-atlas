Bolgheri Sassicaia

Appellation

Bolgheri Sassicaia is an appellation in Italy in Castagneto Carducci. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.3 km east of Donoratico and 28 km north of Piombino.

Bolgheri Sassicaia is a single-estate DOC inside the Bolgheri zone, made by Tenuta San Guido. It was a sub-zone of Bolgheri from 1994 and became its own DOC in 2013.
