Vagliagli

Sub-zone

Vagliagli is a UGA of Chianti Classico in Italy in Castelnuovo Berardenga. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 12 km north of Siena and 41 km south of Florence.

Vagliagli covers the western part of the commune of Castelnuovo Berardenga inside the zone, north-east of Siena. Macigno dominates in the north, often above 400 m. Marine sands and conglomerates, lake deposits and alberese appear further south, where altitude falls below 300 m.
