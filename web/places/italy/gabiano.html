Gabiano

Appellation

Gabiano is an appellation in Italy in Gabiano and Moncestino. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Freisa and Grignolino. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.4 km west of Trino and 26 km south-west of Vercelli.
