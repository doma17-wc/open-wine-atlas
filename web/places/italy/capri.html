Capri

Appellation

Capri is an appellation in Italy in Anacapri and Capri. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Piedirosso, Falanghina and Greco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Capri and 33 km south of Naples.
