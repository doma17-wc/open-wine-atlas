Bianco dell'Empolese

Appellation

Bianco dell'Empolese is an appellation in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Trebbiano Toscano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

A white wine and Vin Santo from the hills around Empoli, west of Florence. Trebbiano Toscano is the main grape.
