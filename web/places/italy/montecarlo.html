Montecarlo

Appellation

Montecarlo is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Montecarlo is a small hill zone east of Lucca. Its white wines blend Trebbiano with French varieties such as Pinot Bianco, Roussanne, Sauvignon Blanc and Semillon.
