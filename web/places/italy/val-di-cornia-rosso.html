Val di Cornia Rosso

DOCGAppellation

Val di Cornia Rosso is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

A small DOCG for red wine in the Val di Cornia, inland from Piombino in the provinces of Livorno and Pisa. It was split from the Val di Cornia DOC in 2011. Sangiovese leads the blend with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.
