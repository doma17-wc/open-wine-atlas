Val di Cornia Rosso

DOCGAppellation

Val di Cornia Rosso is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
