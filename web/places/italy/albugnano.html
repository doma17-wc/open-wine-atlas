Albugnano

Appellation

Albugnano is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Barbera, Bonarda, Freisa and Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km east of Chieri and 23 km east of Turin.
