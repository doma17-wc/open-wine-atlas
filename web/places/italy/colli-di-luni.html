Colli di Luni

Appellation

Colli di Luni is an appellation in Italy across 17 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Trebbiano Toscano, Vermentino, Albarola and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
