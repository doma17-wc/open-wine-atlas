Barbaresco

DOCGAppellation

Barbaresco is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies next to Alba and 26 km south-west of Asti.

Nebbiolo from Barbaresco, Neive, Treiso and part of Alba, north-east of Barolo. At least 26 months of ageing, 9 in wood. Usually a little earlier to approach than Barolo. Single sites follow an official MGA list.
