Malvasia di Casorzo d'Asti

Appellation

Malvasia di Casorzo d'Asti is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Barbera, Freisa, Grignolino and Malvasia Di Casorzo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km south-west of Casale Monferrato and 21 km north-west of Alessandria.
