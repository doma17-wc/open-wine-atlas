Cesanese del Piglio

DOCGAppellation

Cesanese del Piglio is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cesanese Comune and Cesanese D'Affile. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks.
