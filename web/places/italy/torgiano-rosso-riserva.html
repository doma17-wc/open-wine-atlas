Torgiano Rosso Riserva

DOCGAppellation

Torgiano Rosso Riserva is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Torgiano. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 9.1 km south-east of Perugia and 23 km north-west of Foligno.
