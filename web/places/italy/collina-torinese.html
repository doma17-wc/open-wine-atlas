Collina Torinese

Appellation

Collina Torinese is an appellation in Italy across 28 communes. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Collina Torinese covers the hills east and south of Turin in 28 communes. It makes red and sweet red wines from Barbera, Bonarda, Freisa, Malvasia Nera and Pelaverga (Cari). Volumes are very small.
