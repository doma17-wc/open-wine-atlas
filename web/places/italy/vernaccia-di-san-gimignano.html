Vernaccia di San Gimignano

DOCGAppellation

Vernaccia di San Gimignano is a DOCG appellation in Italy in San Gimignano. The rules allow white wine, based on Vernaccia Di S. Giminiano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.3 km west of Poggibonsi and 30 km north-west of Siena.

The only white DOCG of Tuscany, from the commune of San Gimignano in the province of Siena. It received the DOC in 1966 and the DOCG in 1993. The Vernaccia grape covers 751 ha of the regional vineyard.
