Moscadello di Montalcino

Appellation

Moscadello di Montalcino is an appellation in Italy in Montalcino. The rules allow white wine, based on Moscato Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

A sweet or late-harvest white from Moscato Bianco in the commune of Montalcino. It recalls the sweet wine for which Montalcino was known before Brunello.
