Barolo

Sub-zone

Barolo is a commune of Barolo in Italy in Barolo. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 12 km south-west of Alba and 38 km south-west of Asti.

Whole commune in the zone (art. 3). In 2018 the Consorzio counted just over 260 ha of Nebbiolo da Barolo, 41 wineries and 37 MGA. The Enoteca Regionale del Barolo groups it with La Morra in the Tortonian zone of less compact blue marls, while Michele Chiarlo and the Wine Scholar Guild say the Cannubi hill has both Tortonian and Serravallian soils. Brunate, Cerequio and Fossati (with La Morra), Bussia (with Monforte d'Alba) and Ravera (with Novello) cross its border.
