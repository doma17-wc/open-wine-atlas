Coste della Sesia

Appellation

Coste della Sesia is an appellation in Italy across 16 communes. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine, based on Erbaluce, Nebbiolo, Croatina and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
