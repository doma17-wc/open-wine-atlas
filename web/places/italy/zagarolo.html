Zagarolo

Appellation

Zagarolo is an appellation in Italy in Gallicano Nel Lazio and Zagarolo. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks. It lies around the town of Zagarolo and 15 km south of Guidonia Montecelio.
