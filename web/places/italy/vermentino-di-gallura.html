Vermentino di Gallura

DOCGAppellation

Vermentino di Gallura is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 23 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Vermentino. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.

Sardinia's only DOCG: Vermentino on granite soils in the north-east of the island.
