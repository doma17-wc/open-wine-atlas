Pomino

Appellation

Pomino is an appellation in Italy in Rufina. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.6 km north-east of Pontassieve and 23 km east of Florence.

Pomino is a high hill zone in the commune of Rufina, east of Florence. Its rules favour French varieties such as Chardonnay, Pinot Bianco and Pinot Nero.
