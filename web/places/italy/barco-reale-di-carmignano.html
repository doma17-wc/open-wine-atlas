Barco Reale di Carmignano

Appellation

Barco Reale di Carmignano is an appellation in Italy in Carmignano and Poggio A Caiano. The rules allow red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 2.7 km west of Poggio A Caiano and 11 km south-west of Prato.
