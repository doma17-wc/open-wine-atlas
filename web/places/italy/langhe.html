Langhe

Appellation

Langhe is an appellation in Italy across 93 communes. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Langhe is the umbrella DOC of the Langhe hills in the province of Cuneo, in 93 communes. It covers many varietal wines and blends, the best known being Langhe Nebbiolo. It is often used for wines declassified from Barolo or Barbaresco and for international varieties. New Langhe Nebbiolo plantings were capped from 2025.
