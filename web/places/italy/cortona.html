Cortona

Appellation

Cortona is an appellation in Italy in Cortona. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Cortona lies in the commune of the same name in the south of the province of Arezzo, above the Val di Chiana. It is known for varietal Syrah.
