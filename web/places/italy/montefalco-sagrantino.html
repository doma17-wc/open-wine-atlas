Montefalco Sagrantino

DOCGAppellation

Montefalco Sagrantino is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sagrantino. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks.

Sagrantino from Montefalco in Umbria, one of the most tannic grapes in the world, made dry or as a sweet passito.
