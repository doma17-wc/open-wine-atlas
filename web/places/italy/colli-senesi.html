Colli Senesi

Sub-zone

Colli Senesi is a sub-zone of Chianti in Italy across 15 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese.

Colli Senesi is the largest Chianti sottozona. It covers hills of the province of Siena around Chianti Classico and reaches south to Montalcino and Montepulciano. It has stricter grape rules than the rest of Chianti.
