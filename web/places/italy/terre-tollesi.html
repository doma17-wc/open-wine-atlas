Terre Tollesi

Appellation

Terre Tollesi is an appellation in Italy in Tollo. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Pecorino, Passerina and Montepulciano. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 8.6 km south of Francavilla al Mare and 15 km south-east of Pescara.
