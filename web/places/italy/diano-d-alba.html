Diano d'Alba

Sub-zone

Diano d'Alba is a commune of Barolo in Italy in Diano d'Alba. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 6 km south of Alba and 32 km south-west of Asti.

Partly in the zone, on its eastern edge. Three Barolo MGA: Gallaretto, La Vigna and Sorano, the last shared with Serralunga d'Alba. Better known for Dolcetto di Diano d'Alba.
