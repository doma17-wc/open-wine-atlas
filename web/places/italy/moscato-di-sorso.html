Moscato di Sorso

Appellation

Moscato di Sorso is an appellation in Italy in Sennori and Sorso. The rules allow white wine, based on Moscato Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies around the town of Sorso and 11 km north of Sassari.
