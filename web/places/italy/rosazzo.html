Rosazzo

DOCGAppellation

Rosazzo is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Corno Di Rosazzo, Manzano and San Giovanni Al Natisone. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies next to Manzano and 15 km south-east of Udine.
