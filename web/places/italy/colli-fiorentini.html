Colli Fiorentini

Sub-zone

Colli Fiorentini is a sub-zone of Chianti in Italy across 17 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese.

Colli Fiorentini covers the hills around Florence, mostly south of the city between the Pesa, Greve and Arno valleys. It borders Chianti Classico to the south.
