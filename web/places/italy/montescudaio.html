Montescudaio

Appellation

Montescudaio is an appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Montescudaio covers hills inland from Cecina in the province of Pisa, north of Bolgheri.
