Oltrepò Pavese metodo classico

DOCGAppellation

Oltrepò Pavese metodo classico is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 41 communes. The rules allow white and rosé wine, based on Chardonnay, Pinot Bianco, Pinot Grigio and Pinot Nero. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
