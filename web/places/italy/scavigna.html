Scavigna

Appellation

Scavigna is an appellation in Italy in Falerna and Nocera Terinese. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km north-west of Lamezia Terme and 13 km north-west of Nicastro.
