Cervognano

Sub-zone

Cervognano is a pieve of Vino Nobile di Montepulciano in Italy in Montepulciano. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 6.9 km north-east of Chianciano Terme and 44 km west of Perugia.

Cervognano lies in the south-east of the commune, between the railway, the Salcheto and Marmo ditches and the SP 326. Tenute del Cerro places it in the southern group (250 to 400 m, silty clays over Pliocene river and lake deposits).
