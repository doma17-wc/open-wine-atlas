San Donato in Poggio

Sub-zone

San Donato in Poggio is a UGA of Chianti Classico in Italy in Barberino Tavarnelle and Poggibonsi. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 5.9 km south-east of Tavarnelle Val di Pesa and 27 km south of Florence.

San Donato in Poggio covers the parts of Barberino Tavarnelle and a small part of Poggibonsi inside the zone, on its western edge. It runs from Badia a Passignano south to Olena and Monsanto. Soils are alberese in the centre, with pietraforte, the Sillano Formation and marine sands near Monsanto.
