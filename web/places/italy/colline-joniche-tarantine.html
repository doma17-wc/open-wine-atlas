Colline Joniche Tarantine

Appellation

Colline Joniche Tarantine is an appellation in Italy across 10 communes. The rules allow white, rosé and red wine, based on Chardonnay, Verdeca, Cabernet Sauvignon and Primitivo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
