Bolgheri

Appellation

Bolgheri is an appellation in Italy in Castagneto Carducci. The rules allow white, rosé and red wine. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 2.3 km east of Donoratico and 28 km north of Piombino.

Coastal Tuscan appellation where Bordeaux varieties made the area famous with Sassicaia and Ornellaia. Bolgheri Sassicaia has its own separate DOC.
