Etna

Appellation

Etna is an appellation in Italy across 20 communes. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks.

Vineyards on the slopes of Europe's most active volcano in Sicily, up to about 1,000 m. Nerello Mascalese for reds, Carricante for whites. Producers name individual 'contrade'.
