Franciacorta

DOCGAppellation

Franciacorta is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 19 communes. The rules allow white and rosé wine, based on Chardonnay, Erbamat, Pinot Nero and Pinot Bianco. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Lombardy's traditional-method sparkling wine from Chardonnay, Pinot Nero and Pinot Bianco, with the local Erbamat also allowed, on moraine soils south of Lake Iseo.
