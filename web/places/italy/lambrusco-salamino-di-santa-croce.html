Lambrusco Salamino di Santa Croce

Appellation

Lambrusco Salamino di Santa Croce is an appellation in Italy across 13 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Ancellotta, Fortana and Lambrusco Salamino. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
