Villamagna

Appellation

Villamagna is an appellation in Italy in Bucchianico, Vacri and Villamagna. The rules allow red wine, based on Montepulciano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5 km south of Chieti and 17 km south of Pescara.
