Boca

Appellation

Boca is an appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo, Uva Rara and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: acid volcanic rocks. It lies 6.4 km west of Borgomanero and 32 km north-west of Novara.
