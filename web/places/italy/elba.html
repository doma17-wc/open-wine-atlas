Elba

Appellation

Elba is an appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

The DOC of the island of Elba in the Tuscan archipelago. It covers dry whites from Ansonica, Vermentino and Trebbiano and reds from Sangiovese.
