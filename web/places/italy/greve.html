Greve

Sub-zone

Greve is a UGA of Chianti Classico in Italy in Greve in Chianti. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 13 km west of Figline Valdarno and 23 km south of Florence.

Greve covers the part of the commune of Greve in Chianti along the Greve valley that is not in Lamole, Montefioralle or Panzano. It runs north to Strada and Chiocchio and east to San Polo, Dudda and Lucolena. Soils mix macigno on the higher western hills, shale near Strada and the Sillano Formation and pietraforte lower down; altitude is 150 to 375 m.
