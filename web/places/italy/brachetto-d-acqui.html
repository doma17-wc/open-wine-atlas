Brachetto d'Acqui

DOCGAppellation

Brachetto d'Acqui is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 26 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Brachetto. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Brachetto d'Acqui is a sweet, aromatic red DOCG from Brachetto grown around Acqui Terme, in 26 communes of the provinces of Alessandria and Asti. Most of it is sparkling or lightly sparkling and low in alcohol. Spumante and passito versions are also made.
