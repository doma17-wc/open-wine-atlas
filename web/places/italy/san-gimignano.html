San Gimignano

Appellation

San Gimignano is an appellation in Italy in San Gimignano. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.3 km west of Poggibonsi and 30 km north-west of Siena.

San Gimignano DOC covers the red, rosato and Vin Santo of the commune of San Gimignano. Its white wine is the separate Vernaccia di San Gimignano DOCG.
