Nizza

DOCGAppellation

Nizza is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 18 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Top tier of Barbera from 18 communes around Nizza Monferrato, a separate DOCG since 2014.
