Cellatica

Appellation

Cellatica is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Incrocio Terzi 1, Marzemino and Schiava Gentile. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 1.5 km north-west of Gussago and 8 km north-west of Brescia.
