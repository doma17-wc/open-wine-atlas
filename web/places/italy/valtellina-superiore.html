Valtellina Superiore

DOCGAppellation

Valtellina Superiore is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 16 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

Nebbiolo, locally called Chiavennasca, on steep terraces along the Adda valley in the Alps of Lombardy.
