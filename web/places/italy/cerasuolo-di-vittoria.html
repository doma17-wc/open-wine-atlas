Cerasuolo di Vittoria

DOCGAppellation

Cerasuolo di Vittoria is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 14 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Frappato and Nero D'Avola. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Sicily's only DOCG: a blend of Nero d'Avola and Frappato from the south-east of the island.
