Montecompatri Colonna

Appellation

Montecompatri Colonna is an appellation in Italy in Monte Compatri, Rocca Priora and Zagarolo. The rules allow white wine. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks. It lies 5.7 km south-west of Zagarolo and 10 km east of Marino.
