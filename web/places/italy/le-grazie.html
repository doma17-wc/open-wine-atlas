Le Grazie

Sub-zone

Le Grazie is a pieve of Vino Nobile di Montepulciano in Italy in Montepulciano. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 7.5 km north of Chianciano Terme and 49 km west of Perugia.

Le Grazie lies north-west of the town of Montepulciano, between the Salarco stream, the Torrita road, the SP 17 and the commune boundary. Tenute del Cerro places it in the northern group of Pievi (300 to 500 m, clay-limestone soils with sand).
