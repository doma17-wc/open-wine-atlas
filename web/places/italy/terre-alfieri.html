Terre Alfieri

DOCGAppellation

Terre Alfieri is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Arneis and Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km south-west of Asti and 36 km south-east of Moncalieri.

Terre Alfieri covers 11 communes between the Roero and the Asti hills, on both sides of the Tanaro. It makes a white from Arneis and a red from Nebbiolo. It was a DOC from 2009 and became a DOCG in 2020.
