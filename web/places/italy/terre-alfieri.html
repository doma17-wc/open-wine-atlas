Terre Alfieri

Appellation

Terre Alfieri is an appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Arneis and Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 14 km south-west of Asti and 36 km south-east of Moncalieri.
