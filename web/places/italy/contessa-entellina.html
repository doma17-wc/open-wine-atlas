Contessa Entellina

Appellation

Contessa Entellina is an appellation in Italy in Contessa Entellina. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 10 km north-east of Santa Margherita di Belice and 29 km south of Alcamo.
