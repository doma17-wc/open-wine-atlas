Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi

Appellation

Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi is an appellation in Italy across 24 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Verdicchio Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
