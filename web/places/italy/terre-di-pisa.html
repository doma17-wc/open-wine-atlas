Terre di Pisa

Appellation

Terre di Pisa is an appellation in Italy across 15 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Sangiovese and Syrah. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Terre di Pisa covers the hills of the province of Pisa, overlapping the Chianti Colline Pisane sub-zone. It began as a red-only DOC.
