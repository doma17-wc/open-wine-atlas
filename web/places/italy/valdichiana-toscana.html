Valdichiana toscana

Appellation

Valdichiana toscana is an appellation in Italy across 12 communes. The rules allow white, red and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Valdichiana Toscana covers the Val di Chiana plain and hills in the provinces of Arezzo and Siena. It was called Bianco Vergine Valdichiana until 2011.
