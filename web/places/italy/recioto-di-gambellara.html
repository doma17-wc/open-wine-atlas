Recioto di Gambellara

DOCGAppellation

Recioto di Gambellara is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Garganega. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies next to Montebello Vicentino and 17 km south-west of Vicenza.
