Bonarda dell'Oltrepò Pavese

Appellation

Bonarda dell'Oltrepò Pavese is an appellation in Italy across 41 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Croatina, Uva Rara and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
