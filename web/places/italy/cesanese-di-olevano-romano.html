Cesanese di Olevano Romano

Appellation

Cesanese di Olevano Romano is an appellation in Italy in Genazzano and Olevano Romano. The rules allow red wine. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks. It lies 3.9 km east of Cave and 29 km south-east of Guidonia Montecelio.
