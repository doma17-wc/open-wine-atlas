Gaiole

Sub-zone

Gaiole is a UGA of Chianti Classico in Italy in Gaiole in Chianti. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. It lies 16 km north-east of Siena and 40 km south of Florence.

Gaiole covers the whole commune of Gaiole in Chianti on the east side of the zone, below the Monti del Chianti. It includes Vertine, Lecchi, Ama, San Polo in Rosso, Castagnoli and Monti. Soils are macigno in the east, alberese in the centre and west and sandier in the south.
