Conegliano Valdobbiadene - Prosecco

DOCGAppellation

Conegliano Valdobbiadene - Prosecco is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 15 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Glera. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

The hilly heart of Prosecco Superiore in the Veneto, from Glera on steep 'hogback' slopes, a UNESCO World Heritage landscape since 2019. Cartizze is its most famous hill.
