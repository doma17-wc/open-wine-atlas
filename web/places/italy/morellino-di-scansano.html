Morellino di Scansano

DOCGAppellation

Morellino di Scansano is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Morellino is the local name for Sangiovese in the hills around Scansano in the province of Grosseto. The zone lies between the Ombrone and Albegna rivers, near the Tyrrhenian coast. A Superiore category was added by ministerial decree of 28 January 2026.
