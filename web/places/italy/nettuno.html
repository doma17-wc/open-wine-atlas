Nettuno

Appellation

Nettuno is an appellation in Italy in Anzio and Nettuno. The rules allow red, rosé and white wine, based on Merlot, Sangiovese, Trebbiano Toscano and Bellone. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies around the town of Nettuno and 5 km north-east of Anzio.
