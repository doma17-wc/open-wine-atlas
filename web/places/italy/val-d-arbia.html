Val d'Arbia

Appellation

Val d'Arbia is an appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow white and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Val d'Arbia covers the valley of the Arbia south-east of Siena. Almost no wine is now claimed under it.
