Sizzano

Appellation

Sizzano is an appellation in Italy in Sizzano. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo, Uva Rara and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.3 km south-east of Gattinara and 20 km north-west of Novara.
