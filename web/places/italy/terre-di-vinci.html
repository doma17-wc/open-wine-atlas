Terre di Vinci

Sub-zone

Terre di Vinci is a sub-zone of Chianti in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese.

Terre di Vinci is the newest Chianti sottozona, around the birthplace of Leonardo da Vinci west of Florence. It came with the Chianti amendment published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale in 2026. Its commune list here comes from press reports, not from the disciplinare text.
