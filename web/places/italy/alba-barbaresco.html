Alba

Sub-zone

Alba is a commune of Barbaresco in Italy in Alba. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies around the town of Alba and 27 km south-west of Asti.

Only part of the frazione San Rocco Seno d'Elvio is in the zone: the part that belonged to Barbaresco and was joined to Alba by DPR 17 aprile 1957 n. 482 (art. 3). The Enoteca Regionale del Barbaresco groups it with Treiso on the Lequio formation. Four MGA, three of them shared with Treiso.
