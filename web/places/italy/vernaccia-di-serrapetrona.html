Vernaccia di Serrapetrona

DOCGAppellation

Vernaccia di Serrapetrona is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Belforte Del Chienti, San Severino Marche and Serrapetrona. The rules allow red wine, based on Vernaccia Nera. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
