Greco di Bianco

Appellation

Greco di Bianco is an appellation in Italy in Casignana. The rules allow white wine, based on Greco Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.7 km south-west of Bovalino Superiore and 50 km east of Messina.
