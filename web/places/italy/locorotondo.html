Locorotondo

Appellation

Locorotondo is an appellation in Italy in Cisternino, Fasano and Locorotondo. The rules allow white wine, based on Bianco D'Alessano, Verdeca and Fiano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
