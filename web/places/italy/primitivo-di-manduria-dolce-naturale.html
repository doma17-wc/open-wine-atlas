Primitivo di Manduria Dolce Naturale

DOCGAppellation

Primitivo di Manduria Dolce Naturale is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 19 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Primitivo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
