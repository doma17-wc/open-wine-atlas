Malvasia di Castelnuovo Don Bosco

Appellation

Malvasia di Castelnuovo Don Bosco is an appellation in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Freisa, Malvasia Di Schierano and Malvasia Nera Lunga. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
