Ramandolo

DOCGAppellation

Ramandolo is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Nimis and Tarcento. The rules allow white wine, based on Verduzzo Friulano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.1 km east of Tarcento and 18 km north of Udine.
