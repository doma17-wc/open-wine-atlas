Grignolino d'Asti

Appellation

Grignolino d'Asti is an appellation in Italy across 34 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Freisa and Grignolino. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Grignolino d'Asti covers 35 communes of the province of Asti. Grignolino makes a pale red with high acidity and firm tannin.
