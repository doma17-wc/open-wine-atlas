Fiano di Avellino

DOCGAppellation

Fiano di Avellino is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 26 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Fiano. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

White Fiano from the Irpinia hills in Campania, with a long ageing potential unusual for southern whites.
