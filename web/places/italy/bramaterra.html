Bramaterra

Appellation

Bramaterra is an appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Croatina, Nebbiolo, Uva Rara and Vespolina. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 6.8 km east of Cossato and 16 km east of Biella.
