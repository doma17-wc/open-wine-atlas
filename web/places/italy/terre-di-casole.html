Terre di Casole

Appellation

Terre di Casole is an appellation in Italy in Casole d'Elsa. The rules allow white and red wine, based on Chardonnay and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Terre di Casole covers the commune of Casole d'Elsa in the province of Siena, west of Chianti Classico.
