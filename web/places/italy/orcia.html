Orcia

Appellation

Orcia is an appellation in Italy across 12 communes. The rules allow red, white and rosé wine, based on Sangiovese, Trebbiano Toscano and Malvasia Bianca Lunga. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Orcia covers the Val d'Orcia between Montalcino and Montepulciano in the province of Siena. The Consorzio aims to raise its Sangiovese to DOCG, but this was not done as of 2025.
