Rosso di Montepulciano

Appellation

Rosso di Montepulciano is an appellation in Italy in Montepulciano. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Rosso di Montepulciano is the younger red of the commune of Montepulciano, from the same zone as Vino Nobile.

584 ha ARTEA schedario viticolo, registered as claimable for Rosso di Montepulciano at 31 July 2024: 584 ha, 275 farms (Regione Toscana). The Consorzio press kit 2024 gives about 550 ha registered, and 214 ha at 31 Dec 2023 (source ARTEA). The 214 ha is the area actually claimed for the 2023 harvest: the kit gives 2,145 t of grapes, 10.0 t/ha, the maximum yield. Most Rosso vineyards are also registered for Vino Nobile and are claimed as Nobile, which explains the gap.
