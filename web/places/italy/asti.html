Asti

DOCGAppellation

Asti is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 51 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Moscato Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Sweet, lightly sparkling Moscato Bianco from Piedmont, including the gentler Moscato d'Asti.

Canelli
Canelli was a subzone of Asti for Moscato d'Asti. It became a separate DOCG, Canelli (Moscato Bianco 100%, 9.5 t/ha, Riserva 30 months from 1 October with 20 in bottle), registered by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1327 of 23 June 2023 (OJ L 166, 30.06.2023). Its 7 whole and 10 partial communes all lie inside the Asti zone. It is not an atlas appellation.
