Scanzo

DOCGAppellation

Scanzo is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Scanzorosciate. The rules allow red wine, based on Moscato Di Scanzo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 4.8 km north-east of Seriate and 8 km east of Bergamo.
