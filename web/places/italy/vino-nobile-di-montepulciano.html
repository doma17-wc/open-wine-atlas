Vino Nobile di Montepulciano

DOCGAppellation

Vino Nobile di Montepulciano is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Montepulciano. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Sangiovese, locally called Prugnolo Gentile, from the town of Montepulciano in south-east Tuscany. Not related to the Montepulciano grape.
