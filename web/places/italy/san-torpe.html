San Torpè

Appellation

San Torpè is an appellation in Italy across 16 communes. The rules allow white and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

San Torpè covers hills of the province of Pisa and was called Bianco Pisano di San Torpè until 2011. Almost no wine is now claimed under it.
