Contea di Sclafani

Appellation

Contea di Sclafani is an appellation in Italy across 14 communes. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Nero D'Avola, Perricone, Catarratto Bianco Comune and Catarratto Bianco Lucido. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
