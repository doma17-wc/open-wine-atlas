Brunello di Montalcino

DOCGAppellation

Brunello di Montalcino is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Montalcino. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

100% Sangiovese from the hill town of Montalcino in southern Tuscany. Released no earlier than January of the fifth year after harvest, with at least two years in wood.
