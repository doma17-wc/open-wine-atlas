Canale

Sub-zone

Canale is a commune of Roero in Italy in Canale. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Nebbiolo and Arneis. It lies 11 km north of Alba and 20 km south-west of Asti.

Whole commune in the zone (art. 3) and the most planted commune of Roero, with over 23% of the DOCG area per the Consorzio. Mainly sandy Pliocene soils with grey-blue silt and over 20% clay; marlier soils with gypsum toward Priocca.
