Colli Aretini

Sub-zone

Colli Aretini is a sub-zone of Chianti in Italy across 12 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese.

Colli Aretini covers the hills of the province of Arezzo on the east side of the Chianti zone, on the slopes of the upper Arno valley. The disciplinare lists 14 historic communes, now 12 after mergers.
