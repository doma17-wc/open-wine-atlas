Aleatico di Gradoli

Appellation

Aleatico di Gradoli is an appellation in Italy across 4 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Aléatico. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks. It lies 18 km north-west of Montefiascone and 32 km north-west of Viterbo.
