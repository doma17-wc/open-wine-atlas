Barbaresco

Sub-zone

Barbaresco is a commune of Barbaresco in Italy in Barbaresco. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 3.7 km east of Alba and 23 km south-west of Asti.

Whole commune in the zone (art. 3). The Enoteca Regionale del Barbaresco puts it on the Sant'Agata Fossili marls of the Tortonian, a bluish calcareous marl. 25 MGA, among them Asili, Rabajà, Martinenga, Montestefano, Ovello, Pajè and Pora.
