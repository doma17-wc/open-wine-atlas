Suvereto

DOCGAppellation

Suvereto is a DOCG appellation in Italy in Suvereto. The rules allow red wine, based on Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 9.7 km east of Venturina and 21 km north-east of Piombino.
