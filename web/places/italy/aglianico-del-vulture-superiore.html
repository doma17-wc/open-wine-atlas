Aglianico del Vulture Superiore

DOCGAppellation

Aglianico del Vulture Superiore is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 15 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Aglianico Del Vulture and Aglianico. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.
