Colli Lanuvini

Appellation

Colli Lanuvini is an appellation in Italy in Genzano Di Roma and Lanuvio. The rules allow white and red wine. Regional bedrock: intermediate volcanic rocks. It lies 1.6 km south of Lanuvio and 8 km north-east of Aprilia.
