Castelli di Jesi Verdicchio Riserva

DOCGAppellation

Castelli di Jesi Verdicchio Riserva is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 24 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Verdicchio Bianco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
