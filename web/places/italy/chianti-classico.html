Chianti Classico

DOCGAppellation

Chianti Classico is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 8 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Historic heart of Chianti between Florence and Siena, symbolised by the black rooster. At least 80% Sangiovese. The top tier, Gran Selezione, can name one of the official sub-zones (UGA).
