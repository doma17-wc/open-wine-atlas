Sommariva Perno

Sub-zone

Sommariva Perno is a commune of Roero in Italy in Sommariva Perno. The rules allow red and white wine, based on Nebbiolo and Arneis. It lies 6.2 km north-east of Bra and 30 km south-west of Asti.

Partly in the zone (art. 3); the smallest DOCG area of Roero (0.53% per the Consorzio). Siliceous, sandy, loose and arid soils east of the Rocche.
