Barbera del Monferrato

Appellation

Barbera del Monferrato is an appellation in Italy across 212 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Barbera, Dolcetto, Freisa and Grignolino. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Barbera del Monferrato covers a wide area of the Monferrato hills in the provinces of Alessandria and Asti. It is a young, often lightly sparkling Barbera. The aged version is the separate DOCG Barbera del Monferrato Superiore.
