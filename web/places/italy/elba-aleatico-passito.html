Elba Aleatico Passito

DOCGAppellation

Elba Aleatico Passito is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 7 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Aléatico. Regional bedrock: acid plutonic rocks.

A sweet red wine from dried Aleatico grapes grown on the island of Elba. It was part of the Elba DOC until it became a DOCG in 2011.
