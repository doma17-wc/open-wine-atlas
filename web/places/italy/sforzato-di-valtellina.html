Sforzato di Valtellina

DOCGAppellation

Sforzato di Valtellina is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 6 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: metamorphic rocks.

Valtellina Nebbiolo made from dried grapes, similar in method to Amarone.
