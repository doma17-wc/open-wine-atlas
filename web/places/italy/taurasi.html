Taurasi

DOCGAppellation

Taurasi is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 17 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Aglianico. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Aglianico from the hills of Irpinia near Avellino in Campania, often called the Barolo of the South for its tannin and longevity.
