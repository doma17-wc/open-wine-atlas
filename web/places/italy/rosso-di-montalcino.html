Rosso di Montalcino

Appellation

Rosso di Montalcino is an appellation in Italy in Montalcino. The rules allow red wine, based on Sangiovese. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Rosso di Montalcino is the younger red of the Montalcino commune, made from Sangiovese alone like Brunello. Its vineyard register is separate from that of Brunello.
