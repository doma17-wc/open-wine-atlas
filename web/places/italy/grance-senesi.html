Grance Senesi

Appellation

Grance Senesi is an appellation in Italy across 5 communes. The rules allow red and white wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Grance Senesi covers communes south of Siena named after the medieval farm granges of the Santa Maria della Scala hospital. Very little wine is claimed under it.
