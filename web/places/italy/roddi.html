Roddi

Sub-zone

Roddi is a commune of Barolo in Italy in Roddi. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 5.9 km west of Alba and 31 km south-west of Asti.

Partly in the zone (art. 3). Just over 24 ha of Nebbiolo da Barolo in 2018 and one MGA, Bricco Ambrogio.
