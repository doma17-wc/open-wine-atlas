Lambrusco di Sorbara

Appellation

Lambrusco di Sorbara is an appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow red and rosé wine, based on Lambrusco Di Sorbara and Lambrusco Salamino. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.
