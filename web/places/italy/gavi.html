Gavi

DOCGAppellation

Gavi is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Cortese. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments.

Cortese white from south-eastern Piedmont, near the Ligurian border. Also known as Cortese di Gavi.
