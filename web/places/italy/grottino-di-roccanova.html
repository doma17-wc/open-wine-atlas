Grottino di Roccanova

Appellation

Grottino di Roccanova is an appellation in Italy in Castronuovo Di Sant'Andrea, Roccanova and Sant'Arcangelo. The rules allow red, white and rosé wine. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
