Pinot nero dell'Oltrepò Pavese

Appellation

Pinot nero dell'Oltrepò Pavese is an appellation in Italy across 41 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Pinot Nero. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.
