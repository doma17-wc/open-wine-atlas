Greco di Tufo

DOCGAppellation

Greco di Tufo is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 8 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Coda Di Volpe Bianca and Greco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

White Greco from eight communes around Tufo in Irpinia, where sulphur-rich soils were once mined.
