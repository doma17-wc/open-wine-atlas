Greco di Tufo

DOCGAppellation

Greco di Tufo is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 8 communes. The rules allow white wine, based on Coda Di Volpe Bianca and Greco. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 5.9 km south-west of San Giorgio del Sannio and 13 km south of Benevento.

White Greco from eight communes around Tufo in Irpinia, where sulphur-rich soils were once mined.
