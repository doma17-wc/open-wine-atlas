Dolcetto d'Acqui

Appellation

Dolcetto d'Acqui is an appellation in Italy across 23 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Dolcetto. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Dolcetto d'Acqui covers 23 communes around Acqui Terme in the province of Alessandria. It is a 100% Dolcetto red.
