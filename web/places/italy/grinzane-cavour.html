Grinzane Cavour

Sub-zone

Grinzane Cavour is a commune of Barolo in Italy in Grinzane Cavour. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. It lies 5.7 km south-west of Alba and 32 km south-west of Asti.

Partly in the zone (art. 3). Just over 60 ha of Nebbiolo da Barolo in 2018 and 8 MGA, among them Castello and Canova.
