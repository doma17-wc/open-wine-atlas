Teroldego Rotaliano

Appellation

Teroldego Rotaliano is an appellation in Italy in Mezzocorona, Mezzolombardo and San Michele All'Adige. The rules allow red wine, based on Teroldego. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 1.5 km north-east of Mezzolombardo and 17 km north of Trento.
