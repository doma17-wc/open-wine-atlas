Barolo

DOCGAppellation

Barolo is a DOCG appellation in Italy across 11 communes. The rules allow red wine, based on Nebbiolo. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Nebbiolo from eleven communes in the Langhe hills south of Alba. At least 38 months of ageing, 18 in wood. Since 2010 single-vineyard names follow an official list of Menzioni Geografiche Aggiuntive (MGA), such as Cannubi or Brunate.
