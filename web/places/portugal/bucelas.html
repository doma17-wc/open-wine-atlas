Bucelas

Appellation

Bucelas is an appellation in Portugal in Bucelas, Fanhões and Santo Antão E São Julião Do Tojal. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks. It lies 6.6 km north of Loures and 11 km north of Odivelas.
