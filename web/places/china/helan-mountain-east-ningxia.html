Helan Mountain East (Ningxia)

Appellation

Helan Mountain East (Ningxia) is a Geographical indication in China.

Desert foothills of the Helan Mountains in Ningxia, sheltered from Gobi winds. Vines are buried each winter against the cold. China's best-known region for Cabernet Sauvignon.
