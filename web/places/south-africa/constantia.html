Constantia

Sub-zone

Constantia is a Wine of Origin ward of Cape Town in South Africa.

The oldest wine area of the Cape, on the slopes of the Constantiaberg south of Cape Town. Its sweet Vin de Constance was famous in 18th- and 19th-century Europe.
