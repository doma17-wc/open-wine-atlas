Upper Hemel-en-Aarde Valley

Sub-zone

Upper Hemel-en-Aarde Valley is a Wine of Origin ward of Walker Bay in South Africa. It lies 8.9 km north-east of Hermanus and 35 km south-east of Grabouw.
