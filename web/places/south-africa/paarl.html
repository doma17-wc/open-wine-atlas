Paarl

Appellation

Paarl is a Wine of Origin district of Coastal Region in South Africa. It lies around the town of Paarl and 11 km south-west of Wellington.

Large district around the town of Paarl and its granite domes, north of Stellenbosch, home of the KWV for most of the 20th century.
