South Africa · Wine of Origin district

Elgin

Appellation

Elgin is a Wine of Origin district of Cape South Coast in South Africa. It lies 2.6 km east of Grabouw and 22 km south-east of Somerset West.

Cool, high apple-growing basin in the Overberg, a source of Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.
