Stellenbosch

Appellation

Stellenbosch is a Wine of Origin district of Coastal Region in South Africa.

The heart of South African wine, east of Cape Town between the Simonsberg, Helderberg and Jonkershoek mountains. Cabernet Sauvignon, Bordeaux blends, Pinotage and Chenin Blanc on granite and sandstone soils. The district is divided into wards.
