Montilla-Moriles

Appellation

Montilla-Moriles is an appellation in Spain across 17 communes. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Andalusian wines similar to sherry, but Pedro Ximénez ripens so fully here that the wines often need no fortification.
