Ribera del Duero

Appellation

Ribera del Duero is an appellation in Spain across 81 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

High plateau along the Duero in Castilla y León, mainly Tempranillo (Tinto Fino). Large swings between hot days and cold nights. Home of Vega Sicilia.
