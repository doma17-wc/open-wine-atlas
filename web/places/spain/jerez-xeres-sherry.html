Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Appellation

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry is an appellation in Spain across 9 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Fortified wines from the triangle of Jerez, El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar de Barrameda. Palomino on white albariza soil, aged in soleras, either under a veil of flor or oxidatively.
