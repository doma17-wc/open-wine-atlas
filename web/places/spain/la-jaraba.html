La Jaraba

Vino de PagoAppellation

La Jaraba is a Vino de Pago appellation in Spain in Provencio, El. Regional bedrock: siliciclastic sedimentary rocks. It lies 10 km south-east of Las Pedroñeras and 46 km north-east of Tomelloso.
