Rioja

DOCaAppellation

Rioja is a DOCa appellation in Spain across 141 communes. Regional bedrock: carbonate sedimentary rocks.

Spain's first DOCa, since 1991, along the Ebro in La Rioja, Álava and Navarra. Mainly Tempranillo with Garnacha. Since 2017 labels can show zones, villages and 'viñedos singulares'.
