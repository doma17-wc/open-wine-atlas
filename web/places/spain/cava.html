Cava

Appellation

Cava is an appellation in Spain across 157 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

Spanish traditional-method sparkling wine, made mostly in Penedès in Catalonia from Macabeo, Xarel·lo and Parellada.
