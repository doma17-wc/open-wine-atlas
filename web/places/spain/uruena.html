Urueña

Vino de PagoAppellation

Urueña is a Vino de Pago appellation in Spain in Urueña. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks. It lies 21 km south-west of Medina de Ríoseco and 39 km north-west of Valladolid.
