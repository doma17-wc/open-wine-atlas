Manzanilla-Sanlúcar de Barrameda

Appellation

Manzanilla-Sanlúcar de Barrameda is an appellation in Spain across 9 communes. Regional bedrock: mixed sedimentary rocks.

The lightest style of sherry, aged under flor in the seaside town of Sanlúcar, where the sea air keeps the flor thick all year.
