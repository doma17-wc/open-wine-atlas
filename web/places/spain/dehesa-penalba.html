Dehesa Peñalba

Vino de PagoAppellation

Dehesa Peñalba is a Vino de Pago appellation in Spain in Villabáñez. Regional bedrock: unconsolidated sediments. It lies 7.4 km north-east of Tudela de Duero and 18 km east of Valladolid.
