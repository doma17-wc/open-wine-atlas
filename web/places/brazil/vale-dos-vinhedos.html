Vale dos Vinhedos

Appellation

Vale dos Vinhedos is a DO in Brazil. It lies 5.9 km west of Bento Gonçalves and 11 km north of Garibaldi.

Valley in the Serra Gaúcha of Rio Grande do Sul, settled by Italian immigrants in the 1870s. Brazil's first wine IP (2002) and first DO (2012).
