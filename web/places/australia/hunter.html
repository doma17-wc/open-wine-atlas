Hunter

Appellation

Hunter is a GI region of Hunter Valley in Australia.

One of Australia's oldest wine regions, north of Sydney. Humid and warm, it makes a unique style of low-alcohol Semillon that ages for decades, and medium-bodied Shiraz.
