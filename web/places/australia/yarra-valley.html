Yarra Valley

Appellation

Yarra Valley is a GI region in Australia.

Cool region east of Melbourne, one of the first vineyard areas of Victoria in the 1830s. Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine, with Shiraz on warmer sites.
