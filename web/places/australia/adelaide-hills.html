Adelaide Hills

Appellation

Adelaide Hills is a GI region of Mount Lofty Ranges in Australia.

Cool, high part of the Mount Lofty Ranges just east of Adelaide. Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and a base for sparkling wine.
