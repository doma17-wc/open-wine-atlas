Margaret River

Appellation

Margaret River is a GI region of South West Australia in Australia.

Region in Western Australia between the Indian Ocean and the Leeuwin Ridge, planted from the late 1960s. The ocean keeps the climate mild; Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay are its best-known wines.
