Niagara Peninsula

Appellation

Niagara Peninsula is a VQA Ontario designated viticultural area in Canada.

Ontario's main wine area, between Lake Ontario and Lake Erie, moderated by the lakes and sheltered by the Niagara Escarpment. Icewine, Riesling, Chardonnay and Cabernet Franc.
