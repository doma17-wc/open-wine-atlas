Niagara-on-the-Lake

Sub-zone

Niagara-on-the-Lake is a VQA Ontario designated viticultural area of Niagara Peninsula in Canada. It lies 6.7 km south-west of Niagara-on-the-Lake and 10 km east of St. Catharines.
