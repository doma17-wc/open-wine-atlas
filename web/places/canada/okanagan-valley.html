Okanagan Valley

Appellation

Okanagan Valley is a BC VQA geographical indication in Canada.

Long valley of glacial lakes in British Columbia, semi-desert in the south around Osoyoos and Oliver, cooler in the north.

Legal basis
Ontario: O. Reg. 359/24 under the VQA Act, areas defined by roads and natural features. British Columbia: GIs under the Wines of Marque Regulation, defined by watersheds and schedule maps.

Outline: Shown as a point at the place it is named after. The registered area is much smaller than any administrative unit, and its boundary is not yet available as open data.
