Red Hill Douglas County, Oregon

Sub-zone

Red Hill Douglas County, Oregon is a named sub-zone of Umpqua Valley in the United States, covering 2,237 ha. It lies 18 km north-east of Sutherlin and 58 km south-west of Eugene.
