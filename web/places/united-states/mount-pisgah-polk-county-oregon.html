Mount Pisgah, Polk County, Oregon

Sub-zone

Mount Pisgah, Polk County, Oregon is a named sub-zone of Willamette Valley in the United States, covering 2,294 ha. It lies 4.6 km south of Dallas and 22 km west of Salem.
