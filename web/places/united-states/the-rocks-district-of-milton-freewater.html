The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater

Sub-zone

The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater is a named sub-zone of Walla Walla Valley in the United States, covering 1,280 ha. It lies around the town of Milton-Freewater and 13 km south-west of Walla Walla.
