Green Valley of Russian River Valley

Sub-zone

Green Valley of Russian River Valley is a named sub-zone of Russian River Valley in the United States, covering 7,720 ha. It lies 8.1 km north-west of Sebastopol and 16 km west of Santa Rosa.
