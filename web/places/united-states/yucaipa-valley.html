Yucaipa Valley

Appellation

Yucaipa Valley is an appellation in the United States, covering 14,967 ha. It lies around the town of Yucaipa and 14 km south-east of Redlands.

The boundary of the Yucaipa Valley viticultural area is as described as follows: (1) The boundary begins on the Yucaipa map at the intersection of Highway 38/Mill Creek Road and the western boundary of section 13, T1S/R2W.
