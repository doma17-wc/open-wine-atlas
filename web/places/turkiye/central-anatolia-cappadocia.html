Central Anatolia (Cappadocia)

Wine region

Central Anatolia (Cappadocia) is a Wine region in Türkiye.

Volcanic high plateau around Nevşehir where vines have been grown for thousands of years. Native grapes such as Emir in Cappadocia and Kalecik Karası from Kalecik near Ankara.
