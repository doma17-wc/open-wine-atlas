Valle de Leyda

Sub-zone

Valle de Leyda is a DO zone of Valle de San Antonio in Chile.

Coastal zone of the San Antonio Valley, a few kilometres from the ocean, for cool-climate Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Noir.
