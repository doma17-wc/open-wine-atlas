Valle de Colchagua

Sub-zone

Valle de Colchagua is a DO zone of Valle del Rapel in Chile.

Part of the Rapel Valley, warm and sunny, best known for Carmenère, Cabernet Sauvignon and blends. Apalta, a horseshoe of hillside vineyards, is its most famous sector.
