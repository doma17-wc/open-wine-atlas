Valle del Maipo

Appellation

Valle del Maipo is a DO subregion of Valle Central in Chile.

The valley around Santiago, cradle of Chilean wine in the 19th century. Alto Maipo, at the foot of the Andes, is known for Cabernet Sauvignon.
