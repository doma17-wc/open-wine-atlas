Valle de Casablanca

Appellation

Valle de Casablanca is a DO subregion of Aconcagua in Chile.

Coastal valley between Santiago and Valparaíso, cooled by the Pacific and the Humboldt current. Planted from the 1980s with Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.
