Marlborough

Appellation

Marlborough is a GI in New Zealand.

At the north-east tip of the South Island, New Zealand's largest wine region. Its pungent Sauvignon Blanc, first planted in the 1970s, put the country on the wine map. The Wairau and Awatere valleys are its main parts.
