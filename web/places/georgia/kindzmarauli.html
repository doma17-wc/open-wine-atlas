Kindzmarauli

Appellation

Kindzmarauli is a PDO of Kakheti in Georgia. It lies 3.1 km west of Kvareli and 84 km east of Tbilisi.

Appellation in Kvareli, Kakheti, for semi-sweet red Saperavi.
