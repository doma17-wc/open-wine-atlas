Kakheti

Wine region

Kakheti is a PDO in Georgia.

Eastern Georgia along the Alazani valley, where most of the country's wine is made, including amber wines fermented with skins in buried clay qvevri, a method inscribed by UNESCO in 2013.

Legal basis
Appellations of origin registered with Sakpatenti under the Law of Georgia on Appellations of Origin and Geographical Indications. Most are village micro-zones, shown here as points at the namesake village.

Outline: Built from the administrative units that contain the area. The legal boundary follows other lines (roads, rivers, farms or altitude), so the real area is smaller.
