Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Open Wine Atlas is a free map of the world's wine places. You can use it without an account and without giving us any personal data. This page explains what personal data is processed when you use the map, create an account or join the Founding Member list. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for people in the European Union and the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Your account

An account is optional. It keeps My atlas the same on every device. When you sign in, we store:

  • your email address, when you signed up and when you last signed in
  • when you accepted the Terms of Use and confirmed that you are 18 or older
  • what you choose to put in your profile: display name, level, what you study, location, a short bio and favourite places. Every field is optional.
  • what you keep in My atlas: saved places, tastings, notes, recently opened places and your compare list
  • for security, the IP address and browser of each sign-in request, in the logs of our database provider

We use this data to sign you in with a one-time code sent to your email, to keep your atlas in sync and to show your profile to you. Your profile, tastings and notes are private. No other user can see them. We do not use them for advertising and we never sell them.

Legal basis: the contract you make with us when you create an account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The security logs rest on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and your account (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long we keep it

As long as you have the account. You can delete it at any time under My atlas › Account. This removes your email address, your profile and everything synced, straight away. Backup copies of the database are overwritten within 30 days. Security logs are kept for a short time and then deleted automatically. Under My atlas › Account you can also download a copy of your data at any time.

The Founding Member list

When you reserve a Founding Member spot we store:

  • your email address
  • the date of your signup, of your confirmation and of your consent, and the version of the consent text
  • where you came from (for example the name of a video campaign) and your browser language
  • a one-way coded version of your IP address, used only to stop automated mass signups

We use this data to confirm your email, to hold your spot and to email you about the Founding membership and its launch. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the link in every email, or by writing to us. We never sell or rent the list and never share it with wine sellers or advertisers.

Signups that are not confirmed are deleted after 30 days. When you leave the list we keep only your email address and the date you left, so that we do not write to you again. If you want it deleted entirely, write to us.

Payments

Nothing is for sale yet. When membership opens, payments will run through Apple's App Store or a payment provider that we will name on this page before we take any payment. We will never see or store your card number.

Site analytics

Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without storing your IP address. We see totals, such as how many people opened a page, not who they are.

Cookies and storage in your browser

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The site keeps in your browser your settings, your My atlas and, when you are signed in, the session that keeps you signed in. These are needed for the features you use, so the site does not ask for cookie consent. Without an account, nothing from My atlas leaves your browser.

Services that work for us

These companies process data on our behalf, only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., United States) runs our database and the sign-in. The data is stored in Zurich, Switzerland.
  • Resend (Resend Inc., United States) sends sign-in codes and Founding Member emails.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States) hosts the site. Its servers log requests, including IP addresses, for a short time for security.

Where data goes to the United States, it is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, which the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner also recognises, or by the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified.

What the map loads

To draw the map and fill a place, your browser contacts these services directly. They receive your IP address and, for some of them, the coordinates of the place you look at, as with any website. We send them no account data.

  • map, vineyards and rivers: OpenFreeMap
  • terrain and relief: Mapterhorn
  • other base maps, only when you choose them: EOX, IGN France, IGN España, swisstopo, OpenTopoMap
  • climate and vintages: Open-Meteo
  • soil: ISRIC SoilGrids
  • place summaries: Wikipedia
  • program libraries: jsDelivr; fonts: Google Fonts

Age

The atlas is about wine and is meant for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you and get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to hello@openwineatlas.com. We answer within 30 days. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

We update this page when what we do changes, and put the date at the top. If you have an account, we tell you by email about important changes before they apply.
